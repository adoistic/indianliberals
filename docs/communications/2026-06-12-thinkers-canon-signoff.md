## What changed

The Thinkers page no longer lists all ~670 people in the archive. It now
shows only a curated canon, with everyone else moved to a clearly-labelled
full directory (`/thinkers/directory/`). Nobody was deleted — figures like
Mukesh Ambani or Joseph Stalin exist in the archive *because liberal works
cite or critique them*, and removing their pages would break the
cross-reference graph (every "mentioned in" link on other pages). Instead:

- Their pages now carry a prominent notice: *"Referenced figure — not part
  of the Indian liberal tradition. This page exists because works in the
  archive cite, discuss, or critique them."*
- The directory section they appear in is titled *"Referenced figures —
  not part of the liberal tradition"* with the same framing.
- They never appear on the main Thinkers page.
