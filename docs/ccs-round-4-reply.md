# 1. Periodicals

The page was fourteen boxes. It is now six.

All Forum of Free Enterprise material sits under Forum of Free Enterprise. The

# 2. Uploading books

Four of the five books you uploaded on 13 August never reached the archive. The
upload screen asks what the files are, and Series was chosen rather than Primary
work. A series is the shelf, not the books on it, so each file made an empty
shelf and the books themselves were not filed. That is what the boxes under CCS
Viewpoints were.

All four are in properly now, with covers, contents and summaries taken from the
books. The empty boxes are gone. Series is no longer offered on the bulk screen
at all, because a folder of books is never a folder of shelves.

The cover field was there but sitting inside a closed drawer called "Files,
pictures and rights". It is now in the first section of the form, with the
photograph, portrait and logo fields on the other kinds of entry. You should not
have had to hunt for it.

The author's name was a genuine fault on our side. Where a book has no single
author the page fell back to its contributors, and the test it used was
case-sensitive, so a book with seventeen contributors was credited to the one
whose role happened to be typed in lower case. Books with editors now read
"Edited by". While fixing it I found thirteen more works whose author is an
organisation rather than a person, all of which had been quietly showing no
author at all. They read correctly now.

Yes to both of your questions. Pictures can be changed from the backend: open the
entry and drop a new one on the picture field. And content can now be moved
between sections, which it could not before.

# 3. Featured images

Fixed. The article page was cutting tall pictures off, and the reason was that
the article column is nearly twice the width of a listing card, so the same
height limit bit far harder there. Nothing is cropped now. A portrait is drawn
narrower and centred instead of wide and cut. Thirty of the fifty-nine opinion
covers were affected.

# 4. Feedback

There is a form at indianliberals.in/contact. It asks what the message is about,
carries the page the reader was on, and needs no account and no email address.

Messages go to a Feedback screen in the CMS rather than to anyone's inbox, so two
people can see the same queue and mark items dealt with. Corrections come first,
since those are the ones that improve the archive.

# 5. Filters on thinker pages

Fixed, and the same on desktop and on a phone. The filters sit behind one
"Filters" control and are closed until asked for, with a number on the button
when any are on. Minoo Masani's page had 27 filters and 262 pixels of controls
before the first work. It now has 74.
