V. A. Shahane wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> V. A. SHAHANE has published a book of short stories and a collection of critical essays in Marathi. He holds a Ph. D. from Leeds University.
