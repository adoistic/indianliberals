Swami Agehananda Bharati contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 9 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> Swami Agehananda is an Austrian by birth who has turned a Hindu Sadhu. He has lectured in philosophy at Delhi and Banaras.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956), p. 72 reads:

> Swami Agehananda is Austrian by birth. He contributed the article Philosophy East and West to Vol. 1, No. 2 of QUEST.
