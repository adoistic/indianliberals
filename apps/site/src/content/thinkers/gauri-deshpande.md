Gauri Deshpande wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966'), p. 112 reads:

> GAURI DESHPANDE is working for a Ph.D. in English Literature in the time she can spare from her occupation as housewife and mother.
