Sujatha Bala Subramanian wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966'), p. 112 reads:

> SUJATHA BALA SUBRAMANIAN has published over forty short stories in different Indian magazines.
