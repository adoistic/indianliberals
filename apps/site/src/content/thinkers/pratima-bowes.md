Pratima Bowes contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 18 (July-September 1958) to No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 91 reads:

> Pratima Bowes, principal of a Calcutta college, did her doctorate at the University of London. Her thesis on 'The Concept of Morality' is shortly to be published in England.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 101 reads:

> PRATIMA BOWES, principal of a Calcutta college, her contribution 'the Ethical Basis of Blame and Punishment' appeared in Quest 17.
