Jeremy Nelson contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958) to No. 29 (APRIL/JUNE 1961 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1961')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Jeremy Nelson is a young poet from Australia who is studying Political Science in the University of Calcutta on an exchange Scholarship.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> JEREMY NELSON came to India from Australia on an exchange scholarship to do his M.A. in Politics; at present he is a teacher in a New South Wales School.
