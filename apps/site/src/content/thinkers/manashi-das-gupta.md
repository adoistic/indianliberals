Manashi Das Gupta wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ') to No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / '), p. 111 reads:

> MANASHI DAS GUPTA is completing her doctoral dissertation on Social Psychology and Personality with a minor in Ethics at Cornell University, Ithaca.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> MANASHI DAS GUPTA holds a doctorate in social psychology from Cornell University and is at present Principal, Shree Shikshayatan College, Calcutta. She has published short stories in Bengali.
