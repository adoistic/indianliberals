Sudhir Chandra contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 5 issues of the archive's run (No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25') to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 108 reads:

> SUDHIR CHANDRA writes that this will be his "first article to be published in other than college and hostel magazines".

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> SUDHIR CHANDRA gave up his research work on the revolutionary movement in Bengal to prepare for "the competitives".

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 105 reads:

> SUDHIR CHANDRA used to lecture in History in Agra University.
