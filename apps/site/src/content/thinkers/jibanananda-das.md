Jibanananda Das wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 25 (April/June 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 25 (April/June 1960), p. 103 reads:

> JIBANANANDA DAS's deservedly famous poem 'Banalata Sen' has been translated before. This translation is by another important Indo-Anglian poet, P. Lal, who teaches English literature at a Calcutta college.
