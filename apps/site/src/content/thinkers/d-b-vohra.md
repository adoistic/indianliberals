D. B. Vohra wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> D. B. VOHRA is Jt. Director of Establishment in the Ministry of Railways and has published articles on oriental history in various Indian journals.
