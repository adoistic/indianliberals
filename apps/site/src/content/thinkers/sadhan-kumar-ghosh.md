Sadhan Kumar Ghosh contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 14 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955) to No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> Sadhan Kumar Ghosh is professor of English Literature at Asutosh College, Calcutta.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 75 reads:

> Sadhan Kumar Ghosh (Calcutta) is professor of English Literature at Asutosh College. His article entitled The Cold Class War appeared in QUEST, Volume 1, Number 2. (October-November 1955.)
