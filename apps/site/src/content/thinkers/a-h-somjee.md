A. H. Somjee contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 14 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956) to No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57), p. 63 reads:

> A. H. Somjee. Reader in Political Science at M. S. University of Baroda.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 40 reads:

> A. H. Somjee is Reader in Political Science at M. S. University of Baroda. He has frequently reviewed books in Quest.
