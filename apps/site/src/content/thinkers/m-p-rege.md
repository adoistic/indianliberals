M. P. Rege contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue) to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> M. P. Rege has recently come out with a candid critique of the late B. S. Mardhekar's aesthetic theories.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 109 reads:

> M. P. REGE is Vice-Principal and Professor of Philosophy at D. E. Society's Kirti College, Bombay. The paper printed here was prepared for the Seminar on The Indian Tradition and Its Significance for Cultural Freedom convened by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom in August 1964. An expanded version is going to be published by the Indian Renaissance Institute, Dehradun.
