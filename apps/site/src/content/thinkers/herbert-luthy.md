Herbert Luthy wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959) to No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> Herbert Luthy, historian and essayist, is generally recognised as one of the foremost journalists of Europe. He hails from Switzerland and has recently published a volume on modern French politics.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> HERBERT LUTHY, historian and essayist, is generally recognised as one of the foremost journalists of Europe. He hails from Switzerland and has recently published a volume on modern French politics.
