Joseph Jordens wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 42 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the issue 'monsoon 1964')).

> JOSEPH JORDENS has a doctorate from the University of Louvain on Theism in Bhagvad Gita and is at present a lecturer in the Department of Indian Studies, University of Melbourne.
