G. D. Parikh wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> G. D. Parikh is professor of Politics and Economics, Ruia College, Bombay University. He is associated with Nav Bharat, a Marathi monthly devoted to inquiry and learning.
