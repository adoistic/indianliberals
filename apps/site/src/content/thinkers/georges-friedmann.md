Georges Friedmann wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> GEORGES FRIEDMANN is the Director of Studies at the Ecole Pratique des Hautes Etudes at the Sorbonne, one of the most eminent contemporary French sociologists, the author of several books on the sociology of work and a study of Leibnitz and Spinoza.
