Naresh Guha contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63'), p. 112 reads:

> NARESH GUHA is Reader in the Department of Comparative Literature at Jadavpur University. His doctoral dissertation on oriental influences in Yeats' poetry (North-Western University, USA) is to be published shortly.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966'), p. 112 reads:

> NARESH GUHA, Head of the department of Comparative Literature at Jadavpur University, Calcutta, published his first and only book of poems in Bengali in 1952.
