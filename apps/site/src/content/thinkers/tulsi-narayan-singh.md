Tulsi Narayan Singh wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Tulsinarayan Singh is Lecturer in the Dept. of English Literature, Banaras Hindu University. His doctorate thesis on "The Correlation of Form and Content in the English Novel" is to be published shortly.
