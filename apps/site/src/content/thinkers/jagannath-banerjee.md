Jagannath Banerjee wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Jagannath Banerjee has composed numerous Hindi songs in various styles and covering over three hundred ragas, many of which have been published in Bengali and Hindi monthlies with notations and raga analysis.
