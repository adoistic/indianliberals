Benjamin Polk wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> Benjamin Polk is a member of the Indian architectural and town planning professional institutes, also of the American Institute of Architects and the Town Planning Institute of England.
