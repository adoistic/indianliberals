Vasant Palshikar contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959) to No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 101 reads:

> VASANT PALSHIKAR is a sarvodaya worker and a free lance writer in Marathi and English.
