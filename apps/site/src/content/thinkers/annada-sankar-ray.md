Annada Sankar Ray wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 18 (July-September 1958) to No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 91 reads:

> Annada Sankar Ray, an eminent Bengali litterateur, retired from the Indian Civil Service to devote all his time to the service of literature. His publications include two epic novels, many collections of short stories, two books of verse, and several volumes of personal essays.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965'), p. 116 reads:

> ANNADA SANKAR RAY, a distinguished and versatile writer in Bengali, retired from the I.C.S. in 1950 to devote all his time to creative writing.
