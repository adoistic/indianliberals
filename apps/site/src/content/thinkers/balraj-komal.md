Balraj Komal wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56), p. 68 reads:

> Balraj Komal's poems have appeared in most Urdu literary magazines. His first collection was published in 1954.
