Denis de Rougemont wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 3 (December 1956-January 1957) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> DENIS de ROUGEMONT, a citizen of Geneva, is an eminent critic of society, politics and religion. He has written some twenty books, several of them available in English.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / '), p. 111 reads:

> DENIS de ROUGEMONT was born in Switzerland and commands an international reputation as a thinker and writer. Since his first publication in 1932 of La Paysan du Danube he has brought out 19 books which have been translated into many languages-the last, Man's Western Quest (1957) was reviewed in Quest 19.
