Narendra Mitra wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> Narendra Mitra has published sixteen books so far—novels and collections of short stories. Has been in journalism for the last ten years; at present with the Ananda Bazar Patrika.
