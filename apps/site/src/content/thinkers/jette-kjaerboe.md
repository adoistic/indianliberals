Jette Kjaerboe wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966'), p. 111 reads:

> JETTE KJAERBOE's "deep interest in India's economic development and its great culture" brought her to India, and she is now working with "The Swallows" at Madras. Majored in Modern Languages and has contributed so far to Danish magazines.
