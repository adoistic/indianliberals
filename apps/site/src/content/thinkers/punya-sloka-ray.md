Punya Sloka Ray contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 17 issues of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958) to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Punya Sloka Ray lectures on Philosophy at the Utkal University; holds a doctorate from the University of Tuebingen, West Germany, with a dissertation on the Special Logic of the Human Sciences.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> Punya Sloka Ray lectures on Philosophy at the Utkal University, Cuttak.
