Molly Daniels wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> Molly Daniels was born in Travancore. Her mother tongue is Malayalam · but she writes only in English. Lives in Bombay.
