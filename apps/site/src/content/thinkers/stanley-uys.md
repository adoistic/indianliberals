Stanley Uys wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:

> STANLEY UYS is a distinguished Cape Town journalist and correspondent for some well-known English newspapers.
