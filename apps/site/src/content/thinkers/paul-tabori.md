Paul Tabori wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 40 reads:

> Paul Tabori is Hungarian by birth. He obtained his Ph.D. at Berlin University. He settled in England in 1937. Mr. Tabori is the author of twenty-eight books, and has also written film and television scripts. He edited the two anthologies of The Pen in Exile sponsored by the International PEN Club. On Being Neutral is the text of a speech he delivered in Bombay recently at the meeting organised by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, Mr. Frank Moraes presiding.
