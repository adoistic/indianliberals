P. Lal contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 11 issues of the archive's run (No. 25 (April/June 1960) to No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 25 (April/June 1960) reads:

> This translation is by another important Indo-Anglian poet, P. Lal, who teaches English literature at a Calcutta college.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 100 reads:

> P. LAL teaches English at St. Xavier's College, Calcutta. His first collection of poems, "The Parrots Death and Other Poems", has just been published.
