N. V. Haldankar illustrated *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 6 (June-July 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 6 (June-July 1956), p. 73 reads:

> N. V. Haldankar (Bombay) is one of the prize-winners of the Agfa Grand National Photographic Contest 1955.
