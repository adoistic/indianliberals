N. R. W. Pande wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:

> N. R. W. PANDE, Principal Scientific Officer (Psychology) in the Ministry of Defence, has published two books, Pain & Pleasure (Marathi) and Abilities and Education (English), besides short stories, poems and plays in Marathi.
