M. K. Haldar contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 7 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57) to No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57), p. 14 reads:

> Professor M. K. Haldar, Ph. D. (London), teaches Philosophy at Delhi College. He has appeared in the pages of QUEST as a book reviewer. (Vol. III, No. 1 and No. 2).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 24 reads:

> M. K. Haldar lectures in Philosophy at Delhi College.
