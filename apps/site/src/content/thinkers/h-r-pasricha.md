H. R. Pasricha wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 5 issues of the archive's run (No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25') to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 108 reads:

> H. R. PASRICHA says "I am afraid to disappoint you and others but I am no writer". He held the rank of Lt. Colonel in the army as Consulting Surgeon to Western Command from which position he retired at his request to resume private practice as an orthopaedic surgeon.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963'), p. 107 reads:

> H. R. PASRICHA held the rank of Lt. Colonel in the army as Consulting Surgeon to the Western Command from which he retired at his request to resume private practice as an orthopaedic surgeon. He is a leading member of the Swatantra Party.
