Jagadish N. Hattangadi wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> JAGADISH N. HATTANGADI was not exactly "fifty when he was born" but he seems to be a good many years older than his present age, which is sixteen. This is his first publication outside his college magazine.
