S. D. Deshmukh translated for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue) to Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> S. D. Deshmukh has translated Bangarwadi, Vyankatesh Madgulkar's novel about a schoolmaster's experience of a shepherd community.
