S. Natarajan contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 21 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956) to No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956), p. 72 reads:

> S. Natarajan is the former editor of the Free Press Journal, (Bombay).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 40 reads:

> S. Natarajan is former editor of the Free Press Journal, Bombay.
