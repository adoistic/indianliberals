M. V. Sastry wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 25 (April/June 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 25 (April/June 1960), p. 103 reads:

> M. V. SASTRY, MLC (Andhra), was a former co-worker of M. N. Roy to whose Independent India he contributed regularly.
