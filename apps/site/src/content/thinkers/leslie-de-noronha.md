Leslie de Noronha wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963') to No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> LESLIE DE NORONHA is a practising physician, journalist, short story writer and poet.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:
