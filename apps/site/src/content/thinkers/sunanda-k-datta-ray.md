Sunanda K. Datta-Ray wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> SUNANDA K. DATTA-RAY is a student of Manchester University and "does a bit of writing" for several Indian periodicals in the English language.
