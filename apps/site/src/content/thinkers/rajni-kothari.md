Rajni Kothari contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960) to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> RAJNI KOTHARI graduated from the London School of Economics and teaches Political Science and Classical Economics at the M. S. University of Baroda. He is at present engaged in a comprehensive work on The Foundations of Democracy (a tentative title).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 31 (OCT./DEC. 1961 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1961' with the numeral '31' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 98 reads:

> VINOD SENA, PUNYA SLOKA RAY and RAJNI KOTHARI have appeared before in the pages of QUEST
