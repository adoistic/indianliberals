Hephzibah Jesudasan wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ') to No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / '), p. 111 reads:

> HEPHZIBAH JESUDASAN teaches English Literature in the College for Women at Trivandrum. In collaboration with her husband she has written a book on the history of Tamil literature which is in the press. Publications: Skylights (a collection of poems) and The Song of the Cuckoo and other poems (translated from Subramania Bharati).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 30 (JULY/SEPT. 1961 (contents page); cover dated "summer 1961"), p. 109 reads:

> HEPHZIBAH JESUDASAN, lecturer in English literature in a women's college in South India, has appeared in print in Quest earlier.
