M. G. Bailur wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> M. G. Bailur was editor till recently of the English daily Jai Hind, of Rajkot, Saurashtra. He shares his interest in politics with English Literature and the Philosophy of Science.
