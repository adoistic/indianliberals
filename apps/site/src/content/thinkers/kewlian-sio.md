Kewlian Sio wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63'), p. 112 reads:

> KEWLIAN SIO has had a collection of his short stories and vignettes, A Small World, recently published by Writers Workshop, Calcutta.
