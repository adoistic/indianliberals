Prabhakar Padhye contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> Prabhakar Padhye is well known as a short story writer in Marathi and a contributor in English to Indian and foreign periodicals. He is the Secretary for Asian Affairs of the Congress for Cultural Freedom.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> PRABHAKAR PADHYE used to be well-known as a short story writer in Marathi till a few years ago when his literary career was interrupted by political writing and organisational work.
