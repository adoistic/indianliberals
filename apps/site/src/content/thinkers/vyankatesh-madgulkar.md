Vyankatesh Madgulkar wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue) to Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> Vyankatesh Madgulkar is a pioneer of the new short story, specialising in rural sketches.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57), p. 48 reads:

> Vyankatesh Madgulkar works in All India Radio, Poona. His book of short stories, Kali Aai, was recently awarded a State prize. His novel, Bangarwadi, is being translated into Japanese.
