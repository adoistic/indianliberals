G. P. Pradhan contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 69 reads:

> G. P. Pradhan and A. K. Bhagwat are Professors of English at the Fergusson College, Poona, and the Willingdon College, Sangli, respectively. These institutions are run by the Deccan Education Society of which Tilak and Agarkar were the founders.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> G. P. Pradhan, a well-known Marathi critic, lectures on English Literature at the Fergusson College, Poona; a frequent contributor of Quest.
