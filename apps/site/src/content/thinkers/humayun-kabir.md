Humayun Kabir wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 17 (April - June 1958) to No. 20 (January-March 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Humayun Kabir, at present Minister of Culture and Scientific Research in the Government of India, has taught Philosophy at the Universities of Andhra and Calcutta. His publications include Art, Monad and Society; Studies in Kant; Science, Democracy and Islam and other Essays.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 93 reads:

> Humayun Kabir, distinguished philosopher, litterateur and statesman, was a contributor to our previous number.
