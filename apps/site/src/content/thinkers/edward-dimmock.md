Edward Dimmock translated for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 109 reads:

> EDWARD DIMMOCK is Co-Chairman, Committee of South Asian Studies and Professor of Linguistics, University of Chicago. Has published a translation of Vidya Sundar and other 18th Century Bengali narrative poems, and has a collection of translation of Vaishnava lyrics in the Press.
