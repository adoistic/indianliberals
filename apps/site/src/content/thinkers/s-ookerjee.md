S. Ookerjee contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 8 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955) to No. 25 (April/June 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> S. Ookerjee lectures in philosophy at Wilson College, Bombay University. He is the Secretary of the Bombay Philosophical Society.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956), p. 73 reads:

> S. OoKERJI is Professor of Philosophy at Wilson College, Bombay.
