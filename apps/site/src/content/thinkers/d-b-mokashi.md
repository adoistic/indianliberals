D. B. Mokashi wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> D. B. Mokashi has been writing short stories for over a decade. His apparent artlessness hides a delicate melancholy and directness of approach.
