Allen Ginsberg wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63'), p. 112 reads:

> ALLEN GINSBERG, author of Howl and Kaddish, is a leading representative of the school of modern poetry known as Beat poetry. He has now spent almost a year in India.
