Feroze F. Moos wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> FEROZE F. MOOS holds a Tripos in the Natural Sciences from Cambridge, is a business executive in a cotton mill in Bombay, and calls himself "a spinner and weaver of common cloth". Perhaps he finds this experience useful in an art which may also be described as excellence in spinning and weaving words?

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / '), p. 111 reads:

> FEROZE MOOS holds a Tripos from Cambridge and is a business executive in Bombay.
