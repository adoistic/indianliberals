Vinda Karandikar wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 49 reads:

> VINDA KARANDIKAR has tried to rationalise th e idiom of poetry and stretch it to rhetoric. Translated Aristotle's Poetics.
