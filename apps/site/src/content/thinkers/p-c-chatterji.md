P. C. Chatterji contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 5 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> P. C. Chatterji is a station-director of the All India Radio who spends his leisure in philosophical study and writing. He has recently published An Introduction to Philosophical Analysis.
