Raymond Aron wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> RAYMOND ARON is a noted French political philosopher, Professor at the Sorbonne and author of The Century of Total War, The Opium of the Intellectuals, Introduction to the Philosophy of History, he is currently teaching at Harvard University.
