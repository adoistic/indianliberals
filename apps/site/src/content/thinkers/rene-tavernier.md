Rene Tavernier wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> Rene Tavernier edited Confluences, a journal of literature and art, from 1941 to 1947. Contributor to several French periodicals, including Arts, Preuves, Carrefour and Franc-Tireur.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56), p. 68 reads:

> Rene Tavernier edited Confluences from 1941 to 1947. Regular contributor to several leading French journals.
