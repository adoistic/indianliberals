A. K. Bhagwat contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 75 reads:

> G. P. Pradhan and A. K. Bhagwat are Professors of English at the Fergusson College, Poona, and the Willingdon College, Sangli, respectively. These institutions are run by the Deccan Education Society of which Tilak and Agarkar were the founders.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> A. K. Bhagwat is Professor of English at the Willingdon College, Sangli. In collaboration with Prof. G. P. Pradhan, he contributed an article on Lokamanya Tilak to QUEST Vol. II, No. 1 (August/September 1956).
