A. A. A. Fyzee contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 6 (June-July 1956) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 106 reads:

> A. A. A. FYZEE is an authority on Islam and was lately Vice-Chancellor of Kashmir University.
