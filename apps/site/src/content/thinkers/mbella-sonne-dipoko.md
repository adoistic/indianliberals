Mbella Sonne Dipoko wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963'), p. 107 reads:

> MBELLA SONNE DIPOKO, born in Cameroun, was reporter with Radio Nigeria, Lagos, until 1960. He has published short stories and poems some of which have been broadcast by the BBC.
