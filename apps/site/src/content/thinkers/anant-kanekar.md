Anant Kanekar wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 48 reads:

> ANANT KANEKAR solitary light of th e light essay. playwright and travelogue-writer. Anant Kanekar has been elected President of the Marathi Sahitya Sammelan to be held at Aurangabad in the last week of April.
