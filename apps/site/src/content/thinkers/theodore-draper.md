Theodore Draper wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page) to No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page), p. 105 reads:

> THEODORE DRAPER, sociologist and journalist, research associate at Harvard and Columbia Universities, is the author of American Communism and Soviet Russia, and Castro's Revolution: Myths and Realities.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965'), p. 116 reads:

> THEODORE DRAPER, sociologist and journalist, research associate at Harvard and Columbia Universities, is the author of American Communism and Soviet Russia and Castro's Revolution: Myths and Realities.
