S. R. Tikekar contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956) to No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 3 (December 1956-January 1957), p. 79 reads:

> S. R. Tikekar is the author of several books in Marathi on his travels in Burma, Afghanistan and Middle Eastern countries. He is a prolific writer and broadcaster on a variety of subjects.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62'), p. 93 reads:

> S. R. TIKEKAR is a free lance journalist of Bombay.
