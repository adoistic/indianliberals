Kironmoy Raha contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly) to No. 30 (JULY/SEPT. 1961 (contents page); cover dated "summer 1961")).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> KIRONMOY RAHA is a senior officer in the Income Tax Department who finds occasional writing a relief from the tedium of his files.
