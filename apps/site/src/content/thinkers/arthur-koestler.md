Arthur Koestler wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> ARTHUR KOESTLER is too well-known to need any introduction. It is not so well-known, however, at least in India, that four years ago in his preface to the Trail of the Dinosaur he bade farewell to politics and political writing. His latest publication The Sleep Walkers is a history of man's changing vision of the Universe.
