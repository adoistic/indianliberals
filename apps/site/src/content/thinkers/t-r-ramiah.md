T. R. Ramiah wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955) to Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> T. R. Ramiah was a brilliant student of the Bombay University before he entered Government service. He is deeply interested in English and American literature.
