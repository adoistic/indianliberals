Ashin Das Gupta contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62') to No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62'), p. 95 reads:

> ASHIN DAS GUPTA did his doctoral thesis at Cambridge University and now teaches history at Presidency College, Calcutta.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 109 reads:

> ASHIN DAS GUPTA teaches History in Calcutta University.
