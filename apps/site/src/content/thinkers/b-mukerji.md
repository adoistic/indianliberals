B. Mukerji wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

> B. MUKERJI is Director of the Chittaranjan National Cancer Research Centre, Calcutta. This article is based on an address to the 3rd Congress of the International Academy of Philosophy in Delhi (Philosophy of Biological Science Section).
