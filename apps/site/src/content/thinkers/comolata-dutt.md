Comolata Dutt wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> Comolata Dutt is Supervisor of Western Music, All India Radio, Delhi. She composed the music for Pavlova's Indian Ballets, Radha Krishna and Hindu Wedding. Is a Fellow of the Trinity College of Music, London.
