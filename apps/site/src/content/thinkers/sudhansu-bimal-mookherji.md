Sudhansu Bimal Mookherji contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 36 (JAN./MAR. 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1962-63'), p. 112 reads:

> SUDHANSU BIMAL MUKHERJEE was until September 1962 Professor of History, Gadjah Mada State University, Jogjakarta (Indonesia). His publications include Indian Minority in South Africa and The Indenture System in Mauritius (1837-1915)

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 40 (No. , JAN./MAR. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the same issue 'winter 1963-64'; price rupees 1.25), p. 115 reads:

> SUDHANSU BIMAL MOOKHERJI, formerly of the Gadja Mada State University, Indonesia, and of the University College, Mandalay, is author of Indian Minority in South Africa, The Indenture System in Mauritius, etc.
