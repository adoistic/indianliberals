A. P. Barnabas wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 40 (No. , JAN./MAR. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the same issue 'winter 1963-64'; price rupees 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 40 (No. , JAN./MAR. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the same issue 'winter 1963-64'; price rupees 1.25), p. 115 reads:

> A. P. BARNABAS, member of the staff of the Indian Institute of Public Administration, has published numerous articles in various magazines in India and abroad; his first book Social Change in a North Indian Village is to be published shortly.
