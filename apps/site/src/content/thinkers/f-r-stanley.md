F. R. Stanley wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959) to No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> F. R. Stanley is Lecturer in English, Madras Christian College, Tambaram.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> F. R. STANLEY is Lecturer in English, Madras Christian College, Tambaram.
