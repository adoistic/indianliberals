C. L. Nahal contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 7 issues of the archive's run (No. 18 (July-September 1958) to No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 91 reads:

> C. L. Nahal teaches English Literature in a New Delhi College and the University of Delhi.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> C. L. Nahal, teaches English Literature in a New Delhi College and the University of Delhi.
