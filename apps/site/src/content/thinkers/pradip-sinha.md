Pradip Sinha wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963') to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> PRADEEP SINHA is a lecturer in History at St. Paul's College, Calcutta, and is working on the social history of 19th century Bengal.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966'), p. 111 reads:

> PRADIP SINHA is a senior U.G.C. research fellow, University of Calcutta, and has just published his first book, Nineteenth Century Bengal; formerly lecturer in History, St. Paul's College, Calcutta.
