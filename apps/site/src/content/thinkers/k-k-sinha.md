K. K. Sinha contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 7 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to No. 41 (No. , APRIL - JUNE 1964 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 69 reads:

> K. K. Sinha (Calcutta) edits the Journal of Power and River Valley Development. He is active in India's Radical Humanist Movement and has contributed widely to Indian and foreign periodicals.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 3 (December 1956-January 1957), p. 73 reads:

> K. K. Sinha (Calcutta) edits the Journal of Power and River Valley Development. He is active in India's Radical Humanist Movement and has contributed widely to Indian and foreign periodicals. This is his second article in QUEST.
