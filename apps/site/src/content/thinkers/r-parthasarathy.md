R. Parthasarathy wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page), p. 105 reads:

> R. PARTHASARATHI teaches English Literature at Mithbai College of arts and the Chauhan Institute of Science, Bombay. His poems have appeared in the Review of English Literature (Leeds) and the Times Literary Supplement.
