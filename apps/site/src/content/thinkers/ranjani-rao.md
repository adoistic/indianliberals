Ranjani Rao wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956) to Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 24 reads:

> Ranjani Rao's poems have appeared in several previous issues of Quest.
