Jorgen Schleimann wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964')).

> JORGEN SCHLEIMANN is a Copenhagen journalist and is founder and executive director of the Committee for Afro-Scandinavian Cooperation.
