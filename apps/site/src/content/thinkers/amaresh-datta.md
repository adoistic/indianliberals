Amaresh Datta wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 8 issues of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959) to No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> Amaresh Datta, Lecturer in the department of English Literature, University of Saugor, has just finished a book on Shakespeare's Tragic Vision and Art. The International Association of Poetry in Rome is publishing some of his poems in their Journal of Poets.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> AMARESH DATTA, Lecturer in English at the University of Saugor, has now a verse drama and a collection of poems ready for publication.
