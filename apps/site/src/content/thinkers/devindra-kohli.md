Devindra Kohli wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966'), p. 111 reads:

> DEVINDRA KOHLI teaches English literature at Ramjas College, Delhi.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966'), p. 112 reads:

> DEVINDRA KOHLI is Lecturer in English Literature at Ramjas College, Delhi.
