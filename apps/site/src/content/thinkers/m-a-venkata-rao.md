M. A. Venkata Rao wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> M. A. Venkata Rao was a lecturer in Philosophy for over 20 years. He is at present Executive Secretary of the Democratic Research Service.
