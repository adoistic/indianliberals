R. Ramani wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 6 (June-July 1956) to Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 6 (June-July 1956), p. 73 reads:

> R. Ramani (Delhi) is a free lance journalist.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57), p. 14 reads:

> R. Ramani is a well-known Delhi journalist. He contributed the article entitled How Fast Should We Run, the race with Red China, to QUEST Vol. I, No. 6.
