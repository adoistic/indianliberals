Jai Nimbkar wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> JAI NIMBKAR has an M.A. in Sociology from the University of Arizona, is "a full-time housewife", and has published half a dozen stories so far.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 109 reads:

> JAI NIMBKAR, who describes herself as "a full-time housewife" took her M. A. in Sociology from the University of Arizona. A previous story appeared in Quest 26.
