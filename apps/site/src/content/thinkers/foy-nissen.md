Foy Nissen contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 11 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 62 reads:

> Foy Nissen. Former student of F.R. Leavis. Free lance critic and reviewer of literature and the lively arts.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 53 reads:

> Foy Nissen. Free lance critic and reviewer of literature and theatre
