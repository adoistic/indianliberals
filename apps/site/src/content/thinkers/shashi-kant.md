Shashi Kant wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964') to No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964'), p. 109 reads:

> SHASHI KANT contributes articles on historical and cultural subjects to various periodicals. His book Kausambi (in Hindi), a historical study, is in the press.
