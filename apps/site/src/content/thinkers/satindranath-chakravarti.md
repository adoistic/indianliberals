Satindranath Chakravarti wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Satindranath Chakravarti, Head of the Dept. of Philosophy, City College, Calcutta, is a well-known contributor to Marxist periodicals.
