Kastur Chand Lalwani wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 5 issues of the archive's run (No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25') to No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 111 reads:

> KASTUR CHAND LALWANI was Lecturer at Calcutta University (1947-49) and Delhi Polytechnique (1951-54) and since then has been attached to the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur. Author of Industrial Efficiency in India, Indian Planning, its History, Politics and Psychology, etc.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page), p. 105 reads:

> KASTUR CHAND LALWANI was lecturer at Calcutta University (1947-49) and Delhi Polytechnic (1951-54) and since then has been attached to the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur. Author of Industrial Efficiency in India; Indian Planning, its History, Politics & Psychology.
