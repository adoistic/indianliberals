Ashis Barman wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 18 (July-September 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 91 reads:

> Ashis Barman, film critic and short story writer, has just published his first novel. He was one of the Assistant Directors in the film Pathar Panchali.
