Daniel Bell wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> DANIEL BELL is a professor of Sociology at Columbia University, New York. Author of "Work and its Discontents"; another book of his "The End of an Ideology" is due to be published this coming winter.
