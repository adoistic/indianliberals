K. Guru Dutt contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56) to No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56), p. 68 reads:

> K. Guru Dutt is a journalist and critic. He reviews books regularly for The Times of India and other papers.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 105 reads:

> K. GURU DUTT is a journalist and critic. He reviews books for the Times of India.
