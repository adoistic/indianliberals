Bishnu Dey contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (No. 17 (April - June 1958) to No. 30 (JULY/SEPT. 1961 (contents page); cover dated "summer 1961")).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Bishnu Dey, one of the major poets of Bengal after Tagore, teaches English Literature a t Central College, Calcutta. His publications include half a dozen books of verse, two volumes of essays in literary criticism, two books of translation from Western poets, including one from T. S. Eliot.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 93 reads:

> Bishnu Dey, the leading Marxist poet of Bengal, was introduced in our previous issue.
