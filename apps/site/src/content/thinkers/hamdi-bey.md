Hamdi Bey contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956) to No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956), p. 67 reads:

> Hamdi Bey is on the editorial staff of The Statesman, Calcutta. His short stories and articles have appeared in a number of Indian and foreign periodicals.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> HAMDI BEY is a well-known Calcutta journalist.
