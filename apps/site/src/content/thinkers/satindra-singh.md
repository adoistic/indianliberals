Satindra Singh wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956) to Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956), p. 67 reads:

> Satindra Singh is on the editorial staff of The Indian Express (Delhi) and represents Lamerhav, a daily paper of Israel.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956), p. 64 reads:

> Satindra Singh is on the editorial staff of The Indian Express (Delhi) and represents Lamerhav, a daily paper of Israel. He contributed 'Prospects of Indian Democracy' to QUEST Vol. I. No. 4.
