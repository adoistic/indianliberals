V. Anant wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> V. Anant has contributed to the New Statesman and Nation and other British journals.
