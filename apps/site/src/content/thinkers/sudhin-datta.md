Sudhin Datta contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 18 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> SUDHINDRANATH DATTA is a leading poet and critic in Bengali and lectures on Comparative Literature at Jadavpur University. The quarterly Parichaya edited by him in the thirties was mainly responsible for introducing new trends in poetry and criticism into Bengali literature.
