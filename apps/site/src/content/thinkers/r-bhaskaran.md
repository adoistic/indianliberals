R. Bhaskaran contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963'), p. 107 reads:

> R. BHASKARAN is Head of the Department of Politics and Public Administration at Madras University.
