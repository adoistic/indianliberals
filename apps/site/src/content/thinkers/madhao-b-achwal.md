Madhao B. Achwal wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960) to No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> MADHAO B. ACHWAL is a practising architect, and teaches architecture in the M. S. University, Baroda; a literary critic and essayist in Marathi.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:
