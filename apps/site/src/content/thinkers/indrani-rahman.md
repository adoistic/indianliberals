Indrani Rahman wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Indrani Rahman, in private life the wife of a distinguished govt. architect, is a well-known exponent of Bharat Natyam. She has for some time now been receiving training in Orissi and taking a keen interest in its revival. She is the first dancer to present Orissi on the professional stage.
