Walter Z. Laqueur wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956), p. 70 reads:

> Walter Z. Laqueur is the London correspondent of The New Leader (New York) and an authority on the Middle East, with several publications to his credit.
