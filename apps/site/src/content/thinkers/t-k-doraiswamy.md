T. K. Doraiswamy contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58) to No. 31 (OCT./DEC. 1961 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1961' with the numeral '31' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 100 reads:

> T. K. DORAISWAMY teaches English in a Trivandrum college.
