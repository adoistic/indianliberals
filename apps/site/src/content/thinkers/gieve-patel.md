Gieve Patel contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965') to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 109 reads:

> GIEVE PATEL is a final year medical student of Bombay University.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 105 reads:

> GIEVE PATEL'S poems have been published in QUEST. His first book of poems is expected to appear shortly.
