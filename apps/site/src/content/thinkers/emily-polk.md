Emily Polk wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 17 (April - June 1958) to No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Emily Polk hails from the United States but lives in Calcutta. Her collection of poems The Invisible Queen is shortly to be published in England.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 22 (July/Sept. 1959 (quarterly), p. 101 reads:

> EMILY POLK, an American poetess living with her architect husband in Calcutta, has just published her first collection of poems.
