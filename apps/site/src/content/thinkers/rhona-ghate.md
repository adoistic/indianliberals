Rhona Ghate contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> Rhona Ghate holds an economics degree of the London University and is an M. A. in English literature of Nagpur. Mrs. Ghate has been a teacher and a frequent contributor to Indian periodicals.
