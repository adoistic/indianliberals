Agyeya contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:

> AGYEYA is the pen-name of S. H. VATSYAYAN who initiated the modern period of Hindi literature and is regarded by many critics as its most significant new voice.
