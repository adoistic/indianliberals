Raj Chatterjee wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:

> RAJ CHATTERJEE prematurely retired from his post of Assist. Personnel Director of the Imperial Tobacco Co. to devote all his time to journalism and fiction.
