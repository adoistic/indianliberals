Sadanand Bhatkal wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956), p. 73 reads:

> Sadanand Bhatkal is the Hon. Secretary of the Federation of Publishers' and Booksellers' Associations in India.
