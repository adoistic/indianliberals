Monika Varma wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 69 reads:

> Monika Varma (Surajpur, PEPSU) has published her short stories and poems in a number of Indian periodicals.
