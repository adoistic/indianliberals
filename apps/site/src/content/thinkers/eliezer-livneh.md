Eliezer Livneh wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957) to Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> Eliezer Livneh, born in Russia 1902, has lived in Israel since 1920. Pioneer and settler in the Ein-Hazod communal settlement 1920-23. Member of the Hagana, (an illegal defence organisation) 1939-47, director of its cultural department, editor for the military review Maazachoth and the weekly Eshnav. In 1948-49 he edited the official daily of the Mapi, Israel Labour Party. From 1949 to 1955 he was a member of the Israeli Parliament.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> Eliezer Livneh has lived in Israel since 1920 and was a member of its Parliament from 1949 to 1955. His article Sidelights on Israel appeared in Quest Vol. II, No. 4 February/March 1957 issue.
