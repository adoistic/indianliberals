Philip Altbach wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964'), p. 109 reads:

> PHILIP ALTBACH is National Chairman of the Student Peace Union (U.S.A.) and is working toward his Ph.D. degree in the Comparative Education Centre at the University of Chicago.
