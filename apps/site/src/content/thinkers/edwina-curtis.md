Edwina Curtis wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page), p. 110 reads:

> EDWINA CURTIS studied at the Boston School of Art. She and her husband the painter Barney Rubenstein have been living in France since 1952. Exhibitions in Paris and New York.
