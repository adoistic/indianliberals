Keshev Malik wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> KESHEV MALIK has studied at Srinagar, Florence, Paris and New York. His first book The Lake Surface and other Poems appeared last year.
