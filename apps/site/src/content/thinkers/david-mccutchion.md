David McCutchion contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 17 issues of the archive's run (No. 18 (July-September 1958) to No. 51 (OCT./DEC. 1966 (front cover dates the issue 'autumn 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 18 (July-September 1958), p. 91 reads:

> David McCutchion graduated with honours in Modern Languages from Cambridge and is at present teaching English Literature at Vishwa Bharati University, Santiniketan.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> DAVID McCUTCHION graduated with honours in Modern Languages from Cambridge and is at present teaching English Literature at the Visvabharati University, Santiniketan.
