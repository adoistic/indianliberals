Tomoo Otaka wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 4 (February-March 1956), p. 67 reads:

> Tomoo Otaka's paper on 'Authoritarianism in Japan' was presented to the Milan Conference on the Future of Freedom, organised by the Congress for Cultural Freedom.
