Gladys Bhai wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 47 (OCT./DEC. 1965), p. 107 reads:

> GLADYS BHAI, a busy housewife, has published fifteen short stories and twenty articles in various periodicals mostly in India and some abroad.
