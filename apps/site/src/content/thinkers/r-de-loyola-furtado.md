R. de Loyola Furtado wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 17 (April - June 1958) to No. 26 (July/Sept 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> R. de Loyola Furtado is Lecturer in English Literature at Punjab University. His collection of poems The Centre has been published by Cromlech Press, England; Shelly :Concept of Nature, a book of literary criticism, is now in the press .

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> R. de LOYOLA FURTADO, Lecturer in English Literature at Punjab University, was a contributor to Quest 17.
