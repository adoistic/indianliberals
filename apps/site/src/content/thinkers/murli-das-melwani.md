Murli Das Melwani wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62') to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62'), p. 95 reads:

> MURLI DAS MELWANI is in business at Shillong and writes short story in his spare time.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965'), p. 109 reads:

> MURLI DAS MELWANI is a young businessman in Shillong, who has had more than a dozen short stories published in various Indian magazines. A previous story appeared in Quest 32.
