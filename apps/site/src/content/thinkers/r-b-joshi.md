R. B. Joshi wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue) to Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> R. B. Joshi (Bombay) is a well-known Marathi travelogue writer. He has also published short stories. His article Off the Road in Berar appeared in the Marathi Literature Special issue of Quest.
