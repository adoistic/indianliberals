Mangesh Padki contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue) to Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> Mangesh Padki is a leading short story writer of the younger generation.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> Mangesh Padki, translator of The Devil was born in 1923 in Karwar, lives in Poona.
