Ka Naa Subramanyam contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 10 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57) to No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> Ka Naa Subramanyam is a distinguished Tamil novelist and critic. He is a keen student of modern English, European and American writing.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> KA NAA SUBRAMANYAM is one of the most distinguished names in modern Tamil literature. Author of many volumes of short stories and novels; an English translation of his novel is shortly to be published in England.
