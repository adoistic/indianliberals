C. R. M. Rao wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> C. R. M. Rao has been writing on a variety of subjects in Indian periodicals for the past ten years. He is at present in Delhi. Age 30

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / '), p. 111 reads:

> C. R. M. RAO is a young journalist who "has been writing off and on in Indian and foreign journals for over a decade."
