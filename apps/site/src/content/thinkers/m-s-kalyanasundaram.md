M. S. Kalyanasundaram contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57) to No. 20 (January-March 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> M. S. Kalyanasundaram was born in Madura in 1901. Started writing in Tamil in 1928. Won a prize for his story Abdul Qudir which was published in the well-known Tamil weekly Ananda Viketan. The Message first appeared in the same journal in 1934 and later in Munshi Premchand's Hans in a Hindi translation by the author.
