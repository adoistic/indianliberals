Dorothy Norman wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> DOROTHY NORMAN founded and was chairman of American Citizens' Committee for Economic Aid Abroad, 1952-53; and is currently chairman of Citizens' Committee to support U.S. Aid for India. Her volume of poems Dualities was published by Alfred Stieglitz in 1933.
