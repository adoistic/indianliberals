American classical liberal writer and editor, associated with the Atlas Network
and Students For Liberty. He appears in this archive as the editor of *After the
Welfare State*, the 2012 anthology reprinted in India by the Centre for Civil
Society with a foreword by Bibek Debroy, to which he contributed the
introduction and several of the essays.
