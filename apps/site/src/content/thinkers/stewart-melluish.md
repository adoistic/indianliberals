Stewart Melluish contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page), p. 103 reads:

> STEWART MELLUISH has been in India for about five years, as head of the Oxford University Press in Madras.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964'), p. 106 reads:

> STEWART MELLUISH represents the Oxford University Press in Madras.
