Nirendranath Chakravarti wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 69 reads:

> Nirendranath Chakravarti (Calcutta) is on the editorial staff of the Ananda Bazar Patrika. His poems have appeared widely in Bengali periodicals.
