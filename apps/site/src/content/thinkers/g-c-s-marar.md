G. C. S. Marar wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25') to No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 108 reads:

> G. C. S. MARAR has contributed verse to Extension, Atlantic and Poetry of U.S.A.
