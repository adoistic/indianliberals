Pradip Sen wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960) to No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> PRADIP SEN is a business executive and an active member of a small group of young writers who run a Writers' Workshop in Calcutta.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> PRADIP SEN is a Bengali but he writes exclusively in English. His first book of verse And then the Sun came out last year.
