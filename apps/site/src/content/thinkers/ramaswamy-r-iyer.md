Ramaswamy R. Iyer wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956) to No. 17 (April - June 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 40 reads:

> Ramaswamy Iyer is now Under Secretary in the Ministry of Food and Agriculture, Government of India.
