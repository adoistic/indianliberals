William Phillips wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961) to No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> WILLIAM PHILLIPS is a well-known writer and the editor of the distinguished American quarterly Partisan Review. He will be contributing 'Letter from New York' for every alternate issue of Quest.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 33 (APRIL/JUNE 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1962' with the numeral '33' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 108 reads:

> WILLIAM PHILLIPS is a wellknown American writer and editor of the distinguished quartely Partisan Review. These letters from New York are exclusive to Quest.
