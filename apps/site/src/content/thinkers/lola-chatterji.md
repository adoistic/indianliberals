Lola Chatterji reviewed for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 5 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956) to No. 26 (July/Sept 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Lola Chatterji teaches English Literature at Loreto College, Calcutta.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 100 reads:

> LOLA CHATTERJI hails from the U.S.A. but has spent the best part of her life as the wife of an eminent Bengali writer. Her first book of verse will be appearing soon.
