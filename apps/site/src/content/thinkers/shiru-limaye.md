Shiru Limaye wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> Shiru Limaye is a well-known Praja Socialist leader of Maharastra who led a batch of Satyagrahis to Goa in 1955. He is at present serving a long term in a Portuguese prison, after being convicted by a Military Court.
