K. Raghavendra Rao wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (No. 17 (April - June 1958) to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Raghavendra Rao is Lecturer in Political Science at the University of Gauhati. His poems have appeared in Poetry, Chicago.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> K. Raghavendra Rao hails from Mysore and lectures on Politics at Ghauhati University, Assam.
