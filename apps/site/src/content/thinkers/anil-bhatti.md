Anil Bhatti wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> ANIL BHATTI, still in his teens, is a science student. This is his first publication outside school and college magazines.
