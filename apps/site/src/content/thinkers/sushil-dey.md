Sushil Dey wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960) to No. 48 (JAN./MAR. 1966 (front cover: 'winter 1966')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> SUSHIL DEY, a director in the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations at Rome, has published articles on economics and current affairs.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 28 (Jan/Mar 1961), p. 111 reads:

> SUSHIL DEY, an ex-member of the Indian Civil Service, is at present a director in the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations. He writes: "the ideas expressed in the article are entirely my own and are not meant to represent the views of the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations, which I am serving."
