D. G. Nadkarni contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 14 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955) to No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> D. G. Nadkarni writes regularly both in English and in Marathi on theatre, the cinema and literature. He is a lecturer in English Literature at the Bombay College.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56), p. 68 reads:

> D. G. Nadkarni is a lecturer in English Literature at the Bombay College.
