Joseph Roggendorf wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Rev. Father Joseph Roggendorf, S. J., is a German scholar and linguist who has lived for 20 years in Japan. He is Dean of the Graduate School of Sophia University in Tokyo, a leading authority on Japanese literature, and edits the Jochi quarterly magazine.
