Peter Hyun translated for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 20 (January-March 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 20 (January-March 1959), p. 106 reads:

> Peter Hyun, educated in Seoul, New York, Madrid and Paris, has contributed verses and articles to New World Writing, The Westminister Review, l'Express etc.
