N. K. Devaraja wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (No. 26 (July/Sept 1960) to No. 43 (OCT./DEC. 1964 (contents page); cover dated 'autumn 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 26 (July/Sept 1960), p. 103 reads:

> N. K. DEVARAJA is Assistant Professor of Philosophy at the University of Lucknow and has published fiction and literary criticism in Hindi.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page), p. 110 reads:

> N. K. DEVARAJA is Sayaji Rao Gaekwad Professor of Indian Civilisation and Culture and Head of the Department of Indian Philosophy at Banaras Hindu University.
