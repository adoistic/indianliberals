Eugene Kamenka wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 19 (Oct/Dec 1958), p. 103 reads:

> Eugene Kamenka, a graduate of Sydney University, is at present Lecturer in Philosophy, University of Malaya, Singapore.
