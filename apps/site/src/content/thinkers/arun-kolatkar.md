Arun Kolatkar contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956) to Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 75 reads:

> Arun Kolatkar is now in Madras. Two of his poems appeared in QUEST Volume 1, Number 1. (August-September 1955). He writes both in English and in Marathi.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 5 (April–May 1957 (Marathi Literature Special Issue), p. 24 reads:

> Arun Kolatkar is a versatile illustrator who also writes poems and stories in both Marathi and English. Some of his poems have been published in QUEST.
