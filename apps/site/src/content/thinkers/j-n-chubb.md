J. N. Chubb contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956) to No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

> J. N. Chubb is professor of Philosophy at Elphinstone College, Bombay.
