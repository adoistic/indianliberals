G. Sukumaran Nair wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 5 (April-May 1956) to Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 24 reads:

> G. Sukumaran Nair is the Secretary of the Indian Philosophical Congress.
