Hoang van Chi wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25), p. 114 reads:

> HOANG VAN CHI joined the Viet Minh resistance against the French, but his opposition to Ho Chi Minh's Communist rule led him to escape from North Vietnam in 1955. Author of From Colonialism to Communism: A Case History of North Vietnam (Praeger and Pall Mall Press 1964).
