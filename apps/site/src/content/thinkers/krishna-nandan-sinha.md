Krishna Nandan Sinha wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 24 (Jan./Mar. 1960), p. 103 reads:

> KRISHNA NANDAN SINHA holds a doctorate from Arkansas and is at present Head of the English Department, Gaya College. His work on T. S. Eliot is awaiting publication.
