A. Subbiah wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955) to No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 2 (October-November 1955), p. 72 reads:

> A. Subbiah is a Vice President of the Academy of Tamil Culture.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 1 (August-September 1956), p. 75 reads:

> A. Subbiah (Madras) is a Vice President of the Academy of Tamil Culture.
