G. Burce Bunao wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 2 issues of the archive's run (No. 31 (OCT./DEC. 1961 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1961' with the numeral '31' and 'rupees 1.25') to No. 40 (No. , JAN./MAR. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the same issue 'winter 1963-64'; price rupees 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 31 (OCT./DEC. 1961 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1961' with the numeral '31' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 98 reads:

> G. BURCE BUNAO, editor of Comment, experiments in these poems to combine Filipine imagination with Japanese form

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 40 (No. , JAN./MAR. 1964 (contents page); cover dates the same issue 'winter 1963-64'; price rupees 1.25), p. 115 reads:

> G. BURCE BUNAO, one of the editors of COMMENT (Manila), has written a play and several short stories and is expecting to have two volumes of his poems published next year.
