Uma Vasudev contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58) to No. 38 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1963 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1963')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 24 reads:

> Uma Vasudev, Delhi, founded and edited Surge, a youth monthly. She writes film criticism regularly for Thought.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 34 (monsoon 1962 (cover); JULY/SEPT. 1962 (contents page), p. 108 reads:

> Uma Vasudev was at one time editor of SURGE, now contributes regularly to various Indian periodicals.
