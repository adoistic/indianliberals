P. Mahadevan wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 21 (April-June 1959)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 21 (April-June 1959), p. 105 reads:

> P. MAHADEVAN was Professor of English of the Madura College and later its Principal until his retirement in 1953. Author of Subramania Bharati, Patriot and Poet.
