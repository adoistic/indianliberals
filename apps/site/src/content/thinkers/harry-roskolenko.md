Harry Roskolenko wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956), p. 70 reads:

> Harry Roskolenko is an American author who has published several novels and volumes of verse. He visited India recently.
