M. Krishnan wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> M. Krishnan is from Mylapore, Madras. He lives exclusively on writing. His interests range from Indian Sculpture and Tamil poetry to wild life and cricket.
