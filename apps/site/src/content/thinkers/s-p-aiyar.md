S. P. Aiyar contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (No. 25 (April/June 1960) to No. 44 (JAN./MAR. 1965 (contents page); cover dated 'winter 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 37 (APRIL/JUNE 1963 (contents page); cover dated 'spring 1963'), p. 111 reads:

> S. P. AIYAR is Edwin Montagu Lecturer in Politics in the University of Bombay.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 39 (autumn 1963 (cover); , OCT./DEC. 1963 (contents page), p. 103 reads:

> S. P. AIYAR is the Edwin-Montagu lecturer in Political Science in the University of Bombay. He is at present editing a book on The Foundations of Indian Democracy.
