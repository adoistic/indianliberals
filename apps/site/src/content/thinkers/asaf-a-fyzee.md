Asaf A. Fyzee wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 41 (No. , APRIL - JUNE 1964 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1964')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 41 (No. , APRIL - JUNE 1964 (contents page); cover reads 'spring 1964'), p. 105 reads:

> ASAF A. FYZEE has been principal of Law College, Bombay, ambassador of India and member of the Union Public Service Commission. He was visiting professor at McGill University during 1958-59 and at Cambridge University during 1962-63. His publications include Outlines of Mohammedan Law (Oxford) and A Modern Approach to Islam (Asia, 1963).
