Andal contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 4 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57) to No. 18 (July-September 1958)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 3 (Dec. '57 - Jan. 58), p. 40 reads:

> Andal is the pen-name of Mrs. S. Rajee of Madras. Her translation of Subramanya Bharati's English Education appeared in the previous issue of Quest.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 4 (Feb.-March 58), p. 53 reads:

> Andal. Pen-name of Mrs. S. Rajee of Madras
