A. K. Ramanujan contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57) to No. 27 (No. , Oct./Dec. 1960 (contents page); cover reads 'Autumn 1960 / ')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 1 (August-September '57), p. 17 reads:

> A. K. Ramanujan is a lecturer in English literature at Lingaraj College, Belgaum. Most of his literary work is in Kannada but he has had a poem accepted by Poetry London-New York and is now at work on a doctoral thesis on 'Myth and Symbol in W. B. Yeats.' Two of his articles have appeared in the Southern Folklore Quarterly, University of Florida, U.S.A. He is 28.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. III No. 2 (Oct.-Nov. '57), p. 42 reads:

> A. K. RAMANUJAN No te: The 'three eousins' are perhaps the three 'gunas' the thrcc-fukl modes of nature; and 'Kanna' is rendered here as 'the unwilled past.'
