Troy Wilson Organ wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 35 (OCT./DEC. 1962 (contents page); cover reads 'autumn 1962' with the numeral '35' and 'rupees 1.25'), p. 111 reads:

> TROY WILSON ORGAN, Professor of Philosophy, Ohio University, was at Santiniketan during 1958-59 as a Fulbright Fellow. Apart from contributions to numerous journals in USA and India, he is the author of The Examined Life (Houghton-Mifflin, 1956).
