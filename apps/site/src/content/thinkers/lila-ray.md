Lila Ray contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 6 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955) to No. 46 (No. , JULY/SEPT. 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'monsoon 1965')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 1 (August 1955), p. 68 reads:

> Lila Ray is joint author with A. S. Ray of Bengali Literature. She has translated extensively from Bengali. An anthology of short stories in translation is in press.

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 17 (April - June 1958), p. 95 reads:

> Lila Ray has earned reputation by her English translation of Bengali short stories. Her poetry has recently appeared in The Atlantic.
