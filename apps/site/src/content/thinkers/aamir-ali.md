Aamir Ali contributed to *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 10 issues of the archive's run (Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56) to No. 45 (APRIL/JUNE 1965 (contents page); cover reads 'quest 45 ... spring 1965', rupee 1.25)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. I No. 3 (Dec. '55—Jan. '56), p. 66 reads:

> Aamir Ali is on the staff of the International Labour Organisation, Geneva.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 3 (December 1956-January 1957), p. 79 reads:

> Aamir Ali is on the staff of the International Labour Organisation, Geneva. He contributed 'I See A Bull Fight' to QUEST Vol. No. 3 (December 1955).
