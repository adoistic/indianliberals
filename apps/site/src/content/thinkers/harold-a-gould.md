Harold A. Gould wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in one issue of the archive's run (No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62')).

*Quest*'s contributor note in No. 32 (JAN./MAR. 1962 (front cover dates the issue 'winter 1961-'62'), p. 95 reads:

> HAROLD A. GOULD a cultural anthropologist, specially interested in Indian history, has been in this country for a number of years now.
