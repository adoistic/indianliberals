D. D. Karve wrote for *Quest*, the journal of ideas sponsored by the Indian Committee for Cultural Freedom, appearing in 3 issues of the archive's run (Vol. II No. 2 (October-November 1956) to Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57)).

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 4 (February-March 1957), p. 10 reads:

> D. D. Karve is Principal of the Fergusson College, Poona.

*Quest*'s contributor note in Vol. II No. 6 (June-July '57), p. 48 reads:

> D. D. Karve is the Principal of Fergusson College, Poona.
