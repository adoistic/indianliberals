The Indian Liberals Archive is a valuable repository of works supporting free enterprise and India’s liberal tradition. Researchers and enthusiasts can access a range of rare primary and secondary sources in one place. I have used the archive extensively for my research and writing, and I believe this collection will continue to enable scholars to undertake sound archival research. Those based outside India’s major cities, without the resources or opportunity to access these documents physically, will stand to benefit immensely from this archive.
