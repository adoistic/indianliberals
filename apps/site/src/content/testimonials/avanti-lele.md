The Indian Liberals Archive is a unique resource, combining a user-friendly interface with a diverse collection of musings, opinion pieces, digitised books, and archival material. It is a treasure trove for researchers across disciplines. As the Minoo Masani Liberal Fellow, I regularly used the Archive to trace the evolving interpretations of liberalism. It has also been invaluable to my doctoral research in South Asian literature.
