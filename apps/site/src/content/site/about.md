Indian Liberals is a digital archive of primary works by historically significant Indian liberal thinkers, alongside thinker profiles, organisation pages, curated excerpts (“musings”), opinion pieces, interviews, and a federated feed from ThePrint’s “Indian Liberals Matter” column.

The archive is maintained by the [Centre for Civil Society](https://ccs.in). The site you are reading was rebuilt by [Thothica](https://thothica.com) using the same architectural template that powers [Falsafa](https://falsafa.ai) and [Liberty Lighthouse](https://libertylighthouse.ccs.in).
