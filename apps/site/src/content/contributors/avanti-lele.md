Avanti Lele is a Minoo Masani Liberal Fellow. She is pursuing her PhD in English
Literature at Lancaster University, and has worked as a lecturer in English
Literature and as a Spanish language instructor. Her research interests include
women's writing, liberal feminism, postcolonial studies and indigeneity.
