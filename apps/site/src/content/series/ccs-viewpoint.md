  Books and pamphlets from the Centre for Civil Society in New Delhi. The run
  began in 1997 with the numbered Viewpoint pamphlets, each making a single
  contrarian case at essay length: that population causes prosperity, that Peter
  Bauer was a true friend of the world's poor. It has since widened into fuller
  volumes, among them Milton Friedman's two pieces on Indian planning recovered
  from the 1950s, the S. V. Raju festschrift, and an anthology of essays by young
  Indians on what it means to be a liberal here. Written for circulation rather
  than the seminar room, they mark the arrival of a younger, campaigning
  liberalism after 1991.
