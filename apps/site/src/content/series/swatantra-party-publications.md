blurb: >-
  The pamphlet output of India's first liberal party. Founded in 1959 by
  C. Rajagopalachari, Minoo Masani and N. G. Ranga against the Nagpur resolution
  on cooperative farming, the Swatantra Party fought four general elections and
  argued its case chiefly in cheap pamphlets sold at meetings and posted to
  members: the Statement of Principles of 1959, the election manifestos, the
  annual answer to the Union Budget, and a run of short polemics on land reform,
  devaluation, planning and poverty. Read in sequence they are the closest thing
  India has to a sustained liberal opposition programme, written while the
  argument was still live.
