blurb: >-
  Instituted after the death of A. D. Shroff in 1965, the Forum's annual
  memorial lecture is the flagship of its booklet run and the closest thing
  Indian liberalism has to a standing public platform. Each year a figure from
  banking, industry, the judiciary or the academy was invited to take stock —
  K. Santhanam on federal financial relations, C. D. Deshmukh on the illiterate
  citizen, M. C. Chagla on the judiciary in a parliamentary democracy, and in
  later decades Nandan Nilekani, Subir Gokarn and D. Subbarao. The lectures were
  printed as Forum booklets shortly after delivery, which is how they survive
  here.
