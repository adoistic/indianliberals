  English in 2007 and in Hindi in 2010 — the only full-length biography of Masani
  in the collection. The run is not numbered on the books themselves; sibling
  titles listed in the 2007 volume include lives of Dadasaheb Phalke, Homi
  Jehangir Bhabha, Hasrat Mohani, Indira Gandhi and Yusuf Meherally.
