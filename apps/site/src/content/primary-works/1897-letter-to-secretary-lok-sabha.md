## Summary

This single-page English letter, dated August 1965, is addressed to the Secretary of the Lok Sabha in New Delhi. The unidentified Member of Parliament gives formal notice of a no-confidence motion and requests that it be placed on the agenda of the House's first sitting.

## Key points

- The document is a formal parliamentary notice letter.
- It is dated August 1965.
- The recipient is the Secretary of the Lok Sabha.
- The sender's name and signature are not visible.
- The proposed motion states that the House has no confidence in the Council of Ministers.
- The sender authorises the Secretary to make any necessary wording changes to conform to normal form.
