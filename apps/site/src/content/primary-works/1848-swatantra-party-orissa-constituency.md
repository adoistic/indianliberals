## Summary

This single-page English administrative record headed “ORISSA” lists seven individuals or groups connected with prospective membership, local organisation, or support for the Swatantra Party. The remarks document requests for membership, interest in studying anti-Sanskrit and anti-English movements, offers to work for the party, and expressions of support for its political position.

## Key points

- The record contains seven numbered entries from people and organisations in Puri, Parlakhemundi, Cuttack, Ganjam, and Balasore.
- Several correspondents seek membership in, or information about joining, the Swatantra Party.
- One entry offers to work for the party in Cuttack but gives no definite commitment.
- Hari Hara Das reports that his friends believe the party could voice the feelings of a large number of people.
- Pandit A.S.N. Murti welcomes the party's new endeavour and proposes that an invitation be sent to Rajaji.
- A Bar Association representative asks for guidance on organising the party in Orissa.
- S.K. Bhatta requests visits by Rajaji, Masani, and Menon to Cuttack to assist with organisation.
