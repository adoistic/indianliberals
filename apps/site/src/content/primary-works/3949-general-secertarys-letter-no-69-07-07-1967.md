## Key points

- The letter addresses eligibility during a temporary grace period for members whose subscriptions are expiring.
- Membership valid on June 30, 1967, determines inclusion in the party Membership Registers.
- The Membership Registers function as the electoral rolls for the party elections.
- Members whose subscriptions expire between July 1 and November 30, 1967, remain eligible to vote.
- Those members cannot stand as candidates unless they renew their subscriptions on or before the date of expiry.
- Returning Officers must verify that candidates are members in good standing when accepting nominations.
