## Summary

This one-page Swatantra Party circular, dated April 20, 1966, announces a change in the date and venue of the Central Parliamentary Board's next meeting. The meeting is rescheduled for Thursday, May 26, at 10:00 a.m., at Sir Homi Mody's residence in Bombay, replacing the previously announced May 15 date.

The agenda focuses on internal party administration and electoral preparation: confirming the minutes of the February meeting in Madras, reviewing the functioning of the party's parliamentary and state-assembly groups, assessing the progress of prospective parliamentary candidates, considering processed candidate recommendations, and taking up any other business with the chair's permission. The circular is signed by M. R. Masani, General Secretary.

## Key points

- The circular is dated April 20, 1966, and was issued by the Swatantra Party's Central Office in Bombay.
- The Central Parliamentary Board meeting is rescheduled from May 15 to May 26, 1966.
- The meeting is to be held at Sir Homi Mody's residence in Carmichael Road, Bombay.
- The board will confirm the minutes of its February 26–27 meeting in Madras and consider resulting action.
- The agenda includes reviewing the party's groups in Parliament and the state assemblies.
- The board will assess prospective parliamentary candidates and consider processed candidate recommendations.
- M. R. Masani signs the circular as General Secretary.
