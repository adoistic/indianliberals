## Key points

- The sender responds to press reports about Jayaprakash Narayan's health and asks for clarification.
- The letter refers to a draft chapter of Narayan's memoirs and an earlier letter dated October 2.
- The sender expects Narayan to begin telling the story of the years 1939–1940 within the next few weeks.
- Narayan is urged to record his own recollections of the period he has just completed.
- The letter frames Narayan's experience as a story that should be told and as evidence bearing on his political conduct.
- The sender asks whether Narayan has considered returning to public life and requests an account of his thoughts and plans.
