## Key points

- The document is Central Office Circular No. 46, issued by the Swatantra Party on June 16, 1966.
- It announces General Secretary M. R. Masani's travel itinerary from June 19 to July 3, 1966.
- The itinerary covers Bombay, Rajkot, Delhi, Bangalore, Mysore, Madras, and Calcutta.
- Flight numbers, departure and arrival times, hotels, and local contact addresses are provided.
- The circular is signed by S. V. Raju in his capacity as Executive Secretary.
- No political programme, speech, policy statement, or explanation of the visits is included.
