## Key points

- The document is an official Swatantra Party office notice.

- The meeting was scheduled for Friday, November 13, 1959.
- The meeting time was 6 p.m.
- The venue was the Swatantra Party office.
- The notice is signed by D. Lynn, identified as Honorary Secretary.
