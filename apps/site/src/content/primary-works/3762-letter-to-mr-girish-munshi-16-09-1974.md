## Summary

This one-page English office letter, dated September 16, 1974, is issued from the Swatantra Party national headquarters in Bombay and signed by V. P. Bhatia. It addresses Girish Munshi as “Girishbhai” and confirms an appointment with Piloo Mody on Thursday, September 19, 1974, at 11 a.m. in Mody & Colgan’s office at Stadium House, Churchgate, Bombay 20. The letter states that the appointment has also been confirmed with Parmanandji and asks Munshi to confirm it when he meets Mody.

Handwritten marginal notes appear in the lower-right portion of the page, apparently adding political or organisational reminders, but their wording is only partly legible. The page provides evidence of routine coordination within the Swatantra Party at the time of its 1974 dissolution, but it does not explain the purpose or outcome of the proposed meeting.

## Key points

- The letter is dated September 16, 1974 and printed on Swatantra Party national headquarters letterhead.
- V. P. Bhatia confirms an appointment between Girish Munshi and Piloo Mody.
- The meeting is scheduled for September 19, 1974, at 11 a.m.
- The specified venue is Mody & Colgan’s office in Stadium House, Churchgate, Bombay 20.
- The appointment has also been confirmed with Parmanandji.
- Handwritten marginal notes are present but are only partly legible and cannot be fully interpreted.
