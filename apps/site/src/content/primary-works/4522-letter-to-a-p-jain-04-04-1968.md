## Key points

- Masani writes to A. P. Jain on 4 June 1968.
- He acknowledges Jain's letters of 25 and 29 May.
- He thanks Jain for sending newspaper clippings, some of which he had not previously seen.
- He plans to discuss the Madras proceedings when visiting Delhi on 1 July.
- The visit is connected with Parliamentary Accounts Committee meetings.
- He identifies The Hindu and The Times of India as reasonably accurate sources for interim reporting.
