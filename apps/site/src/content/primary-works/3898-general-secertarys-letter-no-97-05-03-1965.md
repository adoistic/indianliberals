## Key points

- M. R. Masani writes in his capacity as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- Two State Units have scheduled their State Conventions for the same weekend.
- The scheduling conflict is attributed to insufficient consultation and coordination with the Central Office.
- The Central Office’s office-bearers cannot attend both conventions simultaneously.
- State Presidents and General Secretaries are asked to consult the Central Office before making similar decisions.
