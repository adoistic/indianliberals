## Summary

This one-page telegram, sent from Madras on 6 August 1974 and addressed to M. R. Masani in Bombay, conveys congratulations for what the sender calls Masani’s “heroic lead at Delhi.” The message says that Masani “saved our honour” and concludes with the attribution “Rajaji spoke through you,” invoking C. Rajagopalachari as a source of political or moral authority.

The document is a brief congratulatory political communication rather than an essay or extended argument. Its sender is not clearly identified on the rendered page; the closing text appears to read “Chanakyan Ramabhadran,” but this cannot be confidently established as a signature. The telegram form is issued by the Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department.

## Key points

- The document is a one-page telegram addressed to M. R. Masani.
- It is dated 6 August 1974 and routed from Madras to Bombay.
- The sender congratulates Masani on his “heroic lead at Delhi.”
- The message claims that Masani “saved our honour.”
- It states that “Rajaji spoke through you,” identifying C. Rajagopalachari as an invoked political authority.
- The sender is not securely identified; “Chanakyan Ramabhadran” appears at the end of the message but may be a signature or attribution.
- The telegram form contains printed English and Hindi administrative instructions from the Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department.
