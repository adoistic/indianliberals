## Key points

- The document is an organisational directory rather than an argumentative essay.
- It records Swatantra Party state-unit office bearers elected in 1967.
- Listings generally identify a President and General Secretary.
- The Bihar entry identifies T. Parmanand as Convener.
- The directory covers 14 state or regional units across India.
- Addresses, telephone numbers, and telegram addresses are supplied for many units.
- H. M. Patel is listed as Gujarat President, while Bhanu Pratap Singh is listed as Uttar Pradesh President.
