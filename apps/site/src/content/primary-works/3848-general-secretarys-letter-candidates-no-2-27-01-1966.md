## Key points

- The letter is dated 27 January 1966 and is issued from the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay.
- It concerns the Party’s scheme for financial assistance to prospective parliamentary candidates.
- A meeting is announced for New Delhi on Saturday and Sunday, 19 and 20 February.
- The meeting’s exact time, venue, and agenda were to be communicated later.
- Candidates are asked to arrange their own accommodation in Delhi.
- Travel and out-of-pocket expenses may be met from Party funds for candidates who face difficulty covering them.
- The letter requests written confirmation of attendance and is signed by M. R. Masani as General Secretary.
