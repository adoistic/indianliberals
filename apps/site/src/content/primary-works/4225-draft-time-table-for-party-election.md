## Summary

This one-page document, titled "Draft Timetable for Party Elections," sets out a staged schedule for organisational elections. It gives deadlines from July 31, 1964, for enrolling Workers and Life Workers and receiving Register Entries and subscription quotas, followed by scrutiny and verification of registers by September 15. Elections are then scheduled at the primary (constituency), district, and state-committee levels during October and November 1964.

## Key points

- Worker and Life Worker enrolment for party-election purposes is scheduled to close on July 31, 1964.
- Register Entries and the Centre's subscription quota are due by August 15, 1964.
- Scrutiny and verification of Workers' and Life Workers' registers is to conclude by September 15, 1964.
- Primary-level elections are scheduled for completion by October 15, followed by district-level elections by October 31.
- Elections to State Committees and the General Council are scheduled for November 15 and November 30, respectively.
- The General Council is to elect the National Executive by December 31, 1964, with the new Executive assuming office on January 1, 1965.
- Constituency, district, and state committees are to assume office immediately after their respective elections.
