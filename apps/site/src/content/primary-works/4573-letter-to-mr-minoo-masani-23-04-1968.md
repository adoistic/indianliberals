## Summary

This one-page letter, dated 23 April 1968, is from Sunkara Satyanarayana, General Secretary of the Andhra Pradesh Swatantra Party, to M. R. Masani. Satyanarayana encloses Resolution No. 5 of the party's Executive Committee, passed at a meeting in Bapatla on 19 April 1968.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 23 April 1968 and was sent from the Andhra Pradesh Swatantra Party office in Vijayawada.
- Sunkara Satyanarayana identifies himself as the party's General Secretary.
- The enclosed document is Resolution No. 5 of the Andhra Pradesh Swatantra Party Executive Committee.
- The resolution was passed at a meeting held in Bapatla on 19 April 1968.
- Satyanarayana responds to Masani's resignation from the Central Party's Executive Committee and Parliamentary Board.
- The resignation followed the death of the party's official candidate in the recent Rajya Sabha biennial elections from Gujarat.
- Satyanarayana asks Masani to withdraw his resignation in the interest of the party.
