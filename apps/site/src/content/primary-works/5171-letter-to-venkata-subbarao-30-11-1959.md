## Key points

- The letter is dated 30 November 1959.
- Masani is replying to a letter from Venkata Subbarao dated 18 November.
- Masani had already made reservations at the Ritz Hotel.
- He therefore considers it unnecessary to trouble Khan Bahadur Taraporevala.
- Masani states that he will arrive in Hyderabad at 10:10 on Tuesday morning, 8 December.
