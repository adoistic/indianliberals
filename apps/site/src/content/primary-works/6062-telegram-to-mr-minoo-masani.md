## Key points

- The document is an Indian Post and Telegraphs Department telegram and confirmation copy.
- The sender is identified as JAYAPRAKASH and the recipient as MINOO MASANI.
- The telegram was sent from Patna to Masani’s address on Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
- The confirmation copy is dated 26 June 1969; the first page also bears a 28-6-69 date stamp.
- Jayaprakash refers to an introduction to the work “Socialism Reconsidered.”
- The message expresses disappointment that the promised introduction has not been received.
- The record contains no printed title or publication information.
