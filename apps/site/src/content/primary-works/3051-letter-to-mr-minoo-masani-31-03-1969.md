## Key points

- The letter is dated March 31, 1969, and is written from Bombay.
- N. K. Somani, M.P., addresses Minoo Masani.
- Somani refers to forthcoming elections for the PAC, PUC, and Estimates Committees.
- He says the current Estimates Committees are due shortly.
- He notes that Swatantra nominees have served for the last two years.
- Somani asks Masani to arrange his nomination to the PAC.
- The letter ends with personal regards and the signature “N. Somani.”
