## Key points

- The document is Circular No. 1/65 of the Swatantra Party's Central Parliamentary Sub-Committee.
- It is dated March 19, 1965, and issued from the party's Central Office in Bombay.
- The first meeting of the sub-committee is to be held in Dhenkanal, Orissa, on Sunday, March 28.
- The sub-committee was appointed by the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The Board had met in Delhi on March 14.
- Mr. R. N. Singh Deo is tasked with settling and communicating the meeting's time and place.
- M. R. Masani signs the circular as General Secretary.
