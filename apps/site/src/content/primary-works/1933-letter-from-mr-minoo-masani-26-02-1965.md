## Summary

This one-page English letter, dated February 26, 1965, is written by Minoo Masani from 57 Lodhi Estate, New Delhi, to Mr. Ian Singh. Masani thanks Singh for his letter and welcomes him as an associate member of the present Lok Sabha.

## Key points

- The letter is dated February 26, 1965.
- Minoo Masani writes from 57 Lodhi Estate, New Delhi 3.
- The recipient is Mr. Ian Singh, addressed at 29 South Avenue, New Delhi.
- Masani thanks Singh for his letter of February 26.
- Masani states that he and Professor Ranga are pleased to have Singh as a colleague in the present Lok Sabha.
- The letter formally welcomes Singh as an associate member.
