## Summary

This one-page typed letter, dated 3 August 1964 and signed by M. R. Masani, is a short administrative follow-up addressed to Prof. N. G. Ranga. Masani reminds Ranga of an earlier request, sent on 25 July, that Ranga select a full-time organiser for his Chittor constituency so the appointee can attend a Worker's Training Camp being run by the Central Office in Bombay from 15 to 23 August 1964.

Masani presses Ranga to settle the appointment as soon as he returns to Andhra Pradesh after their scheduled meeting in Madras on the 8th, expressing anxiety that the candidate must not be left out of the course or have his appointment needlessly delayed. The letter is on plain paper (no letterhead visible in the rendered page) and carries the reference mrm:pz; the addressee's care-of address is given as c/o I. B. Rao, 36B Motilal Nehru Road, Ballygunge, Calcutta 26.

## Key points

- Letter dated 3 August 1964 from M. R. Masani to Prof. N. G. Ranga.
- Follows up on an earlier request of 25 July asking Ranga to nominate a full-time organiser for his Chittor constituency.
- The Central Office is organising a Worker's Training Camp in Bombay from 15 to 23 August 1964 (inclusive).
- Masani expects the appointment to be finalised right after his meeting with Ranga in Madras on the 8th.
- The letter conveys anxiety that Ranga's nominee should not be excluded from the training course through delay.
- Addressed care of I. B. Rao at 36B Motilal Nehru Road, Ballygunge, Calcutta 26; reference line 'mrm:pz'.
