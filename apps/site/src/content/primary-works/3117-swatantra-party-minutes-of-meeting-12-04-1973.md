summary: |-
  These draft minutes record the Swatantra Party National Executive meeting held at Kalki Buildings, Madras, on 12 April 1973. After confirming the minutes of the previous meeting, the Executive discussed the political situation in Orissa following the dissolution of the Legislative Assembly and the formation of the Pragati Party. The minutes stress that the Orissa unit could cooperate with other groups within the legislature while retaining the Swatantra Party’s separate organisation and identity. An attached note sets out the party’s position for the 1973–74 Orissa mid-term elections, including contesting under its own symbol and negotiating seat allocations with cooperating parties.
  
  The Executive also approved arrangements for the Swatantra Party’s Sixth National Convention, scheduled for 14–15 April in Madras, and considered draft resolutions for adoption. These concerned the current political situation, the economy, election strategy, inter-state river waters, Aligarh Muslim University and Banaras Hindu University, and international relations. The annexures preserve the Orissa State Executive’s condemnation of the Assembly’s dissolution and the Governor’s rule, as well as the detailed convention programme. The document is an institutional record rather than an authored political essay.

## Key points

- The National Executive met in Madras on 12 April 1973 under the chairmanship of party president Piloo Mody.
- The previous meeting’s minutes were confirmed subject to corrections concerning Orissa-related references and a person’s name.
- The Executive discussed the dissolution of the Orissa Assembly and the Orissa unit’s participation in the Pragati Party or a broader United Front arrangement.
- The party resolved to preserve its separate identity, flag, principles, symbol, and organisational structure while cooperating electorally.
- The attached March note prescribed independent Swatantra Party symbols and programmes, with seat allocation based on the 1971 election results and further negotiation.
- The Executive approved the programme for the Sixth National Convention in Madras on 14–15 April 1973.
- Draft resolutions covered election strategy, inter-state river waters, Aligarh Muslim University and Banaras Hindu University, international relations, and the economic situation.
