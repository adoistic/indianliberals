  In this letter dated 8 April 1968, Minoo J. Shroff writes to N. Dandekar about Minoo Masani's resignation from the party's National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board. ## Key points

- The letter is dated 8 April 1968 and is addressed to N. Dandekar.
- It concerns Minoo Masani's resignation from the party's National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- The resignation is characterized as a matter of grave concern.
- The sender requests an emergency General Council meeting.
- If an emergency meeting is impractical, the matter should be prioritized at the next General Council meeting.
- The document is a privately addressed typed letter on Minoo J. Shroff's professional letterhead.
