## Key points

- The letter confirms an earlier conversation with the Regional Transport Officer on March 23, 1976.
- It supplies details of 25 vehicles purchased by the former Swatantra Party and sent to destinations in several Indian states.
- The listed owners include G. B. Sukhee, V. K. Sinha, V. P. Bhatia, S. V. Raju, Padma Vaswani, and M. R. Masani.
- The vehicles were registered in January 1971 and dispatched from the Mahindra & Mahindra works.
- Registration charges and wheel taxes were collected at the Mahindra & Mahindra factory.
- The letter states that none of the vehicles returned to Bombay because they were sold in the areas to which they were sent.
- The sender asks the Regional Transport Office to correct its records and cancel notices issued against the vehicles.
