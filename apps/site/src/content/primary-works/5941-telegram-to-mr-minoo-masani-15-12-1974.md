## Summary

This one-page English telegram, dated 15 December 1974, is addressed to Minoo Masani at 148 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay. Signed “SACHIDANAND,” it responds to Masani’s telegram and clarifies that the sender will not be visiting Delhi or Bombay in December.

## Key points

- The telegram is dated 15 December 1974.
- It is addressed to Minoo Masani in Bombay.
- The sender says they are not going to Delhi or Bombay in December.
- The sender plans to visit Varanasi from 25 to 27 December.
- The message refers to further Delhi travel on the second and third and to a Bombay–Poona visit from 21 to 25 January.
- The sender invites Masani to come to Varanasi if possible.
