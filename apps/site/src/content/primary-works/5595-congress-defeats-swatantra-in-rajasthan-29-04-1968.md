## Key points

- Congress won both the Dausa Lok Sabha and Chomu Vidhan Sabha seats.
- The defeated opponents belonged to the Swatantra Party.
- The contests are described as among the most keenly fought elections in Rajasthan.
- The results were presented as a significant boost to Congress confidence.
- The report discusses the scale of Congress organisation and campaign activity.
- The clipping comments on the Swatantra Party's electoral position and internal campaign effort.
