## Key points

- The letter concerns the Swatantra Party and is dated 30 September 1974.
- It is addressed to Girish K. Munshi, General Secretary of the Swatantra Party (Maharashtra).
- The Election Commission acknowledges Munshi’s correspondence dated 23 August 1974.
- The Commission says that a decision will be taken on requests received from the party’s national organisation, not from a state unit.
- The letter invokes paragraph (1) of the Electoral Rolls (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968.
- K. G. Ganesan signs as Under Secretary (Legal), Election Commission Secretariat.
