  This three-page English letter, dated July 26, 1964, is from Dahyabhai V. Patel in New Delhi to C. Rajagopalachari (“Rajaji”) in Madras.   The typed page focuses on rising prices and the government's inability to control them. Patel argues that the government's responses are adding to public confusion and urges an urgent meeting of the party's Parliamentary Board or Central Organisation Committee to assess the situation, issue directions to its parliamentary members, and consider whether a wider conference resolution should be moved. ## Key points

- The letter is dated July 26, 1964, and is addressed to C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji).
- The handwritten first two pages are only partly legible in the rendered images.
- The typed page identifies rising prices as a growing source of public anger and political danger.
- Patel says government measures have failed to control inflation and have increased confusion.
- He calls for an urgent meeting of the party's Parliamentary Board or Central Organisation Committee.
- He proposes coordinated parliamentary directions and, if necessary, a wider conference resolution.
- He regards a Bombay Docks demonstration as too narrow and symbolic to address the general rise in prices.
- He warns that failure to respond could let opponents depict the party as representing the rich and Rajahs and Maharajas.
