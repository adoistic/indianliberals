## Key points

- The letter is dated April 20, 1992, and issued on Statesman House letterhead in Calcutta.
- Basudev Ray acknowledges receipt of Minoo Masani’s letter dated April 16.
- Masani had already written to the Lok Sabha Secretariat concerning the matter.
- Copies of Masani’s communication were sent to Mr. Irani and Basudev Ray.
- Ray says he will discuss the matter with Mr. Irani and take whatever action is necessary.
- Ray closes by assuring Masani that he will keep him posted.
