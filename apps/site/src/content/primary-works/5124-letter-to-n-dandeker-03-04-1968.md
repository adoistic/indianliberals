## Summary

This one-page letter, dated 3 April 1968 and written from Vellore by V. Padmanabhan to N. Dandekar, requests an emergency meeting of the Swatantra Party General Council. The proposed meeting is intended to discuss M. R. Masani’s resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.

## Key points

- The letter is addressed to N. Dandekar, General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- It is dated 3 April 1968 and originates from Vellore.
- The writer requests an emergency meeting of the Party’s General Council.
- The proposed agenda is M. R. Masani’s resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- The letter calls for a decision that would help the party close ranks.
- It appeals for renewed unity under the leadership of the party’s respected leaders.
