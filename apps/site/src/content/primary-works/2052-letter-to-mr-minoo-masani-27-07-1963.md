## Key points

- The letter is dated 27 July 1963 and issued on Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office letterhead.
- A. P. Jain sends M. R. Masani four drafted parliamentary questions.
- The questions concern grievances of Central Government servants posted in Gujarat.
- The writer divides the material across four questions to comply with parliamentary rules on admissibility and length.
- Dates and similar details are deliberately omitted because they could make the questions suggestive and inadmissible.
- The omitted details can instead be addressed in supplementary questions.
- Jain asks Masani to sign and return the draft so that proper questions can be submitted after the relevant date, observing the required minimum ten days' notice.
