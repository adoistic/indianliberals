## Key points

- Masani plans to visit Benaras from December 25 to 26.
- The visit is intended partly to meet Jayaprakash Narayan.
- Masani asks whether Narayan's replies to his questions can be recorded for possible use in future writing.
- He proposes bringing a tape recorder and cassette tapes, while also asking about a stenographer as an alternative.
- He asks whether the Gandhian Institute of Studies can arrange transport from the airport.
- Masani adopts a deferential tone, saying he will take a taxi if assistance is inconvenient.
