## Key points

- The letter is dated 3rd March 1964 and issued from 57 Lodi Estate, New Delhi.
- M. R. Masani acknowledges Dr. K. L. Rao's D.O. letter No. 10(5)/64, dated 21st February.
- Masani declines an invitation to a meeting on Sunday, 8th March.
- He explains that he will be in Sarangpur on public work for the whole day.
- The letter is addressed to Dr. K. L. Rao as Minister for Irrigation & Power, Government of India.
- The document is a typed office letter bearing Masani's handwritten signature.
