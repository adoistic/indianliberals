## Key points

- Masani received a traffic-offence notice from the Deputy Commissioner of Police, Traffic Control Branch.
- The alleged offence concerned Jeep No. MRG-2347 and was recorded on June 6, 1975, at 18.00 hours.
- Masani states that the vehicle had been sold.
- He requests the buyer's name and address and the date of sale.
- He asks whether the Regional Transport Office was informed and requests a copy of any relevant letter.
- He also asks whether the Bombay Municipal Corporation was informed of the sale.
- The letter emphasizes that the matter should be handled with the utmost speed.
