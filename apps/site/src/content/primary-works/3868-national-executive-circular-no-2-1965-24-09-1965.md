## Key points

- The document is headed "NATIONAL EXECUTIVE CIRCULAR NO. 2/1965."
- It was issued from the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay.
- The circular is dated 24 September 1965.
- The next National Executive meeting was scheduled for Saturday, 30 October.
- The meeting venue was Kalki Buildings, Kilpauk, Madras 10.
- The exact meeting time and agenda were to be communicated later.
- M. R. Masani signs the document as General Secretary.
