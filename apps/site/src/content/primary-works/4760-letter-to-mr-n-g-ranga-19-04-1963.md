## Key points

- Masani asks Ranga to reach Madras by 23 April for the Parliamentary Board meeting and to ensure that Dahyabhai attends.
- He considers Ranga's presence essential to deciding the Rajkot by-election.
- He says the Bihar organisational matter has been implemented and that the chapter should be treated as closed.
- He cautions that the Official Languages Bill may be debated in the Lok Sabha before the Madras meeting ends.
- Masani recommends that party members be reminded that the party has taken no decision on the language issue.
- He defends the right of individual party members to express differing views in constitutional and parliamentary forums.
- He asks members to explain and support the party's policy of freedom of expression while making clear that their positions are personal, not binding party policy.
