## Key points

- The letter is dated June 18, 1985.
- S. V. Raju acknowledges a registered parcel from N. K. Ganapiah.
- The parcel contained ten copies of a memorial lecture delivered by Minoo Masani in Bangalore.
- Raju seeks 100 copies of the speech.
- Raju is contacting C. L. Rao in Bangalore to arrange the additional copies and determine the cost.
- The document is a typed office letter on letterhead with a handwritten signature.
