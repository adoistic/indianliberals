## Key points

- The Bombay Region Executive Committee met on 24 April 1972 at 6.15 p.m.
- Nine members are listed as present, with a repeated number 7 in the attendance list.
- Minutes of meetings held on 24 November 1971 and 12 January 1972 were confirmed with slight modifications.
- The regional financial position was described as acute, prompting a decision to intensify fund collection.
- A small group was asked to devise a suitable fundraising scheme.
- Amounts temporarily spent from Convention Funds for regional expenses were to be reimbursed as early as possible.
- The committee discussed non-renewal of memberships and plans to contact local units to increase membership.
