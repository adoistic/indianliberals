## Key points

- Masani forwards reports originating in the Gujarat press to C. Rajagopalachari.
- He characterizes the reports as part of a regular smear campaign.
- He argues that the campaign reflects a clique at the top rather than the majority view of Gujarat members.
- He links the campaign to what he calls appeasement and weak leadership at the Centre.
- He warns that, unless checked, the trend could spread as far as the episode involving Ramgarh.
- The letter is dated 29 May 1968, marked “Express delivery,” and notes two enclosures that are not included on the visible page.
