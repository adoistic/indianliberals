## Key points

- Handwritten personal note from Prof. N. G. Ranga to Minoo Masani on Ranga's M.P. / Swatantra Party President letterhead, dated 18-3-63.
- Ranga transmits, as enclosure, his own parliamentary reply to a speech on Foreign Affairs delivered in the Lok Sabha that day.
- He records that the House gave the (woman) speaker a good hearing, framing the exchange as a courteous parliamentary encounter.
- Ranga asks Masani to confirm receipt of the copy of his Budget speech already sent separately.
- He observes that Morarji Desai (then Union Finance Minister) has made much of his own reply on the Budget speech.
- Received-stamp is dated 22 MAR 1963 with inward number 02281; a marginal note files the letter under "Bihar Resolution file No. 2".
- The letter is one page of internal Swatantra Party correspondence coordinating two lines of parliamentary opposition — foreign policy and the Union Budget — in the same week.
