## Key points

- M. R. Masani writes to C. P. Daru on 6 October 1975.
- Masani refers to a letter from Daru dated 3 October and says a copy is attached.
- He sends Daru a draft petition to Parliament.
- The draft has not yet been shown to Soli Sorabjee for review.
- Masani asks Daru to revise or develop the draft and have a duplicated version ready for discussion on 12 October.
