## Key points

- The document is General Secretary's Letter No. 133, issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office.
- M. R. Masani informs party members of an Election Commission decision concerning the Party symbol.
- The Swatantra Party's star symbol may be used in all States and Union Territories.
- The Party must report candidate numbers to the relevant Chief Electoral Officer by 31 December 1966.
- The requested information concerns both Assembly and Parliamentary elections.
