## Key points

- Masani writes on Swatantra Party letterhead from New Delhi on 12 November 1970.
- The letter concerns accommodation for Mrs. Albuquerque at Vithalbhai Patel House.
- Masani describes Albuquerque's five years of parliamentary staff and liaison work.
- Her duties included liaison between Swatantra Party MPs and work during mid-term and presidential elections.
- Masani states that she acted as liaison between C. D. Deshmukh and the parliamentary parties supporting his presidential candidacy.
- An attached Central P.W.D. form records accommodation details for V. B. Patel House, including a furnished single/double suite and a date of occupation.
