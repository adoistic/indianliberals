## Summary

In this letter dated 5 September 1962, C. Rajagopalachari tells S. V. Raju that, despite his reluctance and pressure from Diwakar and Debhar, he has agreed to travel to Washington, London, and Paris. The proposed trip is expected to last three weeks, but its departure date remains unsettled because the External Affairs Ministry is awaiting Kennedy’s response about when he can meet the delegation.

## Key points

- Rajagopalachari writes to S. V. Raju from his Madras address.
- He has agreed to undertake a three-week visit to Washington, London, and Paris.
- The trip’s departure date depends partly on when Kennedy will be able to meet the group.
- A departure around 12 September is considered, though a later date is also possible.
- Rajagopalachari may withdraw if the trip is postponed until October because he cannot tolerate the climate then.
- Meetings of the C.O.C. scheduled for 30 September and 1 October may be adjourned indefinitely.
- He has asked Poona University to cancel or postpone his engagement from 2 to 4 October.
- He authorizes Raju to forward the letter to Minoo Masani.
