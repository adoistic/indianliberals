## Summary

This two-page English telegram, dated 7 November 1968, is a confirmation copy sent from Masani in Bombay to the Swatantra Party in New Delhi. It concerns arrangements for an upcoming general-body meeting, including the proposed chairing of the meeting and the expected arrival of Professor Ranga.

## Key points

- The document is an Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department telegram confirmation copy.
- It is dated 7 November 1968 and sent from Bombay to New Delhi.
- The sender is identified only as “MASANI”; the recipient is the Swatantra Party.
- The message discusses a general-body meeting scheduled for the morning of 11 November.
- The sender indicates that they will arrive in Delhi on Sunday evening and can chair the meeting.
- Professor Ranga is expected to reach Delhi on the evening of the 11th.
