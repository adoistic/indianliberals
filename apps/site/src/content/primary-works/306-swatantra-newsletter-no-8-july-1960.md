## Summary

The July 1960, No. 8 issue of the Swatantra Newsletter reports on the Swatantra Party's central meetings, public-aid work, student-politics position, and organisation-building across its state units. It announces Central Organising Committee and General Council meetings in Bombay, including a ticketed open session and functions honouring Rajaji and visiting leaders. The newsletter records the Party's support for the University Grants Commission's recommendations on student discipline and its call for students to remain outside party politics.

Much of the issue is devoted to state-level political activity in Andhra, Assam, Bihar, Bombay, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Madras, Maharashtra, Mysore, Punjab, and Rajasthan. These reports emphasize membership drives, district organisation, public meetings, legal-aid committees, local grievances, and opposition to Congress policies on land ceilings, education fees, and the Hindu Succession Act. The Punjab section is particularly focused on civil-liberty and press-freedom claims concerning the Akali agitation, alleging indiscriminate arrests, press censorship, detentions, and police mistreatment. The issue is complete as rendered, but it is a party newsletter reporting selected activities rather than a systematic account of every state or policy question.
