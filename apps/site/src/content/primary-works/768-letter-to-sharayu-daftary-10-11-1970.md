## Key points

- P. M. Shah writes to Smt. Sharayu Daftary from Bombay on Nov 10, 1970.
- The letter invites Daftary to address a meeting of party workers from Bombay North East District.
- The meeting is scheduled for 22 November at 9:30 a.m. at Brahman Sabha Hall in Kurla.
- Daftary is expected to answer questions from meeting participants.
- Shah says that a circular issued about the meeting is attached.
- A handwritten note records a prior telephone conversation and Daftary’s agreement to address the meeting.
- The letterhead date says 1970, while the body says the meeting is on 22 November 1979.
