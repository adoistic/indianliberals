## Key points

- The letter is dated 25 October 1961 and marked confidential.
- It is addressed to Sri Kamakshya Narayansingh, Raja Bahadur of Ramagarh.
- The subject is the recipient's overdraft account.
- The bank acknowledges receiving a cash payment of Rs. 20,000 through the recipient's representative.
- The payment is identified as the instalment payable for the current month.
- The letter is signed by an unidentified accountant on behalf of the bank.
