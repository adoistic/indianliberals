## Key points

- Rajagopalachari addresses Minoo Masani in a personal letter.
- He expresses approval that a copy of the resolution is being sent to the Prime Minister.
- He asks that another copy be sent formally from the party office.
- The letter is dated 22 May 1964; the document bears a receipt stamp dated 26 May 1964.
- The correspondence concerns political-party procedure and communication with the Prime Minister.
