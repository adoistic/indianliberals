## Key points

- The principal letter is addressed to Girish Munshi and dated 26 May 1975.
- Narayan says he replied to a telegram from Vijaypal Singh Saxena in consultation with Parmanandji.
- The enclosed reply concerns an arrest in Bulandshahr Jail and a request for financial help.
- Narayan states that Girish Munshi is away from Bombay until approximately 10 June and that the office cannot send money.
- Local expenses are to be met through collections at local-unit level.
- Narayan requests details of the arrests, with a copy to National Headquarters at Madras.
- The first letter also notes that Raju is on holiday in Mahabaleshwar and that Srikumar has sought Mr. Raghavachariar’s views.
