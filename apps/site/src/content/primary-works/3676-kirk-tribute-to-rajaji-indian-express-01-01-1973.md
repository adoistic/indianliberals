## Key points

- The clipping reproduces a condolence message from New Zealand Prime Minister Norman Kirk.
- The message is addressed to C. R. Krishnaswami, identified as Rajagopalachari’s son.
- Kirk describes C. Rajagopalachari as one of India’s greatest politicians, statesmen, and philosophers.
- Rajagopalachari is praised for his commitment to spiritual values and concern for the individual.
- The message portrays Rajagopalachari as a figure whose influence extended beyond India and mattered to modern India.
