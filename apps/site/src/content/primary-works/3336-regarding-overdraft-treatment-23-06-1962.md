## Key points

- The correspondence concerns an overdraft account held by or associated with the Swatantra Party.
- The letter refers to an earlier bank communication numbered O/D 5064 and dated 12 June 1962.
- Raja Bahadur Kamakhya Narain Singh was in Europe when the earlier communication was received.
- Repayments had previously been made by the Swatantra Party's Central Office through the bank.
- The writer notes that the June instalment had not yet been paid.
- A copy was forwarded to M. R. Masani, identified in the annotation as the Swatantra Party's General Secretary, for immediate action.
