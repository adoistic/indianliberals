## Key points

- The letter is addressed to S. V. Raju and appears to be signed by P. Ramachandran.
- It requests Raju’s participation in a Swatantra Party State Council meeting.
- It announces the Madurai District Swatantra Party’s fourth Political Conference.
- The events are scheduled for 8 and 9 January 1977 in Madurai.
- The accompanying pages are printed Tamil notices giving meeting details, speakers, office-bearers, venues, and timings.
