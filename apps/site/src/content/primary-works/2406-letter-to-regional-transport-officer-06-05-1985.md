## Key points

- The letter is dated 6 May 1985 and is addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, Regional Transport Office, Bombay.
- Masani denies owning or possessing vehicle MRG-2347.
- He says the vehicle was a jeep formerly belonging to the Swatantra Party and sold by the party in 1967.
- He states that the transport authorities had been given due notice of the sale.
- He returns the demand notice and asks that he not be harassed over the matter.
- He lists nine vehicles said to have been sold by the Swatantra Party as far back as 1967.
- Handwritten annotations appear beside several vehicle numbers, but their full meaning and provenance are not established from the rendered pages.
