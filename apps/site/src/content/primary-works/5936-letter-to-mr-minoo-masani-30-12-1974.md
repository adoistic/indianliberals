## Key points

- Sugata Dasgupta writes in his capacity as Director of the Gandhian Institute of Studies.
- The letter is dated 20 December 1974 and is sent from Rajghat, Varanasi.
- Dasgupta acknowledges receipt of Masani’s letter dated 17 December.
- He asks Masani to telephone as soon as he arrives.
