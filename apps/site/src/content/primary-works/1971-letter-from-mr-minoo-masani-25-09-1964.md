## Key points

- The letter is dated 25 September 1964 and was written in New Delhi. M. R. Masani signs as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party. The intended recipients are the secretaries of the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group. The letter anticipates applications from Members of Parliament seeking to join the party. It also addresses possible returns by former party members or people who had applied for membership elsewhere. Applications are to be sent to the Central Office for consideration by the Party's Parliamentary Board. The Parliamentary Group's Executive Committee is to provide recommendations.
- No action should be taken before the Board grants approval.
