  This single-page press statement reports remarks by M. R. Masani, General Secretary of the Swatantra Party, at a public meeting in Kalbadevi, Bombay. He presents profits as the result of several contributors—investment, labour, technical skill, and management—and warns that taxing profits in this manner would eliminate joint-stock enterprise from fields where large capital investment is unnecessary.

## Key points

- Masani criticises the proposed super-profits tax as based on a failure to distinguish between interest and profit.
- He argues that profits arise from investment, labour, technical skill, and management rather than capital alone.
- He warns that the tax would discourage or eliminate joint-stock enterprise in activities requiring limited capital.
- He describes higher excise and customs duties as a burden on the poor and a threat to economic stability and development.
- He characterises the Budget as inflationary and argues that verbal assurances cannot override economic effects.
- He opposes price controls under the Defence of India Rules, comparing them to treating symptoms rather than disease.
- He cites Soviet black markets and doubts the ability of Indian price controls to succeed.
