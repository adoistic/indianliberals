## Key points

- The Bombay Regional Council met at the Party office on 2 November 1970, chaired by Lalchand Hirachand.
- The Council reviewed correspondence concerning the Leslie Sawhny training programme for democracy and the Workers' Training Programme.
- Members discussed preparations for worker participation in the National Convention and planned a workers' meeting after 8 November.
- Reported membership totals were 227 for Bombay South, 267 for Bombay Central South, 200 for Bombay Central, 409 for Bombay North West, and 327 for Bombay North East.
- The minutes identify organisational weakness in several districts, including inactive or defunct units and limited membership growth.
- A physical-training scheme was approved for pilot implementation at Hiren Shah's unit, with the aim of developing disciplined volunteers for Party work.
- The Council discussed a regional celebration of Rajaji's birthday, vacancies on the Executive Committee and Regional Council, an alleged assault on a Party worker, and cooperation with a Delhi demonstration against rationing.
