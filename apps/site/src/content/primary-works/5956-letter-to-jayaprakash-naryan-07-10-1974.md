## Key points

- Masani writes to Jayaprakash Narayan shortly after returning from Europe.
- He says existing commitments in Bombay and Deolali prevent him from leaving before the end of October.
- He proposes arriving in Patna at 13:15 hours on Saturday, November 2, by IC 409.
- He plans to leave for Calcutta on Sunday, November 3, by IC 206 at 17:55 hours.
- He asks Narayan to arrange a room and a private bath at the Republic Hotel for Saturday night, unless a better option is available.
- The letter closes with concern for Narayan's health despite his strain.
