## Key points

- Minoo Masani praises the recent work of the Swatantra Party’s Matunga Unit.
- The message argues that a strong grassroots organisation is necessary for effective leadership at the top.
- The Matunga Unit is commended for its determination to become self-supporting.
- A programme and souvenir are identified as means of collecting party funds.
- Masani hopes that party units at primary, district, and state levels will emulate Matunga’s example.
- The document is an undated internal party communication with no named recipient.
