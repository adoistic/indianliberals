## Key points

- Lobo Prabhu writes to Madhu N. Mehta in response to a March 31 letter.
- He objects to further publicity for the party's internal affairs.
- He recommends resolving the dispute quietly.
- He asks the National Executive to persuade Mr. Masani to withdraw his resignation.
- He frames Masani's withdrawal as being in the greater interests of the Swatantra Party.
- A copy is sent to N. Dandekar, identified as the party's General Secretary.
