## Key points

- Rajagopalachari writes to Minoo Masani from his Madras address.
- He apologizes for failing to bring a correct copy of resolutions prepared in the G.C.
- He asks that Roy send an accurate copy for publication in Swarajya.
- He hopes the resolutions can appear in the following week's issue at the latest.
- He reports arriving in good health and enjoying the climate after returning from Bombay.
- He thanks Masani for hospitality, including accommodation, beach visits, food, and care.
