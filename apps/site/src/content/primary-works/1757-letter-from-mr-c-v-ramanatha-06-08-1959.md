## Key points

- The letter is dated August 6, 1959, and was written from Pallavaram near Madras Airport.
- Aiyar discusses a special report in the Madras edition of the Hindu about the newly formed Swatantra Party.
- He encloses the recipient’s letter of May 30, written in response to a letter of May 28.
- He praises the recipient for taking an active interest in public affairs after a short holiday.
- He mentions C. R. and V. P. Menon as people associated with the emerging political situation.
- He predicts that the Swatantra Party will succeed in its objectives, despite having been formed prematurely.
