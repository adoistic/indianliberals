## Summary

This one-page letter, dated 3 April 1968, is from M. Dhanukkodi to N. Dandekar, M.P., General Secretary of the Swatantra Party. Dhanukkodi requests an emergency meeting of the party’s General Council to discuss Mr. Masani’s resignation from the Central Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 3 April 1968 and addressed to N. Dandekar, M.P.
- Dhanukkodi asks the Swatantra Party’s General Secretary to convene an emergency General Council meeting.
- The proposed meeting concerns Mr. Masani’s resignation from the Central Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The letter asks the party to decide how to close its ranks and resume united work.
- The appeal invokes the leadership of the party’s respected leaders.
