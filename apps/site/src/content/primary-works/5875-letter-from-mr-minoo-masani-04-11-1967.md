## Key points

- The letter is dated November 4, 1967.
- M. R. Masani writes to the Secretary of the Swatantra Party Tiruppur Constituency.
- Masani acknowledges plans for a souvenir commemorating C. Rajagopalachari's 90th birthday.
- The letter conveys birthday wishes and praise for Rajagopalachari's service to the country.
- Masani expresses the hope that Rajagopalachari will continue providing national guidance and inspiration.
