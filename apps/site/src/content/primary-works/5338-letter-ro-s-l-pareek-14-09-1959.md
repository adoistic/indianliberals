## Key points

- The document is a typed office letter dated 14 September 1959.
- It is addressed to Mr. S. L. Pareek at “Manasavan,” 32 Rani Bazar, Delhi 7.
- The letter responds to Pareek’s letter of 5 September.
- M. R. Masani is said to appreciate Pareek’s suggestion.
- Masani had left for the United Kingdom when Pareek’s letter arrived.
- Miss B. S. Kirsa signs as secretary to M. R. Masani.
- The substance of Pareek’s suggestion is not stated.
