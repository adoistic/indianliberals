## Summary

This one-page personal letter, dated 24 July 1968, is written by M. R. Masani to “Maharaja Saheb,” identified in the address as Mr. R. N. Singh Deo, Chief Minister’s Residence, Bhubaneswar. Masani asks him to send a copy of the Lokanathan Committee Report, which has apparently been in his possession for some time.

Masani also reports progress toward arranging an invitation for Singh Deo to visit the United States at the end of the year. He says the invitation will come from an American university and will be supported by an official invitation. The page contains no further discussion of the report, the proposed visit, or the relationship between the correspondents.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 24 July 1968 and marked “Personal.”
- M. R. Masani writes to Mr. R. N. Singh Deo, addressed as “Maharaja Saheb.”
- Masani requests a copy of the Lokanathan Committee Report for his perusal.
- He reports progress on an invitation for Singh Deo to visit the United States later in 1968.
- The prospective invitation is expected to come from an American university and to be officially supported.
- The letter is signed “M. R. MASANI” and includes the typed initials “mrm;rka” at the foot.
