## Key points

- The letter is dated April 11, 1992 and is addressed to S. C. Rastogi of the Lok Sabha Secretariat.
- The Statesman acknowledges notices of questions of privilege brought by Shri Hannan Mollah and eight other members.
- The complaint concerns the newspaper's Editor, Printer and Publisher, and Minoo Masani, author of the column “As I See It.”
- The disputed column appeared in the March 15, 1992 issue of the newspaper.
- The complainants alleged that the column cast reflections on members of left parties and their allies in the National Front.
- The newspaper states that it will seek Masani's response before sending a substantive reply.
- The letter requests time to have the matter examined fully.
