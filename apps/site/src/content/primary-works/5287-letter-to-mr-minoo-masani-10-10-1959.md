## Key points

- The letter is dated 10 October 1959 and originates from Nidubrolu.
- It is addressed to M. R. Masani at the Swatantra Party office in Bombay.
- The writer says that Dr. D. N. Dwivedi, an advocate, has offered to join the party.
- Dwivedi is described as “a great acquisition” for the party.
- The writer asks Masani to send Hindi-language materials to Dwivedi.
- The letter refers to planned activity in several Indian cities, though some place names and details are difficult to read.
- The sender signs as “Ranga”; the sender’s full identity remains uncertain.
