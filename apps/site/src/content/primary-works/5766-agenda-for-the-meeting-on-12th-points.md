## Key points

- Membership is reported in only 10 of Maharashtra’s 26 districts.
- The agenda calls for expansion into additional districts and broader polling-station coverage.
- An election petition and complaint concerning the Poona district election are under consideration by an Election Tribunal.
- Units are instructed to observe Rajaji Fortnight and organise students and labour.
- Press relations are to be strengthened through contact with reporters from major newspapers and UNI.
- The agenda rejects creating a state-level membership-screening committee.
- Financial business includes bank-account operations, improving the financial position, and a Rs. 60,000 target for units.
- Bombay, Osmanabad, and Parbhani district elections are listed for attention.
