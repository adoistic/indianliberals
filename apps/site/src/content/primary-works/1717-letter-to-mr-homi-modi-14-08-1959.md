## Key points

- The letter is dated 14 August 1959 and was written from the Hyde Park Hotel, Knightsbridge, London.
- The writer addresses the recipient as “My dear Sir Homi.”
- The writer attributes a prior communication to a misunderstanding and says he had not written to the recipient.
- The writer thanks the recipient for offering help and says he has received a good response.
- The writer hopes to return to Bombay in October and meet the recipient briefly before leaving for Dungarpur.
- The letter ends with an expression of belief in the newly founded Swatantra Party.
- The writer’s signature appears to begin “Lakshman,” but the surname is only partially legible.
