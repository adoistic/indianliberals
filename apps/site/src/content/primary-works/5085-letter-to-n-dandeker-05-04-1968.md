## Key points

- The letter is addressed to N. Dandeker, M.P., General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- It requests an emergency meeting of the party's General Council.
- The proposed discussion concerns Minoo Masani's resignation from the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The sender asks the party to take an appropriate decision in response to the situation.
- The stated objective is to help the party close ranks and work unitedly again.
- The sender's handwritten signature is not securely legible.
