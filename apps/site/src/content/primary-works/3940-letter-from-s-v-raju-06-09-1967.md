## Summary

This four-page Swatantra Party Central Office bundle contains internal administrative communications dated September 5–6, 1967. Two supplements, signed by Executive Secretary S. V. Raju, arrange hotel accommodation, return travel reservations, and informal lunches for members attending the Central Parliamentary Board and National Executive meetings in Madras on September 29–30. Both letters direct recipients to contact T. Sadasivam in Madras by September 12 and to provide their travel requirements.

## Key points

- The bundle consists of Swatantra Party Central Office circulars and an attached tabular proposal.
- S. V. Raju's letters concern arrangements for meetings in Madras on September 29–30, 1967.
- Recipients are asked to report hotel and return-journey requirements by September 12.
- The correspondence identifies T. Sadasivam as the local contact for accommodation and travel arrangements.
- N. Dandekar's National Executive Circular No. 22(C) addresses representation on the Mysore and Andhra Pradesh State Councils.
- For Andhra Pradesh, the circular recommends replacing a maximum of 10 representatives per district with the ratio used by the Orissa Unit.
- The attached table compares membership, proposed allocations, and resulting State Council strength for Mysore and Andhra Pradesh.
