## Key points

- The letter is dated 27 September 1975 and is written from Ahmedabad by advocate C. T. Daru.
- Daru says the conference will end at 5:30 p.m.
- He considers Masani's presence necessary until the conference concludes because of pending resolutions.
- Daru proposes a 6:30 p.m. public meeting in Ahmedabad to explain what happened at the conference.
- He suggests travel to Bombay by the Gujarat Mail after 1 October or by a Monday morning flight.
- He asks Masani to state his travel preference so that reservations can be arranged.
