## Key points

- The letter is dated 30 April 1963 and addressed to Minoo Masani.
- It discusses the Gujarat Swatantra Party and Masani’s role as its Gujarat president.
- The sender addresses internal organisational difficulties and differences connected with the party’s work.
- The letter considers the selection of candidates and the need for stronger party organisation.
- The sender asks Masani not to abandon political activity.
- The sender frames continued political work as important to the cause of Gujarat’s and India’s political freedom.
