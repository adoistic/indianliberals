## Key points

- The Swatantra Party’s Gujarat unit was allowed to participate in the Kutch agitation.
- The decision was described as a departure from the party’s normal policy of staying out of agitations.
- The newspaper report links the decision to unresolved questions concerning Kutch.
- The clipping also reports internal and electoral disputes involving Swatantra Party figures in Gujarat.
- A separate typed resolution criticizes growing Central interference in the working of State Governments.
- The resolution objects specifically to the use of Governors to install minority governments in the States.
- The resolution was moved by Mr. P.K. Deo and unanimously adopted by the Swatantra Party General Council.
