## Summary

This one-page English circular, issued on Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group letterhead in New Delhi on November 10, 1965, announces a meeting of the party's Executive Committee scheduled for Thursday, November 11, 1965, at 10:15 a.m. in the Opposition Members' Room, Central Hall, Parliament House. Members are requested to attend.

## Key points

- The document is an internal Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group circular.
- It announces an Executive Committee meeting for November 11, 1965.
- The meeting is scheduled for 10:15 a.m. in the Opposition Members' Room, Central Hall, Parliament House.
- The first agenda item is private members' business concerning Prof. Hiren Mukherjee's resolution, identified as P.L. 480.
- The second agenda item is a foreign policy debate.
- The circular is signed by Kapur Singh, identified as Secretary.
