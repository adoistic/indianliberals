## Key points

- Swatantra Party leaders met Gujarat Chief Minister Hitendra Desai in Bombay.
- The talks were scheduled to continue in Ahmedabad the next day.
- A source reported a possible understanding between the Old Congress and the Swatantra Party.
- The proposed arrangement was said to cover most Gujarat constituencies.
- The talks were described as being at a “fairly delicate stage.”
- Those identified as present included M. R. Masani, R. K. Cooper, and H. M. Patel.
