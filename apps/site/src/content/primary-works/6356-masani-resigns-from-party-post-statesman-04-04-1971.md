## Key points

- M. R. Masani resigned as chairman of the Swatantra Party.
- Masani reportedly intended to remain a party member.
- He affirmed that he would continue advocating the Swatantra Party's philosophy and principles.
- C. Rajagopalachari was trying to persuade Masani to withdraw his resignation.
- The clipping draws on reports from PTI and UNI.
