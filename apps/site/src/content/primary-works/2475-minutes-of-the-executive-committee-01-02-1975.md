## Key points

- The Maharashtra Executive Committee met on 1 February 1975 at 3:30 p.m.
- Parmanand Kejriwal chaired the meeting; six other members were listed as present.
- Leave of absence was granted to Shafii Pirzada.
- The committee reviewed unpaid office rent owed by the BLD from September 1974 onward.
- The chairman was asked to write to Piloo Mody and request that Mr. Bhatia clear the rent.
- The party's financial position was reviewed, and an appeal letter to supporters was approved.
- Changes to the office-bearer list for the party's three Union Bank of India accounts were approved, with accounts to be operated jointly by any two of five authorised persons.
