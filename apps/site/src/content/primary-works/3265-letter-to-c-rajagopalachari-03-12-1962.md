## Key points

- The letter was sent by M. R. Masani to C. Rajagopalachari on 3rd December 1962.
- It was marked “Express Delivery.”
- Masani approved of Rajagopalachari speaking before leaving Poona.
- He complained that others were repeating what he characterised as a “parrot cry.”
- Masani enclosed a copy of his 10th October letter to the Acting President.
- He expected Rajagopalachari to redraft a letter concerning Ramgarh.
