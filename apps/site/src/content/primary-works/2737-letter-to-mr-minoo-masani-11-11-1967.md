  This one-page letter, dated 11 November 1967, is written by K. C. Zacharias from Badagara to Minoo Masani. On Kerala State Swatantra Party letterhead, Zacharias responds to Masani’s letter of 1 November and expresses disappointment that Masani is stepping down as the party’s General Secretary. Zacharias accepts Masani’s explanation that the decision is in the party’s interest and does not argue against it. He nevertheless asks Masani to remain on the National Executive and continue providing guidance to the party. The letter is a brief expression of personal regret, institutional loyalty, and a request for continued leadership.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 11 November 1967 and was sent from Badagara. K. C. Zacharias writes in response to Minoo Masani’s letter of 1 November. Zacharias is unhappy that Masani is stepping down as General Secretary of the party. He accepts Masani’s statement that the decision is in the party’s interest. He asks Masani to remain on the National Executive.
- He seeks Masani’s continued guidance for the party.
