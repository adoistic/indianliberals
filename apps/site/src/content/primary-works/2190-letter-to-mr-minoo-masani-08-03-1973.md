  This untitled letter, dated 8 March 1973 and written from the Swatantra Party (Orissa Unit) in Bhubaneswar, is addressed by G. R. Majhi to Minoo Masani. Majhi recalls that Masani helped secure his election in 1961 and encouraged him to oppose what he calls the Indian National Congress's “injustice and dictatorial trend.” He presents the dissolution of the Orissa Legislative Assembly as a critical turning point, particularly for himself. The immediate issue is a proposal to create an “Orissa Pragati Party” by merging Biju Patnaik's Utkal Congress, Dr. Mahatab's Swadhin Congress, and the Orissa unit of the Swatantra Party. Majhi asks Masani for urgent advice because the state executive is due to meet on 16 March 1973 to decide whether to accept the merger or, if the central party leadership refuses it, sever the state unit's relations with the national centre. The page records a specific episode of party realignment and centre-state organisational tension; no further correspondence or outcome is included.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 8 March 1973 and originates from the Swatantra Party (Orissa Unit) in Bhubaneswar. G. R. Majhi writes to Minoo Masani seeking advice during a politically critical period following the dissolution of the Orissa Assembly. Majhi credits Masani with helping him win election in 1961 and with encouraging resistance to the Congress. A proposed Orissa Pragati Party would merge the Utkal Congress, Swadhin Congress, and the Swatantra Party's Orissa unit. Majhi is uncertain how to respond if the central Swatantra Party leadership rejects the merger proposal. The Orissa state unit is considering severing its relations with the national party centre.
- A state executive meeting scheduled for 16 March 1973 is expected to determine the course of action.
