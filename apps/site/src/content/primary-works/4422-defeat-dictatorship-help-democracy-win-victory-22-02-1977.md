## Key points

- The appeal characterizes the three years following 1975 as a period of governmental repression and national humiliation.
- It argues that the ruling power curtailed citizens’ rights, weakened the Constitution, and rendered the courts ineffective.
- Indira Gandhi is directly identified as the source of the dictatorship described in the letter.
- The text lists forced sterilization, evictions of poor residents, restrictions on workers’ strikes, press censorship, inflation, political imprisonment, and police violence.
- Readers are urged to defeat Congress in the election and support the Janata Party and its allied candidates.
- The appeal presents the election as a choice between dictatorship and popular democracy.
