## Key points

- The letter is dated 15 May 1961 and sent from Madras.
- It is addressed to Kamakhya Narayan Singh, Raja of Ramgarh.
- The sender reports meeting C. Rajagopalachari (“Rajaji”) and “Ranga.”
- An unspecified immediate action has been approved.
- The recipient is asked to arrange a meeting in Bombay to implement prior discussions.
- The sender emphasizes authenticating the identity of the person involved.
- The sender’s handwritten signature cannot be identified from the page.
