  This one-page English letter, dated 17 September 1959, is addressed to journalist Mr. N.S. Sirdeshmukh of Hyderabad Deccan. Miss B.S. Hiral, identified as secretary to M.R. Masani, acknowledges receipt of Sirdeshmukh’s postcard dated 31 July and explains that Masani has forwarded it to her.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 17 September 1959.
- It is addressed to journalist N.S. Sirdeshmukh in Hyderabad Deccan.
- M.R. Masani has passed Sirdeshmukh’s postcard of 31 July to Miss B.S. Hiral.
- Masani is outside India and is expected to return around 6 October.
- Masani is expected to be in Delhi in November for the next session of Parliament.
- The document is routine correspondence rather than an argumentative or political text.
