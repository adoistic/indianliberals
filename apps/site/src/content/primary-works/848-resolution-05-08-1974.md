## Summary

This two-page handwritten resolution, dated “5/8/74,” addresses voting procedure at a convention. It argues that a secret ballot should be used to ensure a fair and free vote on both resolutions before the convention. The document records the adopted wording and bears the signatures of T. B. Ramamurthi, M. Mody, and S. V. Raju.

## Key points

- The document is explicitly headed “Resolution.”
- It seeks to ensure a fair and free vote at the convention.
- It calls for voting by secret ballot.
- The secret ballot is to apply to both resolutions tabled at the convention.
- The resolution is dated “5/8/74,” although the page does not establish the date format.
- Signatures visible on the document include T. B. Ramamurthi, M. Mody, and S. V. Raju.
