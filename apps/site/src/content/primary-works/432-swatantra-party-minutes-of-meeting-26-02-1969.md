## Key points

- The Executive Committee meeting was held on 25 February 1969 at 10:15 a.m. in the Central Hall, Parliament House, New Delhi.
- Seven members are listed as present, including N. G. Ranga, Dahayabhai V. Patel, N. K. Somani, Y. G. Gowd, C. C. Desai, R. K. Amin, and D. N. Patodia.
- Members were assigned speaking responsibilities for the General Budget, Finance Bill, and Railway Budget.
- The committee decided that members speaking in major budgetary or financial debates should not lead debates on demands for grants.
- Four members were nominated to the Joint Select Committee on M. R. Pai's Bill.
- The minutes assign speakers to private members' resolutions concerning the functioning of Central Services and the amortisation of States' debts.
- The committee discussed its response to a convention called by Jayaprakash Narayan and read a letter from Rajaji concerning State lotteries and reservations for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.
