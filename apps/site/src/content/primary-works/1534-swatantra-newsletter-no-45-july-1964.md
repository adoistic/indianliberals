## Summary

The July 1964 issue of Swatantra Newsletter reports on the Swatantra Party’s June conclave in Bombay and its preparations for internal elections, candidate selection, parliamentary coordination, and organisational restructuring. It records decisions concerning Kerala, Delhi, party constitutional amendments, the death of Raghavendra Pratap Singh, and resolutions on the 17th Constitutional Amendment, food scarcity and high prices, South-East Asia, and relations with Pakistan. The issue also presents state-level activity in Andhra Pradesh, Bombay, Gujarat, and Kerala, including campaigns concerning land ceilings, crop insurance, electricity charges, public-sector labour relations, corruption, and the distribution of land to the landless.
