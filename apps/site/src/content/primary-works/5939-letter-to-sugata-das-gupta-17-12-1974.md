## Key points

- The letter is dated 17 December 1974 and signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani plans to arrive in Varanasi by flight IC-411 at 0910 hours on 25 December.
- He intends to return to Delhi by flight IC-402 at 1335 hours on 26 December.
- The visit is intended to include a meeting with J. P., identified in the letter as a person Masani wishes to consult on various matters.
- Masani asks Sugata Das Gupta to reserve time for him during the afternoon or evening of 25 December or the morning of 26 December.
- He looks forward to seeing Sugata Das Gupta and Amritanand Das in Varanasi.
- Masani states that he will stay overnight at the Clarks Hotel and will visit the Gandhi Institute on the morning of 25 December.
