## Summary

This one-page English office memorandum, issued on the letterhead of The Central Bank of India, Ltd. in Bombay and dated 8 February 1962, records an apparent payment approval. The handwritten note names a recipient described as “Bahadur of Rangpur” and specifies a payment of Rs. 20,000, written out as “Twenty Thousand.” The document concludes with an “Accountant.” authorization line.

## Key points

- The document is headed “MEMORANDUM.”
- It is on The Central Bank of India, Ltd. letterhead.
- The place and date are Bombay, 8 February 1962.
- The handwritten note records a payment of Rs. 20,000.
- The amount is also written out as “Twenty Thousand.”
- The named recipient is described as “Bahadur of Rangpur.”
- The memorandum bears an Accountant authorization line.
