## Key points

- Pasricha writes to a recipient addressed as “Mr. Raju” about a previously discussed manuscript.
- He asks Raju to help explain or address matters raised at their last meeting.
- He relies on Raju and Minoo Masani to fill gaps in the manuscript.
- Pasricha has begun revising the manuscript with corrections and additional elaboration.
- He considers the existing language too compressed for its intended purpose.
- He suggests that the draft currently reads more like a thesis than a historical survey.
- The letter closes with regards to Masani and an inquiry about when the recipient will visit Delhi.
