## Key points

- A.P. Jain writes to M.R. Masani from New Delhi on January 22, 1963.
- The requested adjournment motion was not admitted because Shri Dharam Pal and others had already been released.
- The government had not moved a resolution concerning the Colombo proposals.
- Opposition leaders decided not to move an amendment or resolution on the Colombo proposals.
- Prof. Ranga was expected to open the debate, with P.K. Deo speaking the following day.
- "The Judiciary in Peril" was circulated to members of the Joint Select Committee considering the Constitution (15th Amendment) Bill.
- The letter links parliamentary procedure with constitutional and judicial concerns.
