## Key points

- The letter is dated 16 March 1971 and originates from Nadiad, Gujarat.
- It is addressed to Minoo Masani.
- Barot praises Masani for maintaining his constituency despite political pressure.
- Masani’s conduct is presented as an example of moral courage.
- Atal Bihari Vajpayee and Krishna Menon are mentioned as politicians who changed their constituencies.
- Pages 2 and 3 contain a handwritten Gujarati postcard or accompanying note that could not be reliably transcribed here.
