## Key points

- The document is an employment and income-tax certificate issued by the Swatantra Party National Headquarters.
- It is dated April 5, 1971, and addressed to no named recipient.
- S. V. Raju is identified as Executive Secretary of the party’s national headquarters.
- The certificate records an annual salary of Rs 18,000.
- Annual income tax of Rs 2,028 is stated to have been deducted at source.
- The deducted tax was paid into the Reserve Bank of India.
- R. C. Cooper signs the document as General Secretary.
