## Key points

- The letter is dated 24 June 1964.
- Minoo Masani addresses C. Rajagopalachari as “Rajaji.”
- Masani responds to Rajagopalachari’s letter of 11 June concerning R. V. S. Mani’s proposal.
- Masani says he consulted K. M. Munshi about the matter.
- Munshi is reported to have found Mani’s idea pointless.
- Masani explicitly endorses Munshi’s judgment.
