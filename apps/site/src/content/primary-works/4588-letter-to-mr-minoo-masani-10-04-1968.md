It urges Rajaji to use his influence to withdraw the resignations, convene an emergency General Council meeting, restore party discipline, and prevent the Swatantra Party from being weakened by internal conflict. The letters offer a personal view of party-management concerns surrounding the Swatantra Party in Gujarat in 1968, but the file provides no formal title or author byline beyond the sender’s letterhead and signature.

## Key points

- The file consists of two English-language letters from Gondal dated April 5 and April 10, 1968.
- Kusum Kumari C. Joshi writes to Minoo Masani about his political situation, an upcoming meeting, and arrangements for visits by party colleagues.
- The handwritten letter mentions H. M. Patel, S. V. Raju, N. Dandekar, Madhav Hatte, Morarji Desai, and others in connection with party consultations.
- The typed letter to C. Rajagopalachari responds to resignations by Minoo Masani, Maharaja Madho Singhji, and Pujya Bhaikaka.
- The writer asks Rajaji to help secure withdrawal of the resignations and avert a crisis within the Swatantra Party.
- The typed letter argues that the party should operate as a disciplined organisation rather than as an undisciplined crowd pursuing individual opportunities.
- The correspondence reflects concerns about party leadership, internal discipline, emergency meetings, and the consequences of Gujarat political events.
