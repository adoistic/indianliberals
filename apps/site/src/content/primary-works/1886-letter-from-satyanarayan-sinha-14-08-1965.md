## Key points

- The letter is dated August 14, 1965, and issued from New Delhi on Minister of Parliamentary Affairs letterhead.
- Satyanarayan Sinha writes to M. R. Masani, M.P.
- The Prime Minister wished to meet leaders of opposition groups in Parliament.
- The stated subject of the proposed discussion was the Kashmir situation.
- The meeting was scheduled for August 15, 1965, at 7 p.m. in Room No. 9, Parliament House.
- The recipient was asked to attend if the arrangement was convenient.
