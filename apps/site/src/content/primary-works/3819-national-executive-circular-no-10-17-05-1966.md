## Key points

- The document is National Executive Circular No. 10, issued on 17 May 1966.
- It is addressed to Swatantra Party members as an internal party communication.
- Meetings of the Central Parliamentary Board and National Executive are scheduled for 26 May.
- The circular warns that these meetings may not finish their business as planned.
- Members are asked to remain in Bombay on Sunday, 29 May, in case a further meeting is required.
- M. R. Masani signs the circular as General Secretary.
