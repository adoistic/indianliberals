## Key points

- Rajagopalachari writes to a recipient addressed only as “Modi” and refers to the recipient’s recent letter.
- He says a planned appeal for active assistance is unnecessary because of the support already expressed.
- He describes himself as greatly heartened by the recipient’s continued support.
- He argues that tyranny cannot be ended while its victims remain afraid.
- He warns that political friends are becoming increasingly autocratic and are restricting freedom of speech.
- He calls for courage from the recipient and Mr. Shroff in responding to this situation.
- The letter also notes that Rajagopalachari has read a report of observations by an incompletely legible “Sri P...”
