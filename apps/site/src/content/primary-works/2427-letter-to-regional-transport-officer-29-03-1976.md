## Summary

This untitled letter, dated March 29, 1976, was issued by Bharatiya Lok Dal, Bombay, to the Regional Transport Officer in Bombay. V. P. Bhatia confirms a conversation held on March 23 concerning vehicles originally purchased by the former Swatantra Party, which had merged into Bharatiya Lok Dal. The letter supplies a detailed list of 25 vehicles, identifying their registered owners, destinations, and registration numbers.

## Key points

- The letter confirms a prior conversation with the Regional Transport Officer on March 23, 1976.
- It concerns vehicles purchased by the former Swatantra Party and subsequently associated with Bharatiya Lok Dal.
- Twenty-five vehicles are listed under the names of G. B. Sukhee, V. K. Singhe, V. P. Bhatia, S. V. Raju, Padma Vaswani, and M. R. Masani.
- The listed destinations include Gujarat, Mysore, Orissa, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Godhra.
- The letter states that all vehicles were registered in January 1971 and dispatched directly from the Mahindra & Mahindra works.
- It asks the transport authority to correct its records and treat notices issued against the vehicles as cancelled.
- The document is signed by V. P. Bhatia in the capacity of Executive Secretary.
