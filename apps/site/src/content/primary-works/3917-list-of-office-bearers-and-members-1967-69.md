## Key points

- The document is a one-page Swatantra Party list of its newly elected National Executive for 1967–69.
- Prof. N. G. Ranga is identified as president.
- The vice-presidents listed are Dr. K. M. Munshi, Prof. M. R. Masani, Smt. Gayatri Devi, R. N. Singh Deo, and Bhailalbhai Patel.
- N. Dandekar is listed as general secretary.
- The page names four joint secretaries and two treasurers.
- The members' list includes C. Rajagopalachari, H. M. Patel, Minoo Masani, and numerous other party figures.
- The document supplies organisational information only and does not explain the executive's programme or activities.
