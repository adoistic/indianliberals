## Key points

- The letter is dated 16 November 1967 and was written from Pattambi, Kerala.
- The sender thanks M. R. Masani for his letter and advice.
- The sender supports Masani's suggestions and advocates including people with diverse merits in the organisation.
- The sender says they will cooperate with Mr. N. Dandekar.
- A brother's serious illness, involving a heart attack and paralysis, may prevent the sender from attending organisational meetings.
- The sender refers to the National Executive Meeting and General Council meeting scheduled for 25 November.
