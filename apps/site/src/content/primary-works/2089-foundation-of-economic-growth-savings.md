## Key points

- Economic growth is linked to cultivating and incentivising household savings.
- Interest rates on bank deposits and government securities should be increased considerably.
- Monetary correction would adjust repayments to preserve the currency’s purchasing power.
- An experiment in Brazil is presented as having reduced inflation through monetary correction.
- Deficit financing and the resulting increase in money supply should receive parliamentary sanction.
- Government expenditure should be reduced at the Centre, in the states, and across ministries.
- The Ministry of Agriculture is used as an example of government expenditure regarded as excessive.
