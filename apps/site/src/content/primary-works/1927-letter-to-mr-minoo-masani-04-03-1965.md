## Summary

This one-page English letter, dated March 1965, is from Prof. N. G. Ranga to Minoo Masani. Ranga reports that parliamentary leaders had met the Minister of Parliamentary Affairs to allocate time for discussion of various Demands for Grants. He advises Masani that discussion of the General Budget would not begin before 15 March and asks him to adjust his programme accordingly.

The letter also contains handwritten annotations referring to an enclosed report of a delegation and to amendments discussed and agreed upon. The annotations appear to propose changes to the composition or representation of a group, but parts of this handwriting are difficult to read from the rendered page.

## Key points

- Prof. N. G. Ranga writes to Minoo Masani from his parliamentary address in New Delhi.
- The letter reports a meeting between parliamentary leaders and the Minister of Parliamentary Affairs.
- The meeting concerned allocation of parliamentary time for various Demands for Grants.
- Ranga states that discussion of the General Budget would not begin before 15 March 1965.
- He asks Masani to revise his programme in light of the parliamentary schedule.
- Handwritten annotations refer to an enclosed delegation report and agreed amendments.
- The handwriting appears to discuss representation from different groups, though the exact wording is partly illegible.
