## Summary

This typed office letter, dated 20 July 1964 and signed by K. S. Srinivasan as Office Secretary, acknowledges receipt of material from Jal S. Ratnakar. Srinivasan thanks Ratnakar for sending a newspaper or magazine cutting of C. Rajagopalachari's speech published in “The Gateway,” identified as the Rotary Club bulletin.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 20 July 1964.
- It is addressed to Mr. Jal S. Ratnakar at Surya Mahal, Military Square, Fort, Bombay-1.
- K. S. Srinivasan signs the letter as Office Secretary.
- The sender acknowledges receipt of a cutting of Rajaji's speech.
- The speech appeared in “The Gateway,” described as the Rotary Club bulletin.
- The letter is a brief expression of thanks and contains no discussion of the speech's substance.
