## Key points

- The document is General Council Circular No. 20 of the Swatantra Party.
- It was issued from the Party's Central Office in Bombay on April 11, 1966.
- A meeting of the Party's General Council was scheduled for Monday and Tuesday, May 16 and 17, 1966.
- The venue, time, agenda, and other logistical details were to be communicated later.
- The circular is signed by M. R. Masani in his capacity as General Secretary.
