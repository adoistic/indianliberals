## Key points

- The letter is dated 16 November 1959 and addressed to Mr. Ram Rai Mumbhi.
- It refers to Mumbhi’s letters of 21 October and 7 November.
- The writer says that copies of a pamphlet on State Trading were requested from Mr. Shroff’s office.
- Mumbhi’s proposal for a regular leaflet service was passed to the Party’s publicity department.
- The Party is described as not yet adequately organised for publicity work.
- The writer expects the publicity service to begin before long.
