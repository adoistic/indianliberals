## Key points

- The letter is dated 1 April 1968 and sent from 2 Tughlaq Road, New Delhi.
- Masani is replying to Shenoy's letter of 21 March.
- Masani expresses gratitude that Shenoy stepped aside.
- He criticises party contacts for misleading Shenoy.
- He refers to an attempt to flout the party's decision and pledges.
