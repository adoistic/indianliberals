## Key points

- The telegram is addressed to Minoo Masani at 148 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
- It states that the sender has received no reply to a previous telegram.
- It reports that Mr Hanumanta Rao will reach Patna on the morning of 17 June.
- Hanumanta Rao is expected to stay for a few days.
- The sender asks Masani to convey, through a messenger if necessary, the result of a letter to their mutual friend Jayaprakash.
- The sender is not identified by a visible byline or signature.
