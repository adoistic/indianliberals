## Summary

This two-page scan contains a typed letter from Girish Munshi to Shri V. S. Srikumar, dated June 27, 1975. Munshi acknowledges Srikumar's letters of June 20 and June 23 and a telegram dated June 25, and explains that he tried unsuccessfully to reach him by telephone at both the office and residence. He asks Srikumar to telephone him at night through the Party office.

## Key points

- The letter is dated June 27, 1975, and is signed by Girish Munshi.
- Munshi acknowledges correspondence from Srikumar dated June 20 and June 23, as well as a telegram dated June 25.
- He reports unsuccessful attempts to contact Srikumar by telephone at his office and residence.
- He asks Srikumar to contact him at his residence at night through the Party office.
- The letter refers to the latest developments and concludes that no action can presently be taken.
- The recipient is identified as a Joint Secretary of the All-India Swatantra Party.
- The second scanned page is a rotated Hindi newspaper clipping that appears unrelated to the letter.
