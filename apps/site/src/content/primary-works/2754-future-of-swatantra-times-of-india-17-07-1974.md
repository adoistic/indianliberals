## Key points

- M. R. Masani submitted a draft resolution proposing a transformation of the Swatantra Party.
- The proposed organisation would abandon electoral and parliamentary activity at both Union and state levels.
- The proposal sought to prevent the party from joining a proposed multi-party opposition led by Piloo Mody.
- The plan was described as a compromise that would preserve the Swatantra Party as a political force.
- The proposed successor organisation was to be called the “Swatantra Seva Sangh.”
- Some party members reportedly favoured dissolving the party to enable a broader opposition.
