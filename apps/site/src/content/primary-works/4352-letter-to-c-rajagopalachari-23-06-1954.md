## Key points

- Masani addresses C. Rajagopalachari at Kalki, Madras.
- The communication responds to Rajagopalachari’s letter of the 20th.
- Masani recommends waiting for Dr. Radhakrishnan’s reply before publishing the memorandum.
- He proposes that a COC meeting could alternatively decide whether to publish.
- The document is marked as a confirmatory copy and uses telegram conventions.
