## Summary

This two-page English administrative letter from the Lok Sabha Secretariat, dated 27 July 1965, responds to a notice submitted by M. R. Masani and N. Dandekar concerning a motion of no-confidence in the Council of Ministers. Deputy Secretary B. B. Tewari states that the Speaker cannot consider the notice so far in advance of the session's commencement.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 27 July 1965 and issued from Parliament House, New Delhi.
- It is signed by B. B. Tewari, Deputy Secretary, Lok Sabha Secretariat.
- The recipients are M. R. Masani, M.P., and N. Dandekar, M.P.
- The subject is a motion of no-confidence in the Council of Ministers.
- The Speaker cannot consider the notice so far in advance of the session.
- The notice may be tabled after the day's List of Business has been circulated.
- The letter cites paragraph 1351 of Lok Sabha Bulletin Part II, dated 21 July 1965.
