## Key points

- Masani writes to A. P. Jain from correspondence dated 14 August 1960.
- He is preparing to oppose Nath Pai's Bill on Friday afternoon.
- He requests the Maharaja of Dhrangadhra's Bill.
- He asks for Piloo Mody's earlier speech opposing the Bill.
- He requests Select Committee evidence, specifically with Nani Palkhivala's evidence marked for reference.
- Masani says he will arrive in Delhi on Monday the 19th by a flight arriving at 9:30 a.m.
