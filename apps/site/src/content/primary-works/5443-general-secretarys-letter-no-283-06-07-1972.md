## Key points

- The letter is General Secretary’s Letter No. 283, issued from the Swatantra Party’s national headquarters in Bombay.
- State units are asked to report membership renewals and fresh enrolment figures as of 31 March 1972.
- Only Delhi, Himachal Pradesh, Kerala, Orissa, and Punjab had responded to the earlier request.
- The figures were needed to help calculate the strength of the party’s various delegations for a National Convention expected later in 1972.
- The second page records 29 units in the Bombay Region.
- The Bombay Region’s listed membership totals 1,025 existing members, with 57 new members in 1972, producing a stated total of 1,082.
- The report breaks down new members by named recruiters or groups, including a J group credited with 26 members in Kotwadi and Gabriel Pillai credited with three.
