## Summary

In this one-page letter dated May 15, 1972, D. N. Patodia writes to M. R. Masani about a letter circulated by N. Dandekar proposing the resignation of all members of the National Executive and the re-election of the entire committee, including its president. Patodia notes that the proposal has generated controversy among members of the National Executive and asks Masani for his views before taking any action.

## Key points

- The letter is dated May 15, 1972, and is signed by D. N. Patodia.
- It is addressed to M. R. Masani, whom Patodia missed during a recent stay in Bombay.
- Patodia refers to a letter circulated by N. Dandekar concerning the National Executive.
- The proposal called for the resignation of all committee members and the re-election of the entire committee, including the president.
- The proposal had caused controversy among some members of the National Executive.
- Patodia seeks Masani's guidance before taking action and asks whether Masani plans to visit Delhi.
