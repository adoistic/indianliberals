## Key points

- Masani writes to Piloo Mody on July 15, 1974.
- He acknowledges receipt of Mody’s letter and draft resolution.
- Masani says he expected Mody to revise Masani’s earlier draft.
- He objects that Mody’s proposal incorporates positions opposed by many participants.
- Masani concludes that further discussion is pointless because the proposed compromise is unacceptable to Mody.
- The letter is copied to the Executive Secretary of the Swatantra Party National Headquarters.
