## Summary

This one-page English telegram from Minoo Masani to Jayaprakash Narayan is dated 21 October 1974 and addressed from Patna 3. Masani asks for Narayan’s travel programme and proposes meeting him in Delhi on 1 November before Narayan continues to Patna.

## Key points

- The document is a typed telegram or confirmation copy dated 21 October 1974.
- Minoo Masani is identified as the sender.
- Jayaprakash Narayan is the named recipient and addressee.
- Masani requests Narayan’s travel programme.
- Masani proposes meeting Narayan in Delhi on 1 November.
- The proposed Delhi meeting is intended to avoid Narayan’s travelling first to Patna.
- Masani asks Narayan to wire whether the meeting is possible and to provide his Delhi address.
