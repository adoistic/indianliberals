## Key points

- A. P. Jain forwards press comments concerning the Rajkot elections and Lohia’s interview in Blitz.
- The letter highlights Lohia’s claim that most Swatantra Party members favoured Hindi.
- It recounts an interruption of Prof. Ranga during a Lok Sabha discussion on continuing the use of English.
- Jain says Shri Yashpal Singh’s intervention was censured by the press.
- The postscript cites Indira Gandhi’s remarks about the Rajkot defeat and asks whether the remarks can be publicised in the Swatantra Party’s favour.
