## Summary

This five-page archival bundle records Swatantra Party Maharashtra correspondence from October 1974. The first letter, signed by General Secretary Girish Munshi, asks S. V. Raju to consent to co-option onto the party’s Executive Committee. Two subsequent English notices, signed by Office Secretary P. Narayan, convene Executive Committee meetings in Bombay and set out an agenda including confirmation of earlier minutes, co-option of members, and consideration of a Bihar Day event with Sarvodaya workers.

## Key points

- Girish Munshi invites S. V. Raju to join the Swatantra Party Maharashtra Executive Committee by co-option.
- The invitation is presented as following a telephone conversation and requests a written reply indicating Raju’s willingness.
- A Bombay-region Executive Committee meeting is convened for 23 October 1974 to prepare for a possible Bihar Day observance.
- A separate circular schedules the Maharashtra Executive Committee meeting for 28 October 1974 at the party office in Bombay.
- The circular’s agenda includes approval of previous minutes, co-option of members, Bihar Day, and other business with the Chairman’s permission.
- The final two pages are handwritten Hindi correspondence concerning political and organisational matters in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh; some handwriting is difficult to decipher.
