## Key points

- The letter follows up on a telephone conversation concerning vehicles bought under individual names by the Swatantra Party.
- It states that the Swatantra Party had merged into the Bharatiya Lok Dal.
- The sender says they personally met Mr. S.B. Tracy and explained the situation concerning the vehicles.
- A copy of a letter to Mr. Tracy is enclosed for the recipient's ready records.
- The enclosed letter is described as providing details about where the vehicles were sent at the time of the 1971 general elections.
- Five notices received by the sender's office are enclosed for the Regional Transport Officer's records.
