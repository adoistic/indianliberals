## Key points

- The letter is dated 22 November 1976 and addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, Bombay.
- It concerns vehicles purchased by the Swatantra Party under various individual names.
- The writer says that the Swatantra Party had merged into the Bharatiya Lok Dal.
- The writer reports personally meeting Mr. S. R. Tracy about the vehicle records.
- The vehicles had been sent to various locations during the 1971 general elections.
- Five notices are enclosed for the transport office's records.
- The author is not identifiable from the visible page; the letter is signed only with a handwritten signature and the designation “(EXECUTIVE SECRETARY)”.
