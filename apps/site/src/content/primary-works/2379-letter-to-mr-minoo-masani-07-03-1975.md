## Summary

This one-page English office letter, dated March 7, 1975, was issued by Tata Services Limited from its Bombay office to Mr. M. R. Masani. It confirms that Jeep No. MRA 4024, registered in Masani’s name, had been purchased by Tata Services Limited for the use of Tata companies. The letter states that possession of the vehicle was taken by a representative of Tata Iron and Steel Co. Ltd. at Bhubaneswar on March 3, 1967, and that the vehicle was sold to Tata Iron and Steel Co. Ltd. with effect from that date. It is signed on behalf of Tata Services Limited by N. R. Khatkhate, identified as Chief Accountant. No printed title or author byline appears on the page.

## Key points

- The document is a dated administrative letter from Tata Services Limited. It is addressed to Mr. M. R. Masani. Tata Services Limited confirms purchasing Jeep No. MRA 4024 for the use of Tata companies. The vehicle was registered in Mr. Masani’s name. A Tata Iron and Steel Co. Ltd. representative took possession at Bhubaneswar on March 3, 1967. The vehicle was sold to Tata Iron and Steel Co. Ltd. effective from March 3, 1967.
- The letter is signed by N. R. Khatkhate, Chief Accountant.
