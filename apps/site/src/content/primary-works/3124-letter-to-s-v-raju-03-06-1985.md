## Key points

- The letter is dated 3 June 1985 and was sent from Harley Estate, Sakleshpur, India.
- N. K. Ganapathiah identifies himself as Secretary General of the Farmers’ Federation of India.
- The recipient is S. V. Raju, editor of Freedom First.
- The sender enclosed ten copies of the Second Rajaji Memorial Lecture.
- The lecture was delivered by M. R. Masani in Bangalore on 22 December 1984.
- The letter requests acknowledgment of receipt.
