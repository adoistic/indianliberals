## Summary

This one-page English office letter, dated 16 December 1974 and addressed to Bhartiya Lok Dal in Bombay, records the rent and electricity charges paid for November 1974. It itemizes rent of Rs. 381.54 and electricity charges of Rs. 136.54, for a total of Rs. 518.08.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 16th December 1974.
- It is addressed to Bhartiya Lok Dal, Bombay.
- It reports rent and electricity expenses for November 1974.
- The listed rent charge is Rs. 381.54.
- The listed electricity charge is Rs. 136.54.
- The total expenditure is Rs. 518.08.
- The recipient's 40% share is calculated as Rs. 207.24.
- P. Narayan signs as Office Secretary.
