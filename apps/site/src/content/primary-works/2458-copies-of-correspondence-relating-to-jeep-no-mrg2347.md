## Key points

- The document concerns Jeep No. MRG 2347.
- A 16 October 1972 entry records a bill-cum-receipt from the Municipal Corporation of Greater Bombay for payment of vehicle tax.
- Correspondence in 1975 concerns the Collector of Bombay, the Regional Transport Office, and a notice relating to a traffic offence.
- A 29 March 1975 item refers to V. P. Bhatia, Secretary of the Bharatiya Lok Dal, providing details of vehicles purchased by the Swatantra Party.
- An entry dated 10 October 1975 records receipt and dispatch of an insurance policy for Jeep MRG 2347.
- Three entries from February 1985 list registered and ordinary letters sent by M.R.M. to the Regional Transport Officer.
