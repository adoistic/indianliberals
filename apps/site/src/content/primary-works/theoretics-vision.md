summary: Theoretics Vision is a collection of theoretical writings by B R Shenoy on economic freedom, economic development, savings, money management, inflation, prices, currency and banking. Most of the theoretical writings in the volume date from 1932 to 1953, covering the period when Shenoy was studying at the London School of Economics and the early years of India's economic development. These writings provide the theoretical framework underlying his later empirical analyses and policy criticism.
