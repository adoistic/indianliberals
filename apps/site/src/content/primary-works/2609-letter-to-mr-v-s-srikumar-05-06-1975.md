## Key points

- P. Narayan acknowledges receipt of Srikumar's June 3 letter.
- K. M. Munshi has returned to Bombay and received Srikumar's April 26 correspondence.
- Munshi also received Mr. Ganesan's counter-affidavit concerning the Election Commission.
- Munshi is expected to write to Srikumar within a day or two.
- Mr. Raju is expected in Bombay by June 8.
- Munshi will stay at Juhu for one more week and can be contacted there for urgent work.
- Letters for Munshi may be sent to Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan as usual.
