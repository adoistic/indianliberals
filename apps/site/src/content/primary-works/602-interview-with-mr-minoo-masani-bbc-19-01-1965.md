## Key points

- The transcript defines the original meaning of non-alignment as abstention from participation in the Cold War blocs.
- China's 1962 attack on India is presented as having made traditional non-alignment irrelevant to India's security situation.
- Military assistance from Western powers is described as compatible with non-alignment, including the assistance accepted by Jawaharlal Nehru in 1962.
- The respondent distinguishes understandable state neutrality from “neutralism,” which is condemned as equidistance between freedom and communism.
- The Swatantra Party is presented as opposing non-alignment and advocating regional and Western mutual-security arrangements.
- India is said to need protection against China's nuclear and diplomatic coercion.
- An independent Indian nuclear deterrent is rejected on psychological, logistical, economic, and developmental grounds.
- The preferred policy is an unconditional United States guarantee against nuclear attack from Communist China.
