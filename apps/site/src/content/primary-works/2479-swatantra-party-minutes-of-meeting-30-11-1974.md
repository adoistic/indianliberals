summary: |-
  This two-page English meeting record documents a meeting of Swatantra Party workers held at the party office in Kalyan on 30 November 1974. It lists twelve attendees and records preparations for the party’s General Council meeting in Madras on 8 December, including efforts to secure delegates from the Maharashtra unit. It also outlines local meetings planned around C. Rajagopalachari’s 96th birth anniversary, including a Matunga meeting to be addressed by Minoo Masani.

## Key points

- The meeting concerned Swatantra Party organisation and mobilisation in Maharashtra.
- A General Council meeting was scheduled in Madras for 8 December 1974.
- Members were urged to attend the General Council meeting, with several individuals persuaded to travel to Madras.
- Local public meetings were planned in Matunga and Mazagaon during the week of Rajaji’s birthday.
- The party treated Bombay’s milk problem as an urgent issue and proposed a symposium to gather current data and statistics.
- The proposed milk symposium was to include Khurody, Minoo Masani, M. R. Pai, and editors of the Economic Times and Financial Express.
- The meeting called for the strengthening of non-functioning local units and the creation of ad hoc committees in Bandra, Chembur, and Santa Cruz.
