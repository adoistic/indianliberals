## Key points

- The document is National Executive Circular No. 16.
- The next National Executive meeting is scheduled for 11 December 1966 at 10 a.m.
- The meeting venue is Vithalbhai Patel House, Rafi Marg, New Delhi.
- The agenda begins with confirmation of the minutes of the 6 November 1966 meeting and related business.
- The National Executive will consider business before the National Convention.
- The agenda includes any other business permitted by the Chair.
- M. R. Masani signs the circular as General Secretary.
