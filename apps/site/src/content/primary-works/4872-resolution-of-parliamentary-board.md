  This one-page Swatantra Party resolution asks the General Council to endorse Prime Minister Tunku Abdul Rahman's appeal for Commonwealth support to Malaysia against what it describes as aggression, following the invasion of India by Chinese Communist troops in October 1962. ## Key points

- The Swatantra Party General Council is asked to support Malaysia's appeal for assistance against aggression.
- The resolution recalls Malaysia's prompt support for India during the Chinese Communist invasion of October 1962.
- It calls on the Indian Government to declare full support for Malaysia's security and freedom.
- It urges India to end non-interference in Laos and support the withdrawal of Communist forces.
- It criticizes India's representative in Laos for not joining other governments in demanding that withdrawal.
- The resolution frames Communist aggression in Malaysia and Laos as a threat requiring coordinated action by democratic countries.
