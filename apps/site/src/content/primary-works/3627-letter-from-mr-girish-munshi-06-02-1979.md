## Key points

- The Swatantra Party, Maharashtra, issued the notice from its office at Sassoon Building, 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay.
- The letter is dated February 6, 1979, and calls an Executive Committee meeting for Friday, February 23, 1979, at 6 p.m.
- The agenda includes reviewing developments since the last meeting.
- The committee was to consider and adopt the statement of accounts for the period ending December 1978.
- The meeting was to address matters relating to the party's premises.
- Other business could be taken up with the chair's permission.
- Handwritten notes add administrative references to earlier periods and the disposal of old files.
