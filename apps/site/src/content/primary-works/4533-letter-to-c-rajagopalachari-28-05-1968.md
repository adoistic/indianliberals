## Key points

- The letter is dated 28 May 1968 and is addressed to C. Rajagopalachari, whom Masani calls “Rajaji.”
- Masani forwards a questionnaire concerning Rajagopalachari’s grandson, Parthasarathy.
- Larsen & Toubro wants Parthasarathy to complete and return the questionnaire to John Bower, the company’s director.
- Masani expresses hope that the questionnaire and prospective opportunity will lead to something beneficial.
- Masani has just returned to Bombay from Europe and anticipates meeting Rajagopalachari over the weekend.
- He plans to leave on the Monday afternoon train after the Sunday evening meeting but may meet Rajagopalachari on Monday morning.
