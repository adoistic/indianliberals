## Key points

- The telegram was sent from Varanasi and addressed to Minoo Masani at 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay.
- It reports a carbuncle on Jayaprakash Narayan’s left hand.
- The injury is described as healing.
- Jayaprakash Narayan’s blood sugar is reported to be under control.
- The message says that his general health is normal.
- The telegram reassures the recipient that there is no cause for anxiety.
