## Key points

- The memorandum concerns internal Swatantra Party discipline and voting.
- It claims that the party’s central leadership will not discipline an MLA for voting against the official candidate.
- The note refers to a dispute involving Mr. Vaidya and Mr. U. N. Mahida.
- It argues that an earlier promise made to Mr. Vaidya should be fulfilled.
- The writer interprets the unfolding events as evidence that central party leaders are retreating from an earlier position.
- A Gujarati newspaper clipping accompanies the handwritten English note.
