## Summary

This one-page telegram from Minoo Masani to Jayaprakash Narayan, dated March 12, 1975, urges Narayan to issue a public statement supporting doctors and students at Vellore Hospital. Masani presents their struggle as directed against corruption, trade-union violence, and alleged police hostility backed by M. Karunanidhi.

The message specifically protests the arrest of a doctor identified as Mathias, described as himself a victim of violence, and concludes that the incident calls for condemnation. The document is a typed telegram on an Indian Posts and Telegraphs form; no fuller context or accompanying correspondence is present on the rendered page.

## Key points

- Minoo Masani sent the telegram to Jayaprakash Narayan on March 12, 1975.
- Masani urged Narayan to issue a statement of solidarity with doctors and students at Vellore Hospital.
- The hospital dispute is described as involving corruption, trade-union violence, and police hostility.
- The message alleges that the police hostility was supported by M. Karunanidhi.
- Masani called attention to the arrest of Dr. Mathias, whom the telegram describes as a victim of violence.
- The telegram concludes by calling for condemnation of the arrest and violence.
