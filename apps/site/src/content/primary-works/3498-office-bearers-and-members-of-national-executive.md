## Summary

This five-page English-language administrative roster records the office bearers and members of the Swatantra Party's National Executive. It identifies the president, four vice-presidents, general secretary, three treasurers, one joint secretary, and a numbered list of national-executive members.

## Key points

- Prof. N. G. Ranga is listed as president, with separate addresses for ordinary periods and Parliament sessions.
- The vice-presidents are Dr. K. M. Munshi, Prof. M. Ruthnaswamy, Smt. Gayatri Devi of Jaipur, and Jathedar Hukam Singh Nagoke.
- M. R. Masani is listed as general secretary.
- Sir Homi Mody, Dr. R. C. Cooper, and Charanjit Rai are listed as treasurers.
- N. Dandekar is listed as joint secretary.
- The membership list includes politicians, former civil servants, advocates, rulers, and other regional representatives from across India.
- The document supplies residential or correspondence addresses, including alternative New Delhi addresses for parliamentary sessions.
