The letter therefore concerns party reorganisation and the disposition of party assets.

## Key points

- The letter is dated July 3, 1974, and addressed to S. V. Raju.
- Munshi says he has received Raju’s letter of July 1.
- Munshi reports consulting Parmanandji about the proposed party transition.
- The issue discussed is whether Swatantra Party assets in Maharashtra would transfer to a new party.
- Munshi states that the new party would naturally receive the assets as the successor party.
- The letter is signed by G. K. Munshi on Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan letterhead in Bombay.
