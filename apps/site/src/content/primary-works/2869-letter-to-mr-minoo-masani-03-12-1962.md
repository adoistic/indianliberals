## Summary

This one-page English office letter, dated 3 December 1962 and issued from the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office in New Delhi, is addressed to M. R. Masani. A. R. Jain encloses Victor Anant’s article, “An India's thoughts from abroad,” published in the Manchester Guardian (Overseas Edition) on 27 November 1962, for Masani’s information.

The letter characterizes Anant’s article as especially timely and says that it argues in favour of India developing closer relations with Pakistan. The document itself does not elaborate on the article’s reasoning; it functions primarily as a brief covering communication transmitting the article to Masani.

## Key points

- The document is a one-page typed letter on Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office letterhead.
- It is dated 3 December 1962 and addressed to M. R. Masani.
- A. R. Jain encloses an article by Victor Anant for Masani’s information.
- The enclosed article appeared in the Manchester Guardian (Overseas Edition) on 27 November 1962.
- The letter says the article supports closer relations between India and Pakistan.
- The covering letter presents the article as particularly interesting and timely.
