## Summary

This two-page English procedural application is addressed to the Prothonotary and Senior Master of the High Court of Judicature at Bombay. It identifies Swatantra Party as the petitioner in Writ Petition No. 14 of 1994, against the Union of India and others, and asks permission to produce the papers and proceedings in the matter before Justices Kamat and Datar.

The application requests that the matter be listed on 14 December 1995, with the typed time of 11:00 A.M. altered by hand to 2:45, and includes a handwritten notation requesting an urgent order. It closes with an illegible signature identifying the signatory only as the advocate for the petitioner. The rendered pages contain no substantive account of the petition's legal grounds or merits.

## Key points

- The document is filed in the High Court of Judicature at Bombay under its Ordinary Original Civil Jurisdiction.
- It concerns Writ Petition No. 14 of 1994.
- Swatantra Party is identified as the petitioner.
- The Union of India and others are identified as respondents.
- The applicant seeks to produce the papers and proceedings in the matter before Justices Kamat and Datar.
- The requested hearing date is 14 December 1995; the typed time of 11:00 A.M. is handwrittenly changed to 2:45.
- A handwritten note asks for an urgent order.
- The advocate's handwritten signature is not legibly resolved, and the pages do not state the petition's substantive legal claims.
