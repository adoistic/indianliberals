## Key points

- The letter is dated April 3, 1968, and is written on Dr. M. Santosham's Lok Sabha parliamentary letterhead.
- Santosham requests an emergency meeting of the Swatantra Party's General Council.
- The proposed agenda is Minoo Masani's resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- The requested meeting is intended to enable the party to work together again.
- The letter does not explain the reasons for Masani's resignation or record any later resolution.
