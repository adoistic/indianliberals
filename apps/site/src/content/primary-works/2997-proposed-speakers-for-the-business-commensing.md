## Summary

This single-page internal planning sheet lists proposed speakers for parliamentary business scheduled to commence during the week. The business includes amendment bills concerning tea, silk, advocates, petroleum, monopolies and restrictive trade practices, the Indian Post Office, chartered accountants, labour laws, the Central Reserve Police Force, Delhi shops and establishments, government liability in tort, and contract labour. The sheet also assigns speakers to discussions on alleged subversive and violent activities, the Food Corporation of India, the communal situation, drought in Rajasthan, sugar policy, and decentralisation of cement. Several entries are crossed out, handwritten annotations appear in the right margin, and the final speaker’s surname is partly obscured. No author, issuing body, date, or publication information is printed on the visible page.

## Key points

- The document is a one-page parliamentary planning record headed “Proposed speakers for the business commencing during this week.” It pairs numbered bills and discussion topics with proposed parliamentary speakers. The listed business spans commercial regulation, labour law, public-sector institutions, policing, constitutional amendment, and government liability. Additional agenda items address subversive and violent activities, communal conditions, drought in Rajasthan, sugar policy, and cement decentralisation. The page includes bills dated 1967, 1968, and 1969, but does not itself print a date. Several lines are crossed out or otherwise difficult to read, including the final entry.
- The handwritten number “2997” appears at the top of the page.
