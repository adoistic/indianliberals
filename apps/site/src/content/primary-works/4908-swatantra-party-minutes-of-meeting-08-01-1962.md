## Key points

- The Organisation Sub-Committee met in Madras on 5 January 1962.
- Professor N. G. Ranga chaired the meeting.
- C. Rajagopalachari and M. R. Masani were recorded as present.
- L. N. Seth was to be nominated to the Swatantra Party’s General Council.
- Sanserchand, Budhi Perkash, and Lajpat Rai were recommended for nomination to the State Executive Committee.
