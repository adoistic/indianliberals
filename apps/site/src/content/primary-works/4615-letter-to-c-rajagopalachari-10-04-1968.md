## Key points

- Masani writes from New Delhi on 10 April 1968.
- He addresses C. Rajagopalachari as “Rajaji.”
- He acknowledges Rajagopalachari’s letter of 5 April.
- He declines a plea to withdraw his resignation.
- He says that there are good reasons not to withdraw the resignation.
- He refers to an explanatory letter to Ranga and encloses a copy.
