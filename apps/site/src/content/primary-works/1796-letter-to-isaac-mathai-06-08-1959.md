## Key points

- The letter is dated 6th August 1959.
- It is addressed to Isaac Mathai, c/o Mathai's Publications, Bombay-7.
- It refers to Mathai's letter of 3rd August.
- Sir Homi Mody agrees to meet Mathai.
- The proposed meeting is at Blavatsky Lodge, French Bridge, on Monday, 10th August, at 5:30 p.m.
- The signatory is not personally named and is identified only as Secretary to Sir Homi Mody.
