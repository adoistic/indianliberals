## Key points

- The Managing Committee met at the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra office on 24 October 1986.
- S. V. Raju chaired the meeting in the absence of President Parmanand Kejriwal.
- J. Venkiteswaran’s resignation as General Secretary was accepted, while he remained a committee member.
- L. R. Sampat became General Secretary and P. N. Subramanian was elected Joint Secretary.
- The listed Managing Committee and office-bearers were to continue for 1987.
- The committee resolved to pursue eviction notices concerning premises at 143, Mahatma Gandhi Road.
- Nitin G. Raut was entrusted with the premises matter and asked to consult senior lawyers.
