## Summary

These one-page minutes record a Swatantra Party parliamentary meeting held on the morning of 1 November 1966 in the Opposition Members' Room, Parliament House. M. R. Masani chaired the meeting, which was attended by twelve members, including N. Dandeker, Dabyabhai V. Patel, P. K. Deo, Kapur Singh, Dhanna Singh Gulshan, P. H. Bheel, S. S. Mariswamy, C. L. N. Reddy, Ram Singh, and P. N. Solanki.

## Key points

- The document records a Swatantra Party parliamentary meeting dated 1 November 1966.
- The meeting took place in the Opposition Members' Room in Parliament House.
- Twelve members were listed as present, with M. R. Masani in the chair.
- The party decided to support a no-confidence motion in the Council of Ministers tabled by Jan Sangh leader Shri U. M. Trivedi.
- M. R. Masani was requested to speak on behalf of the Swatantra Party.
- N. Dandeker and Sardar Dhanna Singh Gulshan were assigned to speak on the Contract Labour Bill.
- Sardar Kapur Singh was assigned to speak on the Kerala Proclamation.
- The meeting adjourned with a vote of thanks to the chair and was signed by P. N. Solanki as secretary.
