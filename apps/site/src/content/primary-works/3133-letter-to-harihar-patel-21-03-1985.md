## Key points

- S. V. Raju’s typed letter is dated March 21, 1985, and addressed to Harihar Patel, whom it calls “Haribabu.”
- Raju thanks Patel for sending papers and says Patel’s suggestions are being acted upon.
- Raju plans to write to Ghasiram Majhi.
- Raju intends to arrange a meeting of former Swatantra Party members with Minoo Masani.
- The handwritten enclosure discusses party organisation, political activity, and the circulation of papers and information.
- The correspondence documents ongoing networking among liberal political figures after the Swatantra Party’s formal dissolution.
