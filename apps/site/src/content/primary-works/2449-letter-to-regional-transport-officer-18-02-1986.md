## Key points

- The letter is addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, Bombay.
- Masani disputes a bill dated 1 February 1986 for Rs. 1,814.
- He states that vehicle No. MRG 2347 never belonged to him and is not in his possession.
- He returns the bill on the grounds that no payment is due from him.
- He says he has repeatedly written about bills concerning jeeps formerly owned by the Swatantra Party.
- He states that the party sold those jeeps in 1967 and that the authorities were properly informed.
- He asks the officer to stop sending him the bills, which he describes as repeated harassment.
