## Key points

- The letter is dated 18 February 1986 and is addressed to the Regional Transport Officer of Old Bombay-Durgapur Lines.
- Masani returns bill no. MRG-2347-860201, dated 1 February 1986, for Rs. 1,814.00.
- He denies responsibility because vehicle no. MRG 2347 never belonged to him and is not in his possession.
- He says he has repeatedly complained about bills for Swatantra Party jeeps sold in 1957.
- He asks the transport office to stop sending him the disputed bills.
- The letter includes the bill as an enclosure.
