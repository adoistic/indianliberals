## Key points

- The letter is addressed to N. Dandeker, M.P., General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- Karunakar Das requests an emergency meeting of the Party's General Council.
- The proposed meeting is to discuss Minoo Masani's resignation.
- Masani's resignation concerns his membership of the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- Das asks the Party to close its ranks and resume united work under its leaders.
