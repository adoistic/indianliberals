## Key points

- The document is Swatantra Party correspondence dated June 28, 1971.
- It is titled “General Secretary's Letter No. 266.”
- Its subject is a sub-committee established to review the party’s policies.
- A questionnaire had been sent to party units at primary, district, and state levels.
- The party planned to translate and distribute the questionnaire in state languages.
- The recipient is asked to submit the questionnaire to the relevant State Executive sub-committee.
- The recipient’s name and the questionnaire itself are not included in the rendered pages.
- The first rendered page is a separate The Hindu clipping dated January 21, 1973, concerning a committee for the Rajaji Foundation.
