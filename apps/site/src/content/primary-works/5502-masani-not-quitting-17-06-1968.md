## Key points

- The report is headed “Masani not quitting” and is datelined Lucknow, June 10.
- It concerns Minoo Masani and the Swatantra Party.
- Bahu Pratap Singh is identified as president of the party’s Uttar Pradesh unit.
- Masani’s decision is presented as an attempt to generate greater awareness and dedication among party members.
- The report links this renewed commitment to the proper functioning of the party.
- The clipping is credited to UNI, but the source newspaper is not identified.
