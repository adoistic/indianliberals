## Summary

This one-page English circular, issued by the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office in New Delhi on February 17, 1965, convenes a meeting of the party’s Executive Committee for February 18, 1965, at 10:15 a.m. in the Party Committee Room, Central Hall, Parliament House.

The agenda concerns parliamentary business: preparing the party’s amendments to the President’s Address and deciding which members will speak on the President’s Address and the Railway Budget. The circular is signed by P. N. Solanki, Secretary, and does not contain an authorial byline.

## Key points

- The document is an internal circular from the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office.
- It is dated February 17, 1965, and addressed to Executive Committee members.
- A meeting was scheduled for February 18, 1965, at 10:15 a.m. in Parliament House’s Central Hall.
- The first agenda item was party amendments to the President’s Address.
- The second agenda item was settling speakers for the President’s Address and the Railway Budget.
- The circular is signed by P. N. Solanki, Secretary.
