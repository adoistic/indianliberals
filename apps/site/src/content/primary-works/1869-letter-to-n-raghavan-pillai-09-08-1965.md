## Key points

- The letter is dated August 9, 1965, and signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani responds to a July 21 letter from Sir N. Raghavan Pillai.
- Pillai had invited Masani to place his views before a committee.
- Masani says he will try to respond to the invitation.
- He expects that a response may not be possible until the following month because of existing commitments.
- The invitation concerns the Indian Foreign Service Committee of the Ministry of External Affairs, but its subject is not specified.
