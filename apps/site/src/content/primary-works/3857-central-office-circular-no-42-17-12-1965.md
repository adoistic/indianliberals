## Key points

- The document is headed “CENTRAL OFFICE CIRCULAR NO. 42.”
- It was issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay on December 17, 1965.
- The circular announces an enclosed publication titled “Our Foreign Policy.”
- M. R. Masani is identified as the author of the enclosed publication, not as the author of the circular.
- Bulk purchases of 100 copies or more qualify for a 25% discount.
- A single copy is priced at 20 paise.
- The circular is signed by A. X. Coelho, Office Manager.
