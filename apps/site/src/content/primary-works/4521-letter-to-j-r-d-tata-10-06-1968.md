## Key points

- Masani writes in the context of a current controversy within the Swatantra Party.
- He identifies developments in Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Haryana as causes of a setback for the party.
- He says he is attempting to mitigate damage to the party's good name and credibility.
- The letters were sent confidentially to senior supporters and associates of the party.
- Masani encloses a letter addressed to members of the General Council.
- He also encloses a copy of Dr. K. M. Munshi's letter.
- The third page records ten additional recipients and indicates that copies were sent by hand to Dharamsey Khatau.
