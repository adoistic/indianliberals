## Key points

- V. S. Srikumar writes to S. V. Raju from the Swatantra Party National Headquarters on 7 May 1976.
- The letter acknowledges correspondence about an income-tax notice and a note addressed to Girish Munshi.
- Srikumar proposes a National Executive meeting on current political developments and opposition-party coordination.
- He urges the party to respond vigorously to proposals he believes would further damage the Constitution.
- Bombay is proposed as the meeting venue, provisionally during the second week of June 1976.
- The proposed meeting may include J. P. and Masani, and Srikumar asks Raju to coordinate with other members.
- Srikumar says he will consult the party president and act on his instructions regarding the income-tax matter.
