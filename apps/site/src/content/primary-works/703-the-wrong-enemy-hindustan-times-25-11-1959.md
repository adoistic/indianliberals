## Key points

- The article says earlier critics of the Nehru government's policies have largely been vindicated.
- It argues that the China policy and the Aksai Chin-Sinkiang road issue reveal serious failures of judgment.
- It claims that Chinese territorial concessions were being accepted under the guise of negotiation.
- It rejects the idea that criticism of the government is primarily partisan or motivated by hostility.
- It questions Congress's commitment to democratic debate and its willingness to accept responsibility for failures.
- It distinguishes the government's good faith from the correctness of its policies.
