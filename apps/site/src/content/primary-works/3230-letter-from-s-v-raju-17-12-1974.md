## Key points

- The letter is dated December 17, 1974, and is written from Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan in Bombay-7.
- C. K. Munshi addresses the recipient as Raju, resolved here as S. V. Raju.
- Munshi proposes a meeting on Saturday, December 21, at 10 a.m., at the Party Office.
- The proposed discussion is to include Parmanandji, Raju, Munshi, and other friends.
- Raju is asked to notify KH, Ramu, Pramod, Koppiker, and others.
- The letter does not specify the party, the matter to be discussed, or the identities of several invitees.
