## Summary

Two brief typed letters on All India Swatantra Party letterhead, both dated 12th November 1975 and addressed from the party's National Headquarters at 10-A, Flowers Road, Madras. Signed by V. S. Srikumar, Joint Secretary, and directed to Mr. M. R. Masani in Bombay, they summon him to two party gatherings scheduled for Saturday the 29th at the Local Library Authority Building on Mount Road, Madras: the second meeting of the National General Council at 3.30 p.m., and the third meeting of the National Executive earlier the same day at 10.30 a.m.

What makes these otherwise routine convening notices historically pointed is the single sentence explaining that "for obvious reasons the agenda is not mentioned in this letter." Written five months into Indira Gandhi's Emergency (declared 26 June 1975), when the Swatantra Party's leadership operated under press censorship and surveillance, the elision is itself the message: internal party business could no longer be committed to writing. Srikumar closes each letter with a request that Masani "kindly participate" and "give us the benefit of your mature advice," and a postscript offering to arrange hotel and return-journey tickets through the National Headquarters.

## Key points

- Two convening letters from the All India Swatantra Party National Headquarters in Madras, both dated 12 November 1975.
- Signed by V. S. Srikumar, Joint Secretary, and addressed to M. R. Masani in Bombay.
- Summon Masani to the second meeting of the National General Council (3.30 p.m.) and the third meeting of the National Executive (10.30 a.m.) on Saturday the 29th at the Local Library Authority Building, 150-A Mount Road, Madras-2.
- Explicitly withhold the agenda from the letter 'for obvious reasons' — a coded acknowledgement of Emergency-era censorship and surveillance.
- Handwritten annotations on page 1 (including the accession number '1186') and Masani's own signature suggest the letter was filed from his side.
- The postscript offers to arrange hotel booking and return-journey tickets through National Headquarters if advance notice is given.
- Document captures Swatantra's internal organisational life in the months after the Emergency was proclaimed and before the party's post-1974 reorganisation ran its course.
