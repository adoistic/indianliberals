## Key points

- The letter is addressed to N. Dandekar, M.P., General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- O. P. Pathak identifies himself as an advocate and a member of the party's General Council.
- Pathak requests an emergency meeting of the General Council.
- The proposed meeting is intended to discuss Minoo Masani's resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- Pathak seeks an appropriate decision that would allow the party to close ranks.
- The letter expresses a desire for the party to resume coordinated work under the leadership of its senior leaders.
