## Key points

- The letter is dated 8 February 1975 and was sent from Coimbatore.
- It is written on All India Swatantra Party letterhead bearing the Madras office address.
- K. Sundaram acknowledges S. V. Raju’s letter of 5 February 1975.
- Four resolutions are enclosed and returned duly signed by Sundaram.
- Letters will be sent directly to the friends listed by Raju, with copies later forwarded to Bombay.
- Sundaram agrees with Raju’s proposal concerning confirmation of the minutes.
- The letter also forwards a few minutes from earlier National Executives.
