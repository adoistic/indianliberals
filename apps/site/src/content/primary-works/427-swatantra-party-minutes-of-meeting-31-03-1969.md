## Key points

- The document amends paragraph 6 of the minutes of the General Body Meeting held on March 26, 1969.
- The meeting discussed the government's initiative to seek suspension of the rules for passage of the Constitution (Twenty-Second Amendment) Bill.
- The party decided not to object if the Speaker did not favour the government's initiative.
- If the Speaker gave permission, the party Leader and the party would support suspension of the rules.
- The amendment is signed by N. G. Ranga in his capacity as Leader.
