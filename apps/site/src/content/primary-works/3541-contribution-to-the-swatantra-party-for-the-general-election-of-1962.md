## Summary

This one-page typed office record lists contributions received by the Swatantra Party for the 1962 General Elections. It states that the contributions came in between June 1961 and March 1962 and records a total of Rs. 23,58,000. The largest listed contribution is Rs. 8,00,000 from the Tata Group of Companies, followed by Rs. 3,64,500 from Associated Cement Companies and Rs. 3,00,000 from Indian Iron & Steel Co. Ltd.

The record presents contributions from a broad range of industrial and commercial firms, including engineering, textiles, cement, construction, automotive, steel, chemical, and trading businesses. It does not identify an author, publisher, place of publication, or explicit document date beyond its reference to the 1962 elections.

## Key points

- The document concerns contributions to the Swatantra Party for the General Elections of 1962.
- It says the contributions were received between June 1961 and March 1962.
- The listed contributions total Rs. 23,58,000.
- The Tata Group of Companies is listed as the largest contributor, with Rs. 8,00,000.
- Associated Cement Companies and Indian Iron & Steel Co. Ltd. are listed with contributions of Rs. 3,64,500 and Rs. 3,00,000 respectively.
- The contributors are predominantly industrial and commercial companies across multiple sectors.
- No author, publisher, place, or explicit publication date is printed on the page.
