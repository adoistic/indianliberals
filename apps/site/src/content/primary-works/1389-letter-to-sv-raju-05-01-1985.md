## Key points

- H. M. Patel writes to S. V. Raju on 5 January 1985.
- Patel thanks Raju for his good wishes after Patel’s success in recent parliamentary elections.
- Patel identifies himself as an opposition member of the Eighth Parliament.
- The letter links personal support with the practical demands of effective parliamentary work.
- The document is a concise private thank-you letter on Charutar Vidya Mandal letterhead.
