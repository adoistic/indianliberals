## Key points

- The letter is dated 14 October 1975 and bears The Times of India, Bombay letterhead.
- Inder Malhotra invites M. R. Masani to write approximately 500 words for a year-end feature on books.
- The feature is intended to gather contributions from about a dozen book-lovers.
- Each contributor is asked to discuss three or four books enjoyed during the year and explain why.
- The requested bibliographic details are the full title, publisher, and price of each book.
- Malhotra asks Masani to confirm participation and send the contribution no later than 15 November.
