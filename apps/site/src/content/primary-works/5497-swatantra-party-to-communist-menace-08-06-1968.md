## Key points

- The Swatantra Party general council identified growing Communist influence in Kerala and Tamil Nadu as a major concern.
- The council sought a favourable Delhi response to Singapore’s mutual-defence proposal for South-East Asia.
- The party’s national executive took disciplinary action against several Gujarat members and expelled another member who had contested Rajaya Santhan’s election.
- M. R. Masani declined re-election to the national executive after objecting to the disciplinary action and what he described as the party’s apparent failure to act against more important members.
- N. Dandekar argued that the Gujarat dispute had been resolved and that the party could now function in a healthier way.
- The reported meeting was attended by only 69 of the 170 members, and party president Prof. N. G. Ranga did not attend.
