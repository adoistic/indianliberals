## Key points

- The first clipping reports A. N. Kosygin’s discussions with India’s Planning Commission in New Delhi.
- It describes a proposed effort to establish parity between private-sector and comparable public-sector industries.
- Kosygin is reported to have said that private industry could provide better wages to workers.
- The article presents Soviet production incentives and industrial development favorably.
- The clipping mentions Kosygin’s visits to Bhilai and Perambur Coach Factory and his comments on Ankleshwar oil deposits.
- The second clipping argues that India had barely accessed the potential of the United States market.
- It calls for improved commercial information, more business exchanges, and stronger banking contacts to expand Indian exports.
