## Key points

- Rev. M. Savino writes as leader of the Naga Integration Central Council delegation.
- The delegation consists of 15 members and is in New Delhi in November 1970.
- Its stated objective is the integration of contiguous Naga-inhabited areas with Nagaland State.
- Savino asks Minoo Masani to convene a meeting with the Swatantra Party's Executive Committee in Parliament.
- The letter requests that the meeting's time, date, and place be communicated to Savino.
