## Key points

- The letter is marked confidential and numbered No. F.9-2/63-CB.
- It was issued from New Delhi on August 3, 1963.
- The recipient is Shri M. R. Masani, M.P.
- The Prime Minister proposed an informal meeting with leading members of Parliament.
- The meeting was scheduled for 5:30 p.m. on Sunday, August 11, 1963.
- The proposed venue was Room No. 9, Ground Floor, Parliament House.
- The sender requested that Masani attend if convenient.
