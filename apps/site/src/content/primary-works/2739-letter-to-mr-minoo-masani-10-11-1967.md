## Key points

- The letter is dated 10 November 1967 and addressed to M. R. Masani, M.P.
- Seshadri acknowledges and thanks Masani for his letter of 7 November 1967.
- Seshadri says that K. T. Kosalram is taking a matter to the Supreme Court and urges Masani not to worry.
- The letter mentions a separate case involving Swatantra MLA Sri Cheemaichami, for which judgment is awaited.
- Seshadri expresses hope that he will be able to resolve or carry through the Cheemaichami case.
- Seshadri responds sympathetically to Masani’s wish to resign his secretaryship, saying that few people would do what Masani is doing.
