## Key points

- A Bombay High Court petition challenges the Government’s monopoly over radio and television broadcasting.
- The petition alleges that denying citizens permission to establish independent broadcasting stations violates Article 19(1)(a).
- Minoo Masani and former All India Radio Director-General P. C. Chatterji are identified as the petitioners.
- The petition treats radio and television broadcasting as media through which individuals exercise freedom of speech and expression.
- It argues that the freedom of the press and the freedom to broadcast are encompassed within the same constitutional guarantee.
- The petitioners contend that no legislation authorises the Government’s monopoly over broadcasting.
- The article states that the petition challenges the monopoly as an executive or administrative creation lacking legal effect.
