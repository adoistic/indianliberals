## Summary

General Council Circular No. 52 is a one-page administrative notice issued by the Swatantra Party national headquarters in Bombay and dated 24 November 1973. Signed by General Secretary Madhu Mehta, it congratulates newly elected General Council members and announces the Council’s first meeting to elect office-bearers and members of the National Executive for the coming two years.

## Key points

- The circular is addressed to members elected to the Swatantra Party General Council.
- It announces a General Council meeting at Ravindra Mandap, Char Bagh, Lucknow, on 15–16 December 1973.
- The meeting is scheduled to begin at 3 p.m. on Saturday and continue through the following evening.
- The State Office offers either free accommodation in MLA quarters or dormitory-style accommodation, or hotel accommodation at the member’s cost.
- Members are asked to notify Uttar Pradesh General Secretary H. K. Srivastava of their arrival details, accommodation needs, and return reservation plans.
- The attached hotel list gives single and double room rates for air-conditioned and non-air-conditioned hotels in Lucknow.
