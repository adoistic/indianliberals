## Key points

- The letter is Swatantra Party General Secretary's Letter No. 13, dated April 28, 1967.
- It concerns voting procedures in the presidential election.
- The voting system is described as a single transferable vote.
- Recipients are instructed to cast their first-preference vote for Mr. Subba Rao.
- Recipients are instructed not to exercise a second preference.
- Dandekar warns that a second-preference vote could injure the party candidate's chances.
- The letter is signed by N. Dandekar as Offtg. General Secretary.
