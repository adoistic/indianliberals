## Summary

In this letter dated 29 April 1970, Minoo Masani thanks Jayanthbhai Bhatt, General Secretary of the Ahmedabad City Swatantra Party, for his letter and for the Ahmedabad City Committee's support for the position taken by the Swatantra Party's National Executive. Masani says he was encouraged that almost all members of the committee agreed with that position.

## Key points

- Minoo Masani wrote from New Delhi on 29 April 1970.
- The letter acknowledges and thanks Jayanthbhai Bhatt for his communication of 17 April.
- Masani notes that almost all members of the Ahmedabad City Committee supported the National Executive's position.
- He asks Bhatt to convey his thanks to the committee's colleagues for their message of support.
- Masani expresses confidence in the loyalty and disciplined support of a large majority of Swatantra Party members in Gujarat.
