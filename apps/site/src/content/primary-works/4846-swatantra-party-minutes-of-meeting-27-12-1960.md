## Key points

- The General Council confirmed the minutes of its 4 October 1969 meeting at Bhubaneswar.
- Members deferred consideration of a sub-committee report on the problems of linguistic states.
- M. R. Masani presented the National Executive’s Mid-Term Report, which the Council adopted after discussion and amendments.
- The Council appointed a committee to examine and recommend modifications to the party’s agricultural resolution.
- Resolutions were unanimously adopted on preventive detention, communal disturbances in Ahmedabad, violence in West Bengal, the death of Sardar Darshan Singh Pheruman, and natural calamities.
- The minutes record apologies and tributes concerning the health or deaths of senior party figures, including N. Dandekar and Sardar Ram Singh.
- The opening page recommends a party structure consisting of five vice-presidents, up to five joint secretaries, two joint treasurers, and twenty executive members.
