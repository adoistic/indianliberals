## Summary

In this letter dated March 12, 1970, S. V. Raju informs M. R. Masani that Dr. Cooper has agreed to be available at the Party Office, using the air-conditioned room of the Bombay Branch, from 1 p.m. to 2 p.m. on most days. Raju specifies that the arrangement excludes Tuesdays and Saturdays.

## Key points

- The letter is dated March 12, 1970 and is addressed to M. R. Masani.
- Dr. Cooper is expected to be available at the Party Office.
- The office will use the air-conditioned room of the Bombay Branch.
- The stated availability is between 1 p.m. and 2 p.m.
- Tuesdays and Saturdays are excluded from the arrangement.
- Raju invites Masani to visit if he wishes to do so.
- Raju recommends confirming Dr. Cooper's actual availability before visiting.
