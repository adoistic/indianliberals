## Summary

This one-page Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office circular, dated April 16, 1969, amends an earlier whip dated April 7. It changes the date of elections to the party’s financial committees from Monday, April 28, to Tuesday, April 29, and instructs members to attend the Lok Sabha between 11:30 and 16:30 to vote. The elections are to be held in Room No. 62.

## Key points

- The circular amends a whip issued on April 7, 1969.
- Elections to the financial committees are rescheduled from April 28 to April 29.
- Party members are instructed to attend the Lok Sabha between 11:30 and 16:30 to vote.
- The elections will take place in Room No. 62.
- The notice lists nominees for the Public Accounts Committee, Committee on Public Undertakings, and Estimates Committee.
- The document is signed by Y. G. Gowd, identified as the Swatantra Party’s Chief Whip.
