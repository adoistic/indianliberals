summary: |-
  These minutes record the Swatantra Party Bombay Region Executive Committee meeting held on 13 November 1972. With the President and Vice-Presidents absent, Shantilal L. Thar chaired the meeting. The committee noted poor attendance, a precarious financial position, difficulty recruiting members, and the Party’s limited public impact. It urged stronger efforts to mobilise opposition to rising prices and to launch a movement or agitation against government policy.
---

## Key points

- The committee discussed weak attendance and proposed that the President write to members who had not been attending meetings.
- The Party’s finances were described as being in a precarious state, while earlier plans to solicit funds had made no progress.
- Members attributed difficulty in recruiting new members to the Party’s inability to project its image and focus public attention on rising prices.
- The committee considered organising taxi drivers around grievances concerning maintenance costs, spare parts, and fuel, and noted the need to engage more seriously with trade-union activity.
- It proposed opposing increases in State Transport fares through a programme that might include legal action.
- The committee criticised proposed increases in municipal water tax and sought clarification regarding a passenger tax on BEST commuters.
- The meeting also addressed Rajaji’s 94th birthday, forthcoming municipal elections, a Rs. 200 staff advance, and three resignations that were accepted with regret.
