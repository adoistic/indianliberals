## Key points

- M. R. Masani refuses to withdraw his resignation from the Swatantra Party's national executive.
- He tells general secretary N. Dandekar that he would reconsider if the party restored ethical and disciplined functioning.
- The national executive approved possible disciplinary action, including expulsion, against members who opposed Swatantra candidates in Gujarat Rajya Sabha elections.
- Masani criticises the executive for failing to address alleged failures of duty by senior party and state office-holders.
- The report links the dispute to wider concerns about idealism, ethics, discipline, and democratic political practice.
- The party's general council was expected to consider the matter in Madras.
