## Summary

In this one-page English letter dated 23 October 195[illegible], M. R. Masani replies to Mr. N. Parthasarathy and thanks him for an invitation. Masani explains that he will only be passing briefly through Madras on the 31st and will leave for Tanjore that same evening.

## Key points

- M. R. Masani acknowledges receiving Parthasarathy's letter of 18 October.
- He thanks Parthasarathy for the invitation.
- Masani will be passing through Madras only briefly on 31 October.
- He is scheduled to leave for Tanjore on the evening of the same day.
- His daytime schedule is occupied by the Swatantra Party district conference.
- He therefore declines participation in any other public engagement on that occasion.
- The letter is addressed to Mr. N. Parthasarathy at The Triplicane Cultural Academy in Madras.
