## Summary

This one-page English letter, dated 21 September 1959, is addressed by Miss B.S. Mirza to Mr. S.L. Pareek. Mirza thanks Pareek for a postcard and says she is enclosing materials requested by him concerning the Swatantra Party.

The enclosures listed are M.R. Masani's opening remarks at the party's Preparatory Convention in Bombay, copies of speeches by C. Rajagopalachari and Prof. Ranga, and the party's Statement of Principles. The letter does not reproduce any of these enclosed materials; it records only their transmission and Mirza's expectation that they will be of interest.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 21 September 1959.
- It is addressed to Mr. S.L. Pareek at 'Menasvayan', 32 Feiz Bazar, Delhi 7.
- Miss B.S. Mirza thanks Pareek for a postcard dated the 17th.
- Mirza encloses M.R. Masani's opening remarks from the Swatantra Party's Preparatory Convention in Bombay.
- The enclosures also include speeches by C. Rajagopalachari and Prof. Ranga.
- A Statement of Principles of the Swatantra Party is enclosed.
- The letter contains no substantive discussion of the enclosed speeches or party principles.
