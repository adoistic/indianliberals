## Summary

This single-page document is a typed copy of a telegram sent by post in confirmation, dated 13 April 1964, from C. Rajagopalachari in Madras to M. R. Masani in New Delhi. Rajagopalachari approves Masani’s “second alternative” line, referring to a letter dated 10 April, and remarks that Masani had made a better intervention on the subject on the seventeenth. The document does not identify the subject or provide the surrounding correspondence.
