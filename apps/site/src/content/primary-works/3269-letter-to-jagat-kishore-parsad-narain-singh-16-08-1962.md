## Key points

- Masani forwards a unanimously adopted party resolution from a joint meeting held in Madras on 21–22 July 1962.
- He asks Jagat Kishore Prasad Narain Singh to facilitate N. Baksi's assumption of office as General Secretary of the Bihar unit.
- The first letter calls for an early meeting of the Bihar unit's new Executive Committee.
- Masani appeals for Singh's fullest cooperation in reorganising the party on what he calls sound lines.
- In the 17 August letter to C. Rajagopalachari, Masani reports that K. M. Munshi has accepted the position expressed in the Madras resolution.
- Masani acknowledges that his telegram and handling of the episode caused Rajagopalachari personal distress, while defending the precautions he took regarding the party's future.
- The second letter states that Masani planned to arrange a release for 30 August rather than 25 August, as Rajagopalachari had suggested.
