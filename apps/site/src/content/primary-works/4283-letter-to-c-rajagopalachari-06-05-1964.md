## Key points

- The document is an express telegram dated 6 May 1964.
- It is addressed to C. Rajagopalachari at 60 Bazullah Road, Madras 17.
- The sender identifies the Kashmir issue as a matter expected to arise during the parliamentary session.
- Several state units are described as insisting that guidance be given to party members.
- The sender proposes a meeting of the Parliamentary Board in Madras on 16 or 17 May.
- The message asks the recipient to wire Bombay if the proposed meeting is convenient.
