## Key points

- Munshi writes to C. Rajagopalachari from Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan on January 12, 1964.
- He cancels a planned visit to Bangalore because his health has become too fragile for travel and lengthy discussions.
- He approves a note prepared by M.R. Masani concerning the Ramgarh affair.
- He characterises Ramgarh’s position as obstructive and predicts lengthy litigation if his side yields.
- Munshi recommends refusing further discussion and allowing matters to take their course.
- He says he has spoken with the Raja of Maksudpur and insists that Ramgarh honour earlier assurances before expecting a response.
- Copies of the letter were sent to Prof. N.G. Ranga and M.R. Masani.
