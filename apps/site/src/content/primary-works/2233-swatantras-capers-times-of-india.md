## Key points

- The article examines Swatantra Party leaders' recovery from electoral defeat and later political setbacks.
- It presents the party's Madras annual convention as a missed opportunity for ideological clarification.
- The commentary portrays Swatantra leaders as distracted by rhetorical claims rather than focused on redefining the party's national role.
- Mr. Mody's blanket denunciation of socialism is criticized as politically and intellectually unserious.
- Mr. Masani's rejection of conventional right-left labels is quoted and then challenged.
- The article also criticizes Swatantra's claim to a superior moral code and its cooperation with discredited politicians in Cuttack.
