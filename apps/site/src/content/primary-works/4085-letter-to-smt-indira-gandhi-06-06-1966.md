## Summary

This one-page English express telegram, sent by Minoo Masani from Bombay on 6 June 1966, is addressed to Prime Minister Indira Gandhi at her New Delhi residence. Masani accepts Gandhi's invitation to meet in Delhi and proposes bringing a colleague.

## Key points

- The document is an express telegram and confirmation copy by post.
- Minoo Masani is the sender.
- The message is addressed to Prime Minister Indira Gandhi in New Delhi.
- Masani agrees to travel to Delhi for a meeting.
- The proposed discussion concerns the implications of devaluation.
- Masani also intends to present suggestions for making devaluation fruitful.
- He offers availability on the 10th, 11th, or any day after the 14th, between 10 a.m. and 5 p.m.
