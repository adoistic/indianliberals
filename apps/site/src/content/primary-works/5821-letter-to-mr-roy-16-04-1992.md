  This five-page English correspondence dossier documents a 1992 parliamentary-privilege dispute involving Minoo Masani, The Statesman, and a column titled “As I see it.” The file opens with a 14 April letter from Basudev Ray, of The Statesman Ltd., to Masani, enclosing copies of a notice of question of privilege brought by Hannan Mollah and eight other members against the newspaper’s editor, printer, publisher, and Masani. ## Key points

- Basudev Ray’s 14 April 1992 letter to Minoo Masani concerns a notice of question of privilege involving The Statesman and Masani’s column “As I see it.”
- The notice was brought by Hannan Mollah and eight other members against the editor, printer, and publisher of The Statesman (Calcutta Edition), as well as Masani.
- Ray asks Masani to send comments to the Lok Sabha at the earliest and notes that Mr. Irani is temporarily out of the country.
- Lok Sabha Secretariat letters dated 26 and 31 March 1992 request an urgent statement from Masani for consideration by the Speaker, Lok Sabha.
- Masani’s 16 April reply says he has already responded directly to the Lok Sabha office and sent Ray a spare copy.
- The dossier records procedural and correspondence aspects of the dispute, not the text of the allegedly objectionable column or the parties’ full arguments.
