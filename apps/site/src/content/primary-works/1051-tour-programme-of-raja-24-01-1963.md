## Key points

- One-page typed itinerary for C. Rajagopalachari covering 27-30 January 1963.
- Rajaji travels Madras to Bangalore by overnight Mail train, departing 8.45 p.m. on 27 January 1963.
- Accommodation in Bangalore: Madras Woodlands Hotel, Sampangi Tank Road.
- Public meeting scheduled at Bangalore at 6.15 p.m. on Tuesday, 29 January 1963.
- Return leg departs Bangalore at 8.40 p.m. on 29 January, arriving Madras Central on 30 January.
- Signed and dated 24 January 1963 by a staff member (signature partially legible).
- Purely logistical document — no speech text, correspondence, or policy content.
