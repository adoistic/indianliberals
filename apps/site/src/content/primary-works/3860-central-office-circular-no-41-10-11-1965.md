## Key points

- The circular is issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office from Bombay.
- It is dated November 10, 1965, and addressed to party units.
- The Lajpatrai Centenary Committee requested the Central Office to encourage state-unit participation.
- The proposed observance is Lajpatrai Martyrdom Day on November 17.
- Units may accept invitations to meetings organized in their states.
- Participation is not required where no invitation has been issued.
- S. V. Raju signs the circular as Executive Secretary.
