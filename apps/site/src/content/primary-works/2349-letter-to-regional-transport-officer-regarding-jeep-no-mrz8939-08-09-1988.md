## Key points

- The letter is dated September 8, 1988 and is addressed to the Regional Transport Officer in Bombay.
- Masani’s subject is Jeep No. MRZ 8939.
- He refers to an earlier letter dated December 9, 1986, sent by registered post.
- He disputes a renewed demand made by the transport office.
- He characterizes the demand as ridiculous and not legally or factually due.
- He asks the officer to stop the alleged harassment.
- The accompanying pages document postal acknowledgment and the sender’s envelope address.
