## Key points

- The letter is addressed to Minoo Masani and dated 5 November 1959.
- Shroff discusses the prospects of an independent political party, evidently the newly formed Swatantra Party.
- He considers the difficulty of explaining the party’s principles and programme to the public.
- The letter refers to economic arguments and the need for an intellectually coherent political position.
- Shroff mentions the roles or views of several contemporary political figures in relation to the party’s development.
- The surviving document consists of two handwritten pages on printed letterhead.
