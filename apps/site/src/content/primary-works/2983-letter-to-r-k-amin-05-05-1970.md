## Key points

- The document is an internal Swatantra Party appeal to all members.
- The Party reports outstanding bills exceeding Rs. 7,000.
- Members are asked to make a special voluntary contribution of at least Rs. 100.
- The proposed contribution is payable in two instalments of Rs. 50.
- Larger contributions are welcomed as assistance to the Parliamentary Office.
- Members are asked to indicate the amount and payment method they intend to use.
- R. K. Amin signs the circular as Secretary.
