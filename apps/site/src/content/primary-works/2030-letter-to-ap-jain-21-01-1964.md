  In this letter dated January 21, 1964, Minoo Masani asks A. P. ## Key points

- The letter is dated January 21, 1964, and is signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani asks A. P. Jain to send a draft report on the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group's work.
- The report was intended for presentation in Bangalore.
- Masani wants to review the draft before considering its distribution to General Council members.
- He directs that materials for Bangalore be distributed only through the Central Office.
- He warns that revisions may be required and notes that related material already appears in the G. S. Report.
