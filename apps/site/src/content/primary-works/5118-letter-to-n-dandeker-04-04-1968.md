## Key points

- The letter is dated 4 April 1968 and is addressed to N. Dandekar, M.P., General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
- Dr. A. R. Khan requests that an emergency meeting of the party’s General Council be convened.
- The proposed meeting is to discuss Mr. Masani’s resignation from the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- Khan asks the party to take an appropriate decision in response to the situation.
- The stated objective is to help the Swatantra Party close ranks and work unitedly under the leadership of its respected leaders.
