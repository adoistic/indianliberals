## Key points

- The letter is dated May 20, 1964, and is presented as an extract.
- Singh describes meeting leading advocates in Chandigarh in connection with High Court petitions.
- He discussed the Swatantra Party's viewpoint on the Kashmir issue.
- The advocates reportedly welcomed a statement issued by the party's Parliamentary Board after a meeting in Madras.
- The statement is described as opposing the delivery of Kashmir to Pakistan.
- The extract calls for an acceptable formula involving all concerned parties to be found as quickly as possible.
