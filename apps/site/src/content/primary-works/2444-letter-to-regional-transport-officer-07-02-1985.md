## Key points

- Masani disputes a motor-vehicle tax bill issued for Jeep No. MRG. 2347.
- The bill is identified as No. 840201, dated December 1, 1984, for April 1984 through January 1985.
- The vehicle had belonged to the Swatantra Party for use during the 1971 elections.
- Masani says the vehicle was sold to someone outside Bombay immediately after the election.
- He cites letters from the Bharatiya Lok Dal's Executive Secretary and S. V. Raju concerning the vehicle's transfer.
- He returns the bill and requests its cancellation, asserting that he owes nothing in respect of the vehicle.
