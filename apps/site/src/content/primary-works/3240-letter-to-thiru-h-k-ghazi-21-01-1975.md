## Summary

Dated 21 January 1975, this one-page letter from W. S. Srinivasan, Joint Secretary of the All India Swatantra Party, is addressed to H. K. Ghazi, Chief Electoral Officer and Special Additional Secretary to the Government of Madras. Srinivasan reports that T. Swaminathan, Chief Election Commissioner, is expected to visit Madras in the first week of February 1975.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 21 January 1975 and addressed to H. K. Ghazi in the Public (Election.III) Department at Fort St. George, Madras.
- T. Swaminathan, Chief Election Commissioner, is expected to visit Madras in early February 1975.
- G. K. Sundaram, identified as President of the All India Swatantra Party, seeks a personal meeting with the Chief Election Commissioner.
- The requested meeting is intended to allow Sundaram to make representations relating to party matters.
- Srinivasan asks the Chief Electoral Officer to arrange a suitable date, time, and place and communicate the details to the party.
- Copies are marked to G. K. Sundaram and Girish Munshi, General Secretary of the All India Swatantra Party.
- The document bears the archival number 3240 in a handwritten circle at the top.
