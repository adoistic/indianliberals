summary: |-
  This one-page letter, dated February 19, 1965, is from M. R. Masani of the Parliamentary Group Office in New Delhi to the Secretary of the Lok Sabha, for the attention of Mr. P. K. Patnaik. Masani writes about an amendment to the President’s Address that had been tabled in his name and others’ and circulated in List 2. He points out that the amendment’s entire paragraph (b), which he considers important, was omitted from the circulated version and requests that the corrected amendment be recirculated.
  
  Masani also asks that clauses extending beyond the text in the attached amendment be dropped because they were not part of the amendment tabled by his group. The letter is signed by Masani and marked as accompanied by an enclosure; the enclosure itself is not included in the rendered page.

## Key points

- Masani writes from the Parliamentary Group Office on February 19, 1965.
- The recipient is the Secretary of the Lok Sabha, for the attention of Mr. P. K. Patnaik.
- An amendment to the President’s Address was tabled in Masani’s name and others’ and circulated in List 2.
- The circulated amendment omitted the whole of paragraph (b).
- Masani requests that the corrected amendment be recirculated.
- He asks that clauses beyond the attached amendment be removed because they were not tabled by his group.
