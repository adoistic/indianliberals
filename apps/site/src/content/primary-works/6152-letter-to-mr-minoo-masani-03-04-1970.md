## Key points

- The letter is dated April 3, 1970, and is signed by S. V. Raju.
- Raju addresses Minoo Masani regarding a draft letter to MPs, a Gujarat MLA, and the State Executive.
- He has revised the draft’s second paragraph to adopt a more cautious tone.
- The material is being circulated for information, not to seek the recipients’ views.
- Raju wants to avoid implying that a decision has already been made to expel Mr. G. C. Desai.
- He asks Masani to wire his agreement so that the covering letters can be ready on Monday.
