## Summary

This two-page English-language institutional record documents the educational eligibility and examination results of Vinod Raju. Page 1 is a provisional certificate issued by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, Pune Divisional Board, for admission to the first year of Junior College (Higher Secondary Standard XI). It states that the named candidate is eligible for provisional admission to a recognised institution, subject to submission of specified documents and later issuance of a final eligibility certificate.

## Key points

- The document records provisional eligibility for admission to First Year of Junior College, Higher Secondary Standard XI.
- The certificate names Vinod Raju as the candidate.
- The issuing authority is the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, Pune Divisional Board.
- Final eligibility depended on submission of certified or attested educational and character documents.
- The listed documents include an application form, statement of marks, passing certificate, school leaving or transfer certificate, migration certificate, and character certificate.
- The second page records University of London General Certificate of Education overseas results for candidate Raju Vinod at Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in January 1983.
- The results notice lists grades in commerce, English language, history, mathematics, English literature, and physics/chemistry.
