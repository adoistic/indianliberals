## Summary

This one-page letter, dated September 3, 1974, is from I. S. Bhargava in Jaipur to S. V. Raju in Bombay. Bhargava asks for Raju's advice following the implementation of the majority decision to merge the Swatantra Party into the Bhartiya Lok Dal.

## Key points

- The letter is dated September 3, 1974, and was sent from Jaipur.
- I. S. Bhargava writes to S. V. Raju.
- The subject is Minoo Masani's opposition to the merger of the Swatantra Party into the Bhartiya Lok Dal.
- Bhargava notes that the majority decision has been implemented and the merger announced.
- He says that several former workers have approached him for guidance.
- He asks Raju what course of action should be recommended to those workers.
