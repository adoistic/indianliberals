## Summary

In this three-page handwritten letter from Gondal to Minoo Masani, the sender, signing as “Khusru,” discusses a political crisis within the Gujarat Swatantra Party. After reviewing papers, including C. C. Desai’s reply and the national headquarters’ notes, the sender argues that the only practical course is to remove an “undisciplined opportunist” from the party. The letter describes tensions between Patels and Rajputs in Gujarat, with the Bhai Kaka controversy presented as a destabilising factor.

## Key points

- The sender urges Masani not to let Gujarat’s political difficulties disturb him, while explaining the decision to attend a meeting.
- A review of C. C. Desai’s reply and the national headquarters’ notes leads the sender to favour expelling an allegedly undisciplined opportunist from the party.
- The letter identifies a conflict in Gujarat between Patels and Rajputs and says the Bhai Kaka controversy has weakened Gujarat’s position.
- The sender warns that even a small provocation could trigger a much larger political crisis.
- The writer argues that H. M. Patel should act immediately, while the party president in Gujarat and the leader in the House appear to favour neutrality or inaction.
- The letter advises Masani to adopt an emotional and rational approach, avoid hasty action, and preserve the party’s stability.
- The sender ultimately says that, despite reservations, he will support action if Masani wants it, while urging restraint and a measured response.
