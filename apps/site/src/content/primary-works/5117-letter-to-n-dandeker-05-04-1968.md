## Key points

- The letter requests an emergency meeting of the Swatantra Party General Council.
- The proposed meeting is intended to address Minoo Masani's resignation.
- Masani's resignation is described as being from the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The writer calls for a decision that would enable the Party to close ranks.
- The letter urges the Party to work unitedly under the leadership of its respected leaders.
