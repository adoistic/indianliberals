## Summary

This typed office letter, dated 14 September 1976, is addressed to S. V. Raju at the All India Swatantra Party in Bombay and concerns the 44th Constitution Amendment Bill. V. S. Srikumar reports that, after discussing the Bill with G. K. Sundaram, the party has decided to constitute a panel of its lawyers to study it and submit a report.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 14 September 1976 and is addressed to S. V. Raju.
- It concerns the 44th Constitution Amendment Bill.
- The party plans to constitute a panel of lawyers to study the Bill and submit a report.
- The report is to be considered at a special party conference in Madras on 9 October 1976.
- The sender asks Raju to provide his detailed views on the Bill in advance.
- The letter requests Raju to encourage participation in the conference from Bombay.
- Girish Munshi and Harihar Patel are identified as lawyers requested to serve on the panel.
