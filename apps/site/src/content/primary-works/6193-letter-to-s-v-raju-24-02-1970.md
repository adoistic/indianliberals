## Key points

- The letter is dated 24 February 1970 and addressed to “O RAJU” in Bombay.
- It directs state units to call public meetings.
- The meetings are intended to condemn the railway budget and demand its rejection.
- The recipient is asked to send material concerning a consumers’ organisation in Delhi.
- A handwritten note requests a transcript of Masani’s speech to the Bombay Regional Convention.
- The document is a one-page confirmation copy received on 26 February 1970.
