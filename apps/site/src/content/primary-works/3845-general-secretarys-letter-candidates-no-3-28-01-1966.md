## Key points

- The letter concerns financial assistance for Swatantra Party parliamentary candidates.
- Masani seeks to reassure candidates about a matter of concern to both the party and the recipients.
- A meeting is scheduled in Bhubaneswar on Wednesday, 9 February, from 11 a.m. onward.
- The meeting venue is the State Party Office at Kharavelanagar, Bhubaneswar-1.
- Candidates who cannot afford travel to Bhubaneswar may charge those expenses to constituency-nursing funds made available by the treasurers.
- The letter is signed for M. R. Masani in his absence and notes that it was dictated by him.
