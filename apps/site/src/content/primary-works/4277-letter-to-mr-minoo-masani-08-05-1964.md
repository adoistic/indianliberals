## Key points

- The letter is dated 8 May 1964 and sent from Madras.
- Rajagopalachari addresses M. R. Masani as “My dear Masani.”
- He acknowledges receiving Masani’s letter of 7 May.
- He proposes arriving on Saturday afternoon, 16 May, and returning on Monday afternoon.
- He states that this itinerary is preferable to the alternative plan.
- Masani is identified in the address as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
