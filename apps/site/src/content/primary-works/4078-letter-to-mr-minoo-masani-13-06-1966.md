## Summary

This single-page English express telegram, dated 8 June 1966 and received on 13 June 1966, records A. P. Jain's arrangements for a meeting involving M. P. Masani and M. P. Dahyabhai Patel. Jain reports that the Prime Minister's Secretary has fixed the meeting for Monday, 13 June, at 5 p.m., and asks the recipients to confirm their arrival.

## Key points

- The document is an express telegram rather than a conventional letter.
- It is dated 8 June 1966 and bears a received stamp dated 13 June 1966.
- A. P. Jain is identified as the sender from 53 North Avenue, New Delhi.
- The message is addressed to M. P. Masani and M. P. Dahyabhai Patel in Bombay.
- The Prime Minister's Secretary reportedly fixed a meeting for Monday, 13 June, at 5 p.m.
- The recipients are asked to confirm their arrival.
- Jain says that the Prime Minister's Secretariat is not writing separately and that Prof. Ranga should also be informed.
