## Key points

- Minoo Shroff addresses Mr. N. Dandekar, the Swatantra Party’s General Secretary.
- The letter requests an emergency meeting of the party’s General Council.
- The proposed agenda concerns Mr. Masani’s resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- Shroff calls for an appropriate decision in response to the resignation.
- The stated objective is to close party ranks and resume united work under the leadership of respected party leaders.
- The printed date field is blank, and the document contains handwritten annotations and a signature.
