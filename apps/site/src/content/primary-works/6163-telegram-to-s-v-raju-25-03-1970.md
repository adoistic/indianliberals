## Summary

This single-page English telegram, sent under the name “MASANI” and addressed to Raju in Bombay, concerns railway travel arrangements from New Delhi to Madras. The sender asks the recipient to approach the IAC and secure reservations on trains IC171 and IC172 on the twenty-seventh, referring to arrangements requested by Delhi. The form is an Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department telegram, received in 1970; the exact telegram date is difficult to read.

## Key points

- The document is a one-page Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department telegram.
- The message is in English and is addressed to Raju in Bombay.
- The sender is identified only by the terminal name “MASANI.”
- The message requests railway reservations for the twenty-seventh.
- The trains named are IC171 and IC172, travelling to Madras.
- The sender asks the recipient to approach the IAC.
- The message refers to a request already made by Delhi.
