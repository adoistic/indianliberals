## Key points

- The Swatantra Party general council met amid a dispute over disciplinary action in Gujarat.
- M. R. Masani’s resignation and the possibility of concessions to him were presented as the meeting’s central question.
- The report links the crisis to the expulsion or removal of several Gujarat party figures.
- Masani was described as seeking a firm and uncompromising response to party rebels.
- The article says Rajaji’s role in keeping Masani satisfied was politically significant.
- Questions were raised about whether Rajaji had encouraged a revolt in the Gujarat unit.
- The report records disagreement over the participation of party leaders in the Parliamentary Board and executive.
