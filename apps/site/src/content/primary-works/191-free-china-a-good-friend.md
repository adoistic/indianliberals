## Key points

- Masani endorses the India–Free China Friendship Week being celebrated in New Delhi and elsewhere.
- He argues that short-term and opportunistic considerations threaten the security and stability of the Republic of China.
- The Republic of China is presented as having a strong record of economic and social advancement over the preceding two decades.
- Masani describes the Communist regime on the mainland as being at war with the Chinese people.
- He urges Indian non-official opinion to express solidarity with Free China and reciprocate its friendship toward India.
