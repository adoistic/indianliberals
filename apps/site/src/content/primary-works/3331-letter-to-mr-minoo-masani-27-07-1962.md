## Summary

This one-page English letter, dated July 1962, is from Dr. B. N. Singh, a Lok Sabha member, to M. R. Masani. Singh acknowledges Masani's letter concerning a bank transaction and says he has passed it to Thakur Bateshwar Prasad Singh, whom he identifies as the constituted attorney of Raja Bahadur K. N. Singh. He adds that Thakur B. P. Singh has received a similar letter from Masani.

## Key points

- The letter is addressed to M. R. Masani and concerns a bank transaction.
- B. N. Singh says he has forwarded Masani's letter to Thakur Bateshwar Prasad Singh.
- Thakur Bateshwar Prasad Singh is identified as the constituted attorney of Raja Bahadur K. N. Singh.
- Singh states that he does not know the details of the transaction.
- Because he lacks knowledge of the transaction, Singh declines to advise Thakur B. P. Singh.
- The letter is written on the sender's Calcutta parliamentary letterhead and carries a July 1962 receipt stamp.
