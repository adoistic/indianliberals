## Summary

This untitled letter, dated November 12, 1979, is written by M. R. Masani to the Regional Transport Officer, RTO Recovery Branch, Bombay. Masani refers to the officer's October 26 post-card concerning a vehicle and states that he has asked the relevant person to provide certification that he bought the vehicle from the Swatantra Party.

Masani also supplies the person's direct address: Mr. P. K. Deo, identified as the former Maharaja of Kalahandi, at The Palace, Bhawanipatna, Orissa. The letter is a narrowly administrative piece of correspondence; it does not develop a broader political or economic argument.

## Key points

- The letter is dated November 12, 1979, and addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, RTO Recovery Branch, Bombay.
- Masani responds to a post-card reference dated October 26, 1979.
- He says he has asked the relevant person to provide certification that he purchased a vehicle from the Swatantra Party.
- Masani gives the officer permission to contact Mr. P. K. Deo directly.
- P. K. Deo is identified in the address as the former Maharaja of Kalahandi, residing in Bhawanipatna, Orissa.
- The document is a single-page administrative letter with a handwritten signature.
