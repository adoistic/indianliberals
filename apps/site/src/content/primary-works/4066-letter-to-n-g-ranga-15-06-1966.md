## Summary

This one-page English telegram confirmation, dated 15 June 1966, is sent from Prof. N. G. Ranga in Kuppam, Chittoor District, Andhra, to M. R. Masani of the Swatantra Party in Bombay. It states that the Prime Minister has fixed a meeting for 5 p.m. on the 22nd, as requested by the two correspondents, and confirms that both will meet her.

## Key points

- The document is a telegram or confirmation copy, although it is catalogued provisionally as a letter.
- It is dated 15 June 1966.
- The sender is identified as Prof. N. G. Ranga, MP, from Kuppam, Chittoor District, Andhra.
- The recipient is M. R. Masani of the Swatantra Party, 147 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
- The message announces a meeting with the Prime Minister at 5 p.m. on the 22nd.
- The meeting is described as having been arranged as desired by the correspondents.
- The document confirms that both correspondents will attend the meeting.
