## Summary

These two pages contain the draft minutes of the Swatantra Party Organisation Sub-Committee meeting held on January 30, 1964, in Rajaji's Room at the Madras Woodlands Hotel in Bangalore. Prof. N. G. Ranga chaired the meeting; C. Rajagopalachari, Kamakhya Narain Singh of Ramgarh, and M. R. Masani were also present. The committee confirmed the previous meeting's minutes and reviewed interim arrangements for party office-bearers in Uttar Pradesh, representations concerning the state units in Orissa, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and West Bengal, and recommendations for additions to the General Council from Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan.

## Key points

- The committee confirmed the minutes of its August 3, 1963 meeting in Madras.
- It decided to continue interim arrangements for Uttar Pradesh state office-bearers, with B. P. Singh acting as president and Layaq Singh as general secretary.
- It declined to interfere with decisions of the Orissa State Executive Committee after considering representations from Dr. Krishnamurthy and Mr. Satyanarayan Mishra.
- It discussed organisational difficulties in Gujarat and instructed the Central Office to encourage district-level organisation in Madhya Pradesh and West Bengal.
- It recommended several Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan figures for co-option to the General Council.
- It recorded resignations and recommended appointments to fill vacancies in the Central Organising Committee and party vice-presidency.
- It called for a meeting of the Bihar Swatantra Party legislators to reorganise the state unit and recommend office-bearers and Working Committee members.
