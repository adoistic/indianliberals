## Key points

- One-page typed letter from Minoo Masani to N. Dandekar, dated 27 May 1975.
- Addressee is 'Mr N Dandekar, Express Building, Churchgate, Bombay 400 020'.
- Masani thanks Dandekar for a copy of a note Dandekar had sent to Morarji Desai ('Morarjibhai') on 10 April.
- Masani states he finds himself 'in happy accord' with what Dandekar had written.
- Masani reports having met Morarji at the same time and having urged him to hold on to his fast, being confident he would win.
- Three days later Masani went to congratulate Morarji on the successful end of the fast, described as 'his ordeal'.
- Reference mark 'mrm/cal' at the foot, typed on plain paper without a printed letterhead visible in the rendered scan.
