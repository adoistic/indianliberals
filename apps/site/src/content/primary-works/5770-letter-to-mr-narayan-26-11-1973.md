## Key points

- Requests a ready list of members of the Executive Committee, District Committee, Primary Committee, Regional Council, and National Council.
- Asks for members’ residential addresses and telephone numbers.
- Requests a press announcement covering the party election results and the names of office-bearers and the Executive Committee.
- Seeks a general account of party funds and commitments.
- Proposes a Wednesday meeting of the Joint Secretaries and requests a statement of party membership for Maharashtra.
