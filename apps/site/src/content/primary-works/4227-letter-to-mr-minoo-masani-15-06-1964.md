## Key points

- Rajagopalachari writes directly to Minoo Masani in a collegial, procedural context.
- He is issuing a notice to members of the organisation.
- He is simultaneously informing the President about the situation.
- He states that he has not received communication concerning the State Executive Committee.
- He refers to arranging or convening a meeting and offering Masani support.
- The letter bears the date June 15, without a securely visible year.
