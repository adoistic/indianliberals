## Summary

This single-page office letter, dated 24 July 1963, is addressed to Mr. A. P. Jain at the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office in New Delhi. It acknowledges Jain's request and returns a letter addressed to the Secretary of the Lok Sabha Secretariat, signed by M. R. Masani.

## Key points

- The document is dated 24 July 1963.
- It is addressed to Mr. A. P. Jain.
- Jain's address is given as the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office, 53 North Avenue, New Delhi.
- The letter transmits or returns an enclosed letter addressed to the Secretary, Lok Sabha Secretariat.
- The enclosed letter is stated to have been signed by Mr. Masani.
- The typed authorization identifies M. R. Masani as the signatory, with handwritten initials appearing above it.
