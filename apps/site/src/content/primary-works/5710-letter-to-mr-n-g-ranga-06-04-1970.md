## Key points

- Rajagopalachari acknowledges receipt of Ranga’s letter dated 3 April 1970.
- He notes tension within the party concerning Gujarat.
- He identifies Sri C. C. Desai as the immediate focus of the dispute.
- He expresses admiration for Ranga’s generosity.
- He reports that his health has not returned to normal.
- He describes himself as suffering from considerable dejection.
