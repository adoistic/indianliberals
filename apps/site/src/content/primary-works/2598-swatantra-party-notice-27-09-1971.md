## Key points

- The notice is dated 27 September 1971 and concerns the Swatantra Party's Bombay Region.
- A meeting of the newly elected Executive Committee is scheduled for 30 September 1971 at 6:15 p.m.
- The agenda includes approval of the outgoing committee's minutes and consideration of B. Narayan's application for Office Secretary.
- The committee is to consider forming a Parliamentary Board for the Bombay Region.
- Several items concern party administration, including a Bandra committee vacancy, Matunga subscription arrears, and screening prospective party workers.
- The notice records disputes over Bombay North-West District and Bandra committee elections, as well as M. A. Rizvi's enrolment and later committee elections.
- F. C. Cana signs the notice as General Secretary.
