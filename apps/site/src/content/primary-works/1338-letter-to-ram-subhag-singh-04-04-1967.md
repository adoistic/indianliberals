## Summary

In this letter dated April 4, 1967, M. R. Masani writes to Ram Subhag Singh, Minister for Parliamentary Affairs, regarding the Minister of Education's proposal to constitute a committee to consider several educational matters. Masani refers to a discussion at a meeting of Opposition leaders and proposes seven individuals to represent Opposition groups in the Lok Sabha committee.

## Key points

- The letter is dated April 4, 1967, and is addressed to Dr. Ram Subhag Singh in New Delhi.
- Masani responds to a request for five Opposition representatives on a proposed Lok Sabha committee.
- The committee would consider several educational matters proposed by the Minister of Education.
- Masani reports that the issue was discussed at a meeting of Opposition leaders.
- Seven names are suggested, representing the Swatantra Party, Jana Sangh, DMK, O.P.I., SSP, PSP, and Independents.
- Masani hopes that all seven nominees can be accommodated, with a slight expansion of the committee if necessary.
