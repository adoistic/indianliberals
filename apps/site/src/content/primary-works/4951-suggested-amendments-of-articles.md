## Key points

- The resolution objects to proposed amendments to Article 226 of the Constitution.
- It warns against restricting the jurisdiction of courts of law over matters affecting Indian citizens.
- It specifically defends the writ powers of the High Courts and Supreme Court.
- It presents writ jurisdiction as a safeguard for citizens’ legal and fundamental rights.
- It argues that the proposed changes would undermine the rule of law and basic freedoms.
- It calls for immediate action to mobilize public opinion against the proposal.
