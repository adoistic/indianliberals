## Key points

- The letter is dated 20 March 1975 and is issued from the company's Operations Division in Bombay.
- It concerns jeep bearing registration number MRA 4096.
- The jeep was registered in M. R. Masani's name but had been purchased by the company in May 1967.
- The vehicle was located at Chanda Cement Works in Maharashtra.
- The company confirms that the relevant taxes had been paid from 1967 onward.
- The company asks Masani to sign and return the transfer forms so ownership can be transferred to the company's works.
