## Summary

In this two-page English letter addressed to Morarji Desai, Kusum Kumari Joshi congratulates him on his political success while sharply criticizing the Front alliance and its conduct in Gujarat. She argues that the Front's lack of a coherent programme damaged Indian democracy, and says that Minoo Masani was justified in leaving it. The letter presents the alliance as internally treacherous and electorally confused, while crediting Indira Gandhi and Congress with an effective anti-Congress campaign.

Joshi focuses especially on the Swatantra Party's losses after it joined hands with Congress (Organisation), arguing that the alliance surrendered anti-Congress votes and enabled Indira Gandhi to secure a majority. She accuses the Gujarat Pradesh Congress Committee and H. M. Patel of political duplicity, alleging that Congress publicly supported the alliance while working against its candidates. The closing page shifts briefly to election campaigning and popular betrayal, ending with a list of recipients that includes the All India Swatantra Party, the Gujarat Swatantra Party, the Gujarat Chief Minister, and the president of the G.P.C.C.

## Key points

- Joshi congratulates Morarji Desai but frames the Front's success as politically damaging.
- She argues that the Front lacked a programme and therefore threatened democratic politics.
- The letter defends Minoo Masani's decision to quit the Front.
- It criticizes the alliance between Swatantra and Congress (Organisation) for sacrificing anti-Congress votes.
- Indira Gandhi is credited with benefiting electorally from the opposition's fragmentation.
- The Gujarat Pradesh Congress Committee is accused of publicly supporting the alliance while undermining it privately.
- The letter alleges that H. M. Patel and Congress delayed or manipulated electoral arrangements to protect Swatantra's opponents.
