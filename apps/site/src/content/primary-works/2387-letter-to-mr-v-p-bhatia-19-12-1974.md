## Key points

- Raju acknowledges receipt of Bhatia's letter of December 17, 1974.
- He considers the letter, apart from its final paragraph, irrelevant to the subject of his December 13 correspondence.
- He condemns what he calls a clique that has brought the party into ignominy.
- He defends the continued relevance of the Swatantra Party, founded by C. Rajagopalachari, for people committed to fundamental values.
- He asks Bhatia to restrict future correspondence to the R.T.O. enquiry regarding jeeps once registered in the party's name.
