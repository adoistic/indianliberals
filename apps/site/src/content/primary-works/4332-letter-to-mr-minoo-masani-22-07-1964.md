## Summary

This three-page correspondence bundle from July 1964 contains a letter from C. Rajagopalachari to M. R. Masani, an attached telegram, and a covering letter from the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office signed by A. P. Jain. Rajagopalachari acknowledges Masani’s letter of July 18, explains that the 25 July issue of Swarajya had already been printed and dispatched, and considers writing a brief paragraph for the 1 August issue, although he doubts that readers will still remember the case by then.

The telegram and covering letter clarify that a previous communication contained a typographical error concerning Dr. Husain Zaheer’s position as Director General of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research. Jain apologises for the confusion, describes the mistake as inadvertent, and encloses an article on Lal Bahadur Shastri by Professor and Mrs. Rudolph of Harvard University. The documents record a small but revealing exchange among liberal political and journalistic actors, focused on correcting the public record and coordinating a response through Swarajya.

## Key points

- Rajagopalachari writes to M. R. Masani on 22 July 1964 from Madras.
- The 25 July 1964 issue of Swarajya had already been printed and dispatched when Rajagopalachari received Masani’s letter.
- Rajagopalachari considers preparing a short paragraph for Swarajya’s 1 August issue but doubts the case will still be remembered.
- A telegram corrects a typographical mistake concerning Dr. Husain Zaheer’s office as Director General of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research.
- A. P. Jain apologises for the error and encloses an article on Lal Bahadur Shastri by Professor and Mrs. Rudolph of Harvard University.
- The bundle documents the circulation, correction, and planned publication of information through a political party office and a periodical.
