## Key points

- Masani notes that no party unit has yet responded to the request made in General Secretary's Letter No. 97.
- Each unit is asked to submit a priority list of parliamentary constituencies.
- The list should include reasons why the State Parliamentary Board considers each constituency worthy of inclusion.
- State Parliamentary Boards are asked to meet and approve the draft lists prepared by their office bearers.
- The Central Office requests the material by the middle of May so it can make recommendations to the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The Central Parliamentary Board is scheduled to meet in Madras on June 4.
- Masani warns that delays will postpone consideration of proposals concerning prospective parliamentary candidates.
