## Key points

- The Bharatiya Kranti Dal presents itself as a democratic, anti-exploitation party influenced broadly by Mahatma Gandhi.
- Its economic programme combines cooperative smallholder agriculture with decentralized, labour-intensive industry.
- Capital-intensive activities required for defence and research are assigned to State ownership or control.
- The aims include nationalism, secularism, socialism, international fellowship, self-reliance, non-discrimination, and peaceful international dispute resolution.
- Membership is open from age eighteen, subject to political, legal, and organisational conditions.
- Elective office requires additional party service, abstention from intoxicants, and other eligibility requirements.
- The party is organised through constituency, district or city, state, and national councils and executives.
- The constitution regulates elections, finances, meetings, parliamentary boards, amendments, vacancies, discipline, and the authority of the National Executive.
