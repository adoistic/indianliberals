## Key points

- The letter is addressed to Shri K. M. Munshi and dated 25 September 1959.
- The sender acknowledges Munshi’s letter of 21 September.
- A cheque for Rs. 2,500 was forwarded to Raghavendra Pratap Singh in Lucknow.
- The payment was made at the instance of Krishnaswamy.
- The sender urges regional organisers to raise funds to cover most of their monthly expenses.
- The Bombay Party chest is described as nearly exhausted.
- The sender asks Munshi to copy the Bombay office on letters concerning his Party activities.
