## Summary

In this one-page letter dated May 30, 1967, M. R. Masani writes to Cooverji H. Bhabha about a possible visit to Orissa. Masani reports that R. N. Singh Deo, the state's Chief Minister, is expected to be in Bhubaneshwar on June 24 and 25, and says he has written to Singh Deo to discuss the proposed visit.

## Key points

- The letter is dated May 30, 1967.
- M. R. Masani addresses Cooverji H. Bhabha as "My dear Cooverji."
- Masani refers to a conversation with Bhabha on May 29.
- R. N. Singh Deo is identified as the Chief Minister of Orissa.
- Singh Deo is expected to be in Bhubaneshwar on June 24 and 25.
- Masani has written to Singh Deo regarding the possibility of Bhabha visiting Orissa.
- Masani asks Bhabha to keep those dates free until he receives Singh Deo's response.
