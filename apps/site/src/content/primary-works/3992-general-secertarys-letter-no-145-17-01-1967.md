## Summary

Dated 17 January 1967, this administrative letter from Swatantra Party General Secretary M. R. Masani announces the completion of his organisational and campaign tour of North India and plans for a similar tour of the South. Masani states that he will be based in Rajkot from 5 February until the elections are over, with the party’s Central Office operating there with a skeleton staff.

## Key points

- M. R. Masani reports completing an organisational and campaign tour of North India.
- He plans to complete a similar tour of South India by the end of January 1967.
- From 5 February, Masani will work from Rajkot until the elections conclude.
- The Central Office will temporarily function from Rajkot with a skeleton staff.
- Routine matters will continue to be handled at the National Headquarters in Bombay.
- Major P. H. Honawar is identified as being in charge of routine matters at the Bombay headquarters.
- The Central Office is scheduled to return to Bombay from 25 February.
