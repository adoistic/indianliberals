## Key points

- The letter is dated 17 September 1959 and addressed from Bombay.
- It acknowledges N. C. Mishra's letter of 4 August concerning the formation of the Swatantra Party.
- M. R. Masani's appreciation for Mishra's interest is conveyed through his secretary, Miss B. S. Mirza.
- Masani is reported to be in the United Kingdom at the time of writing.
- Copies of Masani's opening remarks at the party's preparatory convention and the party's Statement of Principles are being mailed to Mishra.
- The letter's purpose is correspondence and transmission of party-related materials rather than substantive discussion of policy.
