## Key points

- Personal thank-you letter from Minoo Masani to Nani A. Palkhivala dated 3rd March 1971.
- Palkhivala had travelled to Rajkot to speak publicly on Masani's behalf.
- Masani reports that Palkhivala's talk made "a deep impression" on Rajkot's intelligentsia and was still being discussed the following day.
- Masani calls the visit "a great shot in the arm" for his side, prestige-wise — a political-campaign register consistent with the March 1971 general-election period.
- Nargish (Palkhivala's wife) accompanied Masani around the local bazaars and is separately thanked.
- The letter encloses five photographic snaps — of Palkhivala at the meeting and of Nargish on Dharmendra Road.
- Addressed to Palkhivala at Bombay House, Bruce Street, Fort, Bombay 1; typist mark "MRM/pav" at the foot.
