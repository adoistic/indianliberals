## Summary

This single-page institutional document is a membership directory for The Foreign Correspondents’ Association of India, issued in New Delhi for February 1960. It identifies the association’s President, Hans J. Bargman, and Vice-President & Secretary, Vassily V. Kondrashov, followed by a roster of foreign correspondents and news professionals working in India.

## Key points

- The document records the association’s membership as of February 1960.
- It lists Hans J. Bargman as President and Vassily V. Kondrashov as Vice-President & Secretary.
- Members are associated with international newspapers, news agencies, radio and television services, and wire services.
- The directory provides Delhi addresses and telephone numbers for members.
- The listed organisations include Reuters, Associated Press of America, Agence France Presse, the New York Times, the Times, the BBC, and several national news agencies.
- Handwritten annotations and corrections appear throughout the page, including alterations to some names, affiliations, and contact details.
