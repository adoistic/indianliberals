## Key points

- Masani acknowledges receipt of Narayan’s letter of June 12.
- He accepts the invitation to join the National Executive Council of the Citizens for Democracy.
- He agrees to attend the meeting in Bombay on Saturday, July 13, at 9:30 a.m.
- He indicates that his attendance is essential to the meeting.
- The letter is dated June 18, 1974, and references file number REF: LSP:7:196.
