## Key points

- The letter is dated 9 April 1968 and is sent by N. G. Ranga from New Delhi to M. R. Masani.
- Ranga asks Masani to attend the Central Parliamentary Board and National Executive meetings in Delhi on 13–14 April.
- He describes the meetings as a possible turning point for the Swatantra Party in Gujarat and other states.
- The letter mentions the expected or possible attendance of Bhaikaka, H. M. Patel, and Jaideepsinghji of Baria.
- Ranga argues that Masani's views, advice, and guidance are necessary to resolve the situation in Gujarat.
- The appeal treats Masani's resignation as an obstacle to attendance and explicitly invites him to come despite it.
