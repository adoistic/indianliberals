## Summary

This 19 September 1969 letter from M. R. Masani to Sardarni Nirlep Kaur addresses her reported proceedings in the Akali Dal. Masani expresses regret that she is no longer with the Swatantra Party, welcomes her possible return, and proposes meeting in Delhi during his visit from 2 to 4 October to discuss the matter. The tone is personal, conciliatory, and explicitly political.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 19 September 1969 and is signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani writes to Sardarni Nirlep Kaur concerning press reports about Akali Dal proceedings against her.
- He says he has regretted her absence from the Swatantra Party.
- He welcomes her return to the party and asks her to let him know if she decides to rejoin.
- He offers to meet her briefly in Delhi between 2 and 4 October to discuss the situation.
- The accompanying clippings report the formation of a rival Akali Dal and disciplinary action against Nirlep Kaur.
- Pages 3 and 4 contain additional typescript material, including recruitment-agency fee and expense terms, apparently unrelated to the letter.
