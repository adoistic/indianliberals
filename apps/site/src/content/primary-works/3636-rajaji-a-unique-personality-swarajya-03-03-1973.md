This rendered section of SWARAJYA, March 3, 1973, centers on C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji), presenting him through biographical tribute, reflections on his political independence, and an account of his contribution to public life. The issue also includes a short news section on opposition to food rationing and the proposed state takeover of wholesale grain trade in Punjab, Haryana, and Gujarat. The rendered pages show a liberal concern with political dissent, administrative competence, voluntary social organization, and resistance to unnecessary state control.
