## Key points

- Masani writes to A. P. Jain on November 12, 1964.
- He confirms that he will reach Delhi from Patna on the afternoon of November 15.
- He asks Jain to come to his house with relevant papers before the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group meets that evening.
- Masani says that material has been sent to all Members of Parliament belonging to the party.
- The enclosed material includes a background paper on “the BOMB.”
- The second item is N. A. Palkhivala’s booklet on legislative privilege.
