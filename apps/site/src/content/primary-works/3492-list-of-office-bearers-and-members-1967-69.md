## Key points

- The document is an organisational list for the Uttar Pradesh Swatantra Party.
- Bhanu Pratap Singh is listed as president.
- Vahidur Rahman and G. D. Paliwal are listed as vice presidents.
- Layaq Singh is listed as general secretary.
- Baldeo Singh and Chaitanya Raj Singh are listed as joint secretaries.
- Ram Gopal Gupta is listed as treasurer.
- The members' addresses span Lucknow and districts including Kanpur, Gonda, Azamgarh, Behraich, Agra, Moradabad, Gorakhpur, Bijnor, Faizabad, Shahjahanpur, Ballia, Varanasi, and Jalaun.
