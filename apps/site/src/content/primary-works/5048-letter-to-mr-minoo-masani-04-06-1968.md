## Key points

- The letter is dated 4 June 1968 and was written from Bangalore by V. T. Sreenivasan.
- Sreenivasan objects to what he describes as M. R. Masani's shabby treatment at General Council meetings in Madras.
- He criticises Rajaji for giving the issue a wrong turn by quibbling over the word “pledge.”
- The writer presents his intervention as an effort to keep the public record straight for Masani and the Party.
- He says an article on the matter will appear in the next issue of “Mysindia.”
- Handwritten notes record that a copy was sent to S. V. Raju and identify the article as “Observation.”
