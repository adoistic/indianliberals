## Key points

- The letter is dated January 2, 1970.
- M. R. Masani addresses Col. H. R. Pasricha.
- Masani thanks Pasricha and colleagues from Delhi for supporting him in Madras.
- He says he could not meet the other Delhi colleagues because of lack of time.
- He asks Pasricha to convey his grateful thanks to all of them.
- He gives particular thanks to the General Secretary, Mr. M. R. Arya.
- The sheet is marked “PERSONAL” and has no printed title or publication details.
