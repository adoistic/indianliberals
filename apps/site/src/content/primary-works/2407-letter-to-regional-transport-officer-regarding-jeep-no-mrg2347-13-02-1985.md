## Key points

- The letter is addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, Bombay.
- Masani refers to repeated requests to correct the recipient's records.
- He returns a bill dated 1 February 1985 concerning car number MRG-2347.
- He states that he neither owns nor possesses the vehicle identified in the bill.
