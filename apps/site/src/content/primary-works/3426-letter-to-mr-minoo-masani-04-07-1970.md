## Key points

- Rajagopalachari sends Masani an advance galley of an article intended for Swarajya’s 4 July 1970 issue.
- The letter is marked “Strictly Confidential” and “Express Letter.”
- Rajagopalachari asks Masani to go through the article.
- He refers to Masani’s carefully constructed draft manifesto for the Congress (O) party.
- He speculates that the draft may duplicate Nijalingappa’s speech at an A.I.C.C. meeting.
- The article and manifesto referred to in the letter are not included in the rendered page.
