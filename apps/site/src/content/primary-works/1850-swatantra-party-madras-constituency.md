## Key points

- The report lists 254 Madras-area contacts connected with the Swatantra Party.
- Entries record membership interest, local organising, donations, propaganda work, and requests for party information.
- The party's support base included merchants, agriculturalists, landowners, lawyers, professionals, students, workers, and retired officials.
- Several entries describe the formation or proposed formation of local Swatantra Party branches and committees.
- The report notes opposition to Congress land reforms and support for agricultural and commercial interests.
- Contacts offered practical services including canvassing, collecting funds, distributing pamphlets, arranging meetings, and recruiting members.
- The document records support for Rajaji's leadership and for building the party as an opposition force.
