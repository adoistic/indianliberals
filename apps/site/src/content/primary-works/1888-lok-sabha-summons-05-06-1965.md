## Key points

- The document is a formal Lok Sabha summons dated June 5, 1965.
- It is addressed to Shri M. R. Masani.
- The summons invokes clause (1) of Article 85 of the Constitution.
- The President directs that a Lok Sabha session be held at New Delhi.
- The session is scheduled to commence on Monday, August 16, 1965, at 11 A.M.
- The document appears in parallel English and Hindi versions.
- S. L. Shakdher signs as Secretary by order of the President.
