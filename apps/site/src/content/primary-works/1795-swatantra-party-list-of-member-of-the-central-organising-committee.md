## Summary

This one-page English organizational document, issued under the Swatantra Party, lists 13 members of the party's Central Organising Committee. It is an administrative roster rather than an argumentative or programmatic text, and records members' names alongside parliamentary positions, addresses, and other identifying details where available.

The visible list includes C. Rajagopalachari, K. M. Munshi, H. M. Patel, and other party figures and associates located in cities including New Delhi, Bombay, Calcutta, and Madras. The page does not print a publication date, publisher, author, or explanatory note about the committee's responsibilities.
