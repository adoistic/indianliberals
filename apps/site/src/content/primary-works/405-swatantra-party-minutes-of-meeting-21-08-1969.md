## Key points

- The Executive Committee meeting took place on 21 August 1969 at the Swatantra Party room in Central Hall, New Delhi. The recent presidential election and the political situation arising from it were discussed. Y. G. Gowd and K. L. Kaushik were authorised to advise the party leader on speakers for Private Members' business. N. Dandekar reported that he could not attend meetings of the Consultative Committee. The committee considered nominating a replacement for Dandekar in the relevant committee.
- The party audit for the year ending March 1969 was to be undertaken by the auditor of C. C. Desai's company.
