## Key points

- The clipping addresses safeguards against future Watergate-like political scandals.
- Senator Sam Ervin is presented as endorsing Josiah Gilbert Holland’s poem as a guide to public conduct.
- The poem calls for strong minds, courage, faith, readiness to act, and personal honor in public officials.
- It warns against the corrupting effects of office and the spoils associated with it.
- The poem praises people who can resist demagogues and their flattering rhetoric.
