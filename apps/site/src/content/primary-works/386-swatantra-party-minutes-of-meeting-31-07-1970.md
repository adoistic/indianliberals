## Summary

This two-page English-language office record contains minutes from two Swatantra Party parliamentary meetings held in July 1970. The July 31 meeting of the party's Executive Committee in Parliament assigned speakers to the Privy Purses Bill, Fourth Plan, disapproval of the Delhi University Ordinance, Delhi cooperative societies legislation, and other bills. It also records support for J. B. Kripalani's bill to abolish decorations and awards, reservations about bills concerning employment rights, nominations to the Select Committee on the Central Sales Tax Bill, and support for a traders' hartal seeking discussion of cotton nationalisation and related issues.

The July 27 General Body meeting recorded the attendance of 28 members and communicated the Executive Committee's decision concerning proposed no-confidence motions by the Communist Party of India (Marxist) in West Bengal and Kerala. N. G. Ranga gave an account of developments leading to a proposed alliance of three parties, followed by Minoo Masani's detailed report on negotiations. The document is an internal record of parliamentary coordination, legislative positioning, and alliance-building rather than a sustained statement of political doctrine.

## Key points

- The July 31 Executive Committee meeting allocated Swatantra Party speakers to several parliamentary bills and motions.
- Prof. M. R. Masani and Prof. N. G. Ranga were assigned twenty minutes each on the Fourth Plan.
- The committee supported abolition of decorations and awards in J. B. Kripalani's bill.
- The committee expressed concern that bills seeking employment rights might create a dangerous demand and expose the party to opposition.
- The committee supported a traders' hartal concerning cotton nationalisation and requested an adjournment for discussion.
- The July 27 General Body meeting discussed proposed no-confidence motions in West Bengal and Kerala.
- N. G. Ranga and Minoo Masani reported on negotiations concerning a proposed alliance of three parties.
