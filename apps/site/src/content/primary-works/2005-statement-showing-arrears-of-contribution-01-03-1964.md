## Key points

- The statement is dated 1 March 1964.
- It itemises contribution arrears for 18 Lok Sabha members.
- It itemises contribution arrears for eight Rajya Sabha members.
- The Lok Sabha subtotal is recorded as Rs. 5785-00.
- The Rajya Sabha subtotal is recorded as Rs. 3975-00.
- The grand total of arrears is recorded as Rs. 9760-00.
