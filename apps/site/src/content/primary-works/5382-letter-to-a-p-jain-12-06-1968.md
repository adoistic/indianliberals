## Key points

- The letter is dated 12 June 1968 and is addressed to A. P. Jain.
- Masani acknowledges Jain's letter of 6 June and its enclosures.
- The subject is Rajaji's views on a possible move closer to the B.K.D.
- Masani states that he does not know Rajaji's position on the proposed move.
- Masani believes B. P. Singh would have acted with Rajaji's approval if he took steps in that direction.
