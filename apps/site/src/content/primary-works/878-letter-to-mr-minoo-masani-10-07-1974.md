## Key points

- The letter is dated 10 July 1974 and was written from Moradabad by Om Praksh Pathik.
- Pathik addresses Minoo Masani as a respected senior colleague.
- He reports attending a Delhi meeting of the Swatantra Party General Council held on 1 June 1974.
- According to Pathik, most members at that meeting opposed dissolving the party, while H. K. Pathak and some others supported dissolution.
- He refers to a forthcoming Uttar Pradesh State Council meeting in Lucknow on 14 July.
- He asks Masani to write letters and exert influence in favour of the suggestions made by Uttar Pradesh state members.
- He anticipates that most Uttar Pradesh delegates will oppose the motion to dissolve the party at the State Council meeting and the subsequent convention in Delhi.
