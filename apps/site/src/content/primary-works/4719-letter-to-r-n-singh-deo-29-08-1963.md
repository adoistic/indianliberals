## Key points

- The letter is dated 29 August 1963 and is signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani addresses Shri R. N. Singh Deo, identified as President of the Orissa Swatantra Party and an M.L.A.
- Masani says that C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji) received a letter from Smt. Vasanta Subramaniam.
- Subramaniam is described as a constructive worker of the Gandhian persuasion.
- Masani forwards a copy because he thinks Singh Deo may be interested in seeing it.
- The enclosed letter itself is not included in the rendered page.
