## Summary

This four-page excerpt from the 23 April 1973 issue of INSIGHT contains political commentary on the Swatantra Party's national convention, the Congress's internal socialist pressures, and the prospects for government intervention in food markets. The issue's editorial perspective favors a constructive liberal opposition: it calls for constitutional restraint, private initiative alongside selected public-sector reform, agricultural measures benefiting marginal farmers, and policies that distinguish the Swatantra Party from both Congress centralisation and organised labour politics.

The visible pages also examine the Wheat Take Over and its likely effects on procurement, prices, hoarding, wholesalers, and consumers. A separate column, “War of the Forums,” discusses factional conflict within and around the Congress Socialist Forum, arguing that internal divisions and Communist influence have shaped political strategy.
