## Key points

- The letter is addressed to C. Rajagopalachari, referred to as “Rajaji.”
- It reports a unanimous birthday resolution passed by Swatantra Party workers in the Bombay Region.
- The resolution pledges dedication to the cause of Swatantra.
- It calls for persistent efforts to drive “the invaders” out of the country.
- The resolution explicitly connects this political effort with strengthening individual freedom.
- K. S. Srinivasan signs as office secretary.
- The body date reads 8 December 1962, but the top-right date appears to read 8 December 1966.
