## Key points

- The document is an extract from draft minutes of an Organisation Sub-Committee meeting.
- The meeting was held on Wednesday, November 7, at 9 a.m., at 40, Canning Lane, New Delhi.
- The recorded agenda concerns party organisation in Bihar.
- The committee deferred action on developments related to a Madras resolution.
- The deferral was pending the return and communication of the President of the State Unit.
- A further meeting in Madras was proposed to review developments since the July 22 resolution.
