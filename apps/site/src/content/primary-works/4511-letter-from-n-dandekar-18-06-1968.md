## Key points

- The document is a June 18, 1968 letter from N. Dandekar to Bhagwanjibhai Patel.
- The National Executive considered a committee report concerning allegations of anti-Party activity.
- The Gujarat State Executive’s comments were also taken into account.
- Patel was found to have worked against the Swatantra Party’s candidate in the Rajkot Parliamentary Constituency in the 1967 general election.
- The National Executive imposed a two-year debarment from holding any Party office.
- The order took immediate effect and was copied to H. M. Patel and Ratilal Ukabhai Patel.
