## Key points

- Shroff sends New Year greetings to Minoo Masani and conveys good wishes to Professor Dandekar.
- The letter discusses the possible limits of India’s planning programme.
- Shroff argues that apparent concessions should be treated cautiously.
- It refers to taxation policies and the relationship between public cooperation and policy implementation.
- The writer expresses concern about a new price rise and the broader economic consequences.
