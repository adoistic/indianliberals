## Summary

This letter, dated December 20, 1963, is from C. Rajagopalachari in Madras to M. R. Masani, M.P., in New Delhi. Rajagopalachari asks whether Masani will be unable to continue sending money from the Central Office budget to his Madras office, as had been done previously.

## Key points

- The letter is dated December 20, 1963.
- It was written by C. Rajagopalachari from 60 Bazullah Road, Madras-1.
- The recipient is Mr. M. R. Masani, M.P., in New Delhi.
- Rajagopalachari reports that the Madras office's bank account is nearly exhausted.
- The remaining balance is stated as Rs. 140.
- The letter concerns the continuation of transfers from the Central Office budget.
