## Summary

This one-page letter, dated February 19, 1968, was sent from Raj Bhavan, Calcutta, on the letterhead of the Governor of West Bengal to Shri M. R. Masani, M.P. The sender thanks Masani for a kind telegram and its expressed sentiments, and acknowledges the goodwill and recognition received from an esteemed leader.

## Key points

- The letter is dated February 19, 1968, and issued from Raj Bhavan, Calcutta.
- It responds appreciatively to a telegram from M. R. Masani.
- The sender describes the country as passing through a very difficult period.
- The letter calls for courage, resolution, and continued work for the good of the country.
- The sender frames these responsibilities as part of discharging constitutional obligations.
- The handwritten signature is not securely legible; the printed letterhead identifies the office as Governor of West Bengal.
