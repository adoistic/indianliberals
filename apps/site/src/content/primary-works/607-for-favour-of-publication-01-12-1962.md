summary: |-
  This press statement, issued from Bombay on December 1 and signed by M. R. Masani as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party, criticizes the Prime Minister's stated basis for negotiations with Pakistan over Kashmir. Masani argues that negotiations should not be subject to preconditions that preserve the status quo, warning that the government's position could dash hopes of Indo-Pakistani friendship. The statement also records his criticism of the cease-fire following the Chinese invasion, which he says has weakened India's will to continue resisting rather than securing the national objective.
---

## Key points

- Masani describes the Prime Minister's position on negotiations with Pakistan over Kashmir as “most unfortunate.”
- He argues that negotiations cannot be meaningful if the government rules out any change in the status quo in Kashmir.
- The statement portrays the cease-fire after the Chinese invasion as more damaging than the Chinese army's military advance.
- It accuses the Indian Government of allowing Chinese troops to withdraw unmolested and failing to reflect public and military resolve.
- It calls for the Chinese invasion to be referred to the United Nations so that China may be declared an aggressor.
- It urges that military and material assistance to India be provided under the banner of the United Nations.
- It links settlement of the Kashmir dispute with Pakistan to the possibility of a common defence of the subcontinent.
