## Summary

In this letter dated September 11, 1974, S. V. Raju replies to M. D. Joseph, explaining that Minoo Masani is abroad and expected back by the middle of the following month. Raju refers Joseph to an enclosed article he wrote in Freedom First for an account of what happened at the Convention.

Raju also advises Joseph, who is associated with the Swatantra Party in Kerala, to contact C. N. Sudhan in Kottayam. He says Sudhan understands their position because they discussed it during Sudhan’s recent visit to Bombay. The document is a brief piece of political correspondence rather than a substantive policy argument; the enclosed Freedom First article is not included in the rendered page.

## Key points

- The letter is dated September 11, 1974, and was sent by S. V. Raju from Bombay.
- Raju tells M. D. Joseph that Minoo Masani is abroad and expected to return by the middle of the following month.
- Raju encloses an article he wrote in Freedom First describing events at a Convention.
- He recommends that Joseph contact C. N. Sudhan in Kottayam regarding matters in Kerala.
- Raju states that Sudhan understands their position because of a recent discussion in Bombay.
- The referenced Freedom First article is not present in the rendered document.
