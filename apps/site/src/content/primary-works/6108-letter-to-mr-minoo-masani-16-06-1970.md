## Summary

This one-page letter from S. V. Raju, dated June 16, 1970, forwards an attached draft reply prepared for consideration as being from Dr. Cooper. Raju says that the matter had already been taken up with Mr. Masani, who had not replied, and describes Dr. Masani as appearing genuine.

Raju recommends issuing Masani a Life Membership card and arranging for the workers he has enrolled, with the immediate aim of initiating some form of organisation in Asaba District. The attachment itself is not included in the rendered document, so the letter provides only the covering recommendation and not the substance of the draft reply.

## Key points

- The letter is dated June 16, 1970 and signed by S. V. Raju.
- Raju forwards an attached draft reply for consideration as being from Dr. Cooper.
- The matter had previously been taken up with Mr. Masani, who had not replied.
- Raju regards Dr. Masani as genuine.
- He recommends issuing Masani a Life Membership card and supporting the workers he has enrolled.
- The proposed action is intended to help initiate an organisation in Asaba District.
