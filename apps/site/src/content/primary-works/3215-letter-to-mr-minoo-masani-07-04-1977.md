## Summary

This one-page letter, dated April 7, 1977, is from Girish Munshi at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan to Minoo Masani. Munshi thanks Masani for accepting an appointment as arbitrator in a dispute involving Parmanandji, K. H. Subramanian, L. R. Sampat, and S. V. Raju. The letter says that the relevant correspondence is enclosed for Masani's study.

## Key points

- The letter is dated April 7, 1977, and is written from Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan in Bombay.
- Girish Munshi addresses Minoo Masani and thanks him for accepting the role of arbitrator.
- The arbitration concerns a dispute involving Parmanandji, K. H. Subramanian, L. R. Sampat, and S. V. Raju.
- Munshi encloses an exchange of correspondence for Masani to study before the hearing.
- The enclosures also include the party's constitution and the Swatantra Party's 21 principles.
- The writer awaits Masani's communication regarding the date of the hearing.
