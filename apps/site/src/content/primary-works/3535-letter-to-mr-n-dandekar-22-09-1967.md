## Key points

- The document is a confidential office letter dated September 22, 1967.
- It is addressed to N. Dandekar at the Indian Express Building, Churchgate, Bombay 1.
- Cooper recalls a conversation at Dhananjay Khatau's house before Khatau left for Europe.
- The correspondence concerns a list of political contributions collected during the last elections.
- Cooper states that he sent the list to Khatau through Raju.
- Dandekar is asked to follow up on collecting funds for “the Party.”
- A note beneath the signature says the letter was dictated on September 6 and signed in Cooper's absence.
