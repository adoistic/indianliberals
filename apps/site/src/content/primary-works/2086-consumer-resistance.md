## Key points

- Consumer resistance is described as ineffective because consumers have few alternative products to choose from.
- The consumer movement is characterized as being in its infancy and unable to organize across the country.
- The communist method is presented as state control over the economy intended to bring down prices.
- Communist economies are said to experience suppressed inflation, shortages, queues, and accumulation of bank balances.
- The page criticizes the concentration of investment in heavy industries as a cause of food and consumer-goods shortages.
- Centrally planned prices and official cost-of-living calculations are portrayed as disconnected from actual availability and living conditions.
- Dual pricing is criticized for giving communist-party members privileged access to subsidized local and imported goods.
