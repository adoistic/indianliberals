## Key points

- Raju writes to Minoo Masani on February 21, 1970. An airfreight packet contains C. C. Desai's letter and enclosures, letters to Lobo Prabhu and George Fernandes, and press clippings. Raju encloses draft minutes of the Central Parliamentary Board and National Executive. He asks Masani to finalise the minutes promptly so that organisational action can be taken. Raju refers to Professor Shah's expected return to Bombay by the morning flight of February 25. He disputes part of a Times of India editorial and wants the public record corrected concerning C. C. Desai's request.
- A draft letter to the editor is enclosed for use if Masani considers it necessary.
