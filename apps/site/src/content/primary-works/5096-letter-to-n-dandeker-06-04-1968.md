## Key points

- P. K. Deo writes to the Swatantra Party's General Secretary, N. Dandeker, M.P.
- The letter is dated 6 April 1968.
- Deo requests an emergency meeting of the Party's General Council.
- The proposed meeting would address Mr. Mesani's resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- The stated purpose is to enable the Party to close ranks and work unitedly under the leadership of its respected leaders.
