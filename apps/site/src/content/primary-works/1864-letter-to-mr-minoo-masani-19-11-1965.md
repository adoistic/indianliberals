## Summary

This single-page English letter, dated 19 November 1965, was issued from the Indian Parliamentary & Scientific Committee at 52-B, Parliament House, New Delhi. Addressed to Shri M. R. Masani, it asks him to renew his membership of the committee by paying the annual subscription of Rs. 10 for 1965–66.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 19 November 1965.
- It is issued on the letterhead of the Indian Parliamentary & Scientific Committee.
- The recipient is Shri M. R. Masani, M.P., of 57, Lodi Estate, New Delhi.
- The committee requests renewal of Masani's membership for 1965–66.
- The annual subscription requested is Rs. 10.
- Payment could be made in cash or by cheque in Room No. 44, Parliament House, on working days.
- The printed sender is F. N. Singh, Secretary; handwritten annotations and signatures appear above the typed text.
