## Summary

This one-page office memo, dated 28 November 1974, is from S. V. Raju to M. R. Masani concerning a forthcoming visit to Madras. Raju asks Masani to request Mr. Chitranarayan to determine the current value of shares in Indian Overseas Bank Limited.

The memo states that the Party holds 16 shares, each with a face value of Rs. 100, and that these shares are the remaining portion of an estate bequeathed to the Party by a zamindar who died by suicide. The document records a practical inquiry about the value and disposition of property held by the Party; it does not develop a broader argument.

## Key points

- The memo is dated 28 November 1974 and was sent from Bombay.
- S. V. Raju writes to M. R. Masani about a visit to Madras.
- Raju asks that Mr. Chitranarayan find the current value of Indian Overseas Bank Limited shares.
- The Party holds 16 shares with a face value of Rs. 100 each.
- The shares are described as the remaining part of an estate bequeathed to the Party by a zamindar who committed suicide.
