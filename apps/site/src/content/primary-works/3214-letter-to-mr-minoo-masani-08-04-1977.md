## Key points

- The letter is dated April 8, 1977, and was sent from Bombay.
- It is addressed to M. R. Masani.
- The signatories state that Masani has agreed to respond to their request for arbitration.
- Girish Munshi is said to have passed the relevant papers to Masani.
- The signatories look forward to appointing a meeting with Masani.
- The letter is signed by K. H. Subramanian, L. R. Sampat, and S. V. Raju.
