## Key points

- The letter is dated 11 March 1963 and is written from 68 Marine Drive, Bombay 1.
- Patel acknowledges receipt of a letter dated 7 March and enclosed copies of speeches.
- The speeches were delivered during the current session of the Rajya Sabha.
- Patel expresses particular appreciation for the speaker’s treatment of the budget.
- The letter also highlights discussion of the budget’s origins, government measures, and proposed responses.
