## Key points

- The telegram is sent by Jayaprakash from Patna to Minoo Masani in Bombay.
- Jayaprakash expresses surprise and concern that Masani has not replied to two earlier telegrams.
- Masani, Hanumant Rao, or a substitute is asked to reach Patna on the morning of June 17.
- The sender requests that Masani send the world reaction to Jayaprakash's letter via the incoming liner.
- The sender also invites Masani to come personally.
- The document provides no unambiguous year.
