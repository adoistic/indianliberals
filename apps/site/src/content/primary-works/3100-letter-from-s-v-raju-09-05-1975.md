## Summary

This seven-page file contains a series of near-identical administrative letters dated May 9, 1975, signed by S. V. Raju as Joint Secretary of the Swatantra Party. Addressed to organisations and institutions in India and abroad, the letters acknowledge the recipients' periodic sending of literature and notify them of a change of address for future correspondence.

## Key points

- All seven visible letters are dated May 9, 1975.
- S. V. Raju signs each letter as Joint Secretary.
- The letters direct future material to Swatantra Party National Headquarters at L/9/72, Tilaknagar, Bombay 89.
- The correspondence acknowledges recipients for sending literature from time to time.
- Recipients include news organisations, governmental and diplomatic institutions, and an industrial organisation.
- The file has no printed title, formal letterhead, publisher line, or table of contents.
