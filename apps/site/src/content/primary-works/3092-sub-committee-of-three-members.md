## Summary

This single-page English agenda lays out party and parliamentary business for an unidentified meeting. It calls for a three-member sub-committee to examine the Home Ministry's note on defections and the suggestions made at the last Defection Committee meeting, along with nominations to committees connected with the parliamentary session in Bangalore and Rajya Sabha matters.

## Key points

- Proposes appointing a three-member sub-committee to examine the implications of suggestions on defections.
- Lists committee nominations relating to the parliamentary session in Bangalore and the Rajya Sabha.
- Records concerns about reported moves by some members to form a Bhim Sena along the lines of the Soshit Dal.
- Includes Mr. Imam's letter and the voting outcome for elections to the Estimates Committee.
- Notes Mr. Nanji Gowder's signature on a memorandum concerning the Banking Bill.
- Assigns speakers for legislative business including the Public Provident Fund Bill, state budgets, the Civil Defence Bill, and amendments under the Unlawful Activities Prevention Act.
