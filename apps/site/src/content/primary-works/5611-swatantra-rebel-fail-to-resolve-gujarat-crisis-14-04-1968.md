## Key points

- The Swatantra Party's Central Parliamentary Board met in New Delhi to address a Gujarat electoral crisis.
- The crisis followed the defeat of the party's official Rajya Sabha candidate from Gujarat.
- Party leaders disagreed over disciplinary action against Gujarat MLAs who allegedly violated the party mandate.
- M. R. Masani opposed immediate disciplinary action and urged compromise.
- Three proposals included resignation and fresh state elections, disciplinary action against rebels, or action only against those who supported M. N. Mahida.
- Mahida reportedly demanded an apology from legislators who defied the party mandate before they could be pardoned.
- The Board was also considering political developments in West Bengal, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh and Punjab.
