## Summary

This one-page Marathi political leaflet presents an appeal attributed in its title to George Fernandes, addressed to Mumbai's working class: “कारागृहाच्या गजांआडून जोर्ज फर्नांडिस यांचे मुंबईतील कामगार वर्गाला कळकळीचे आवाहन” (“From behind prison bars, George Fernandes's heartfelt appeal to Mumbai's working class”). The text appears to combine a short handwritten message with a printed electoral panel for the Greater Mumbai municipal constituency. It urges workers to support a slate of candidates identified by ward or electoral district.

## Key points

- The leaflet is written and printed in Marathi and is directed to Mumbai's working class.
- Its title attributes the appeal to George Fernandes and frames it as issued from prison.
- The printed electoral section is headed “बृहन्मुंबईतील जनता उमेदवार” (people's candidates in Greater Mumbai).
- Candidates are listed for South Mumbai, Central-South Mumbai, Central-North Mumbai, Western Mumbai, Eastern Mumbai, and North Mumbai.
- The visible candidate names include Ratansinh Rajda, B. C. Kambale, Ahilya Rangnekar, Ram Jethmalani, Dr. Subrahmanyam Swami, and Mrunal Gore.
- The bottom imprint identifies B. R. Dandwate and Hind Mazdoor Panchayat, Mumbai, as publisher and names Sapna Printers in Mahim as printer.
- No publication year or conventional author byline is visible.
