## Key points

- The letter is dated July 16, 1974.
- It is addressed to Mr. Madhu Mehta in Bombay.
- Mehta’s resignation letter was dated July 11, 1974.
- The resignation concerned his position as President of the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra.
- The party’s Executive Committee had met the previous day.
- The committee decided to postpone its decision on the resignation.
