## Summary

This one-page letter from M. R. Masani, dated January 17, 1964, is addressed to Mr. Gopalji, a tobacco merchant in Dhari, Gujarat. Masani acknowledges having seen Gopalji’s letter to T. T. Krishnamachari and reports that the matter is being examined by the Parliamentary Group office.

## Key points

- The document is a brief typed office letter dated January 17, 1964.
- M. R. Masani writes to a correspondent addressed as Mr. Gopalji.
- Masani acknowledges receiving or seeing a copy of Gopalji’s letter to T. T. Krishnamachari.
- The matter raised in that correspondence is being examined by the Parliamentary Group office.
- The letter is signed by M. R. Masani and gives the recipient’s address as Dhari, Gujarat State.
