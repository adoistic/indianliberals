## Summary

This three-page English-language document comprises an office letter dated 15 February 1975, addressed to the Bharatiya Lok Dal in Bombay and signed by P. Narayan, Office Secretary. It records rent of Rs. 387.54 and electricity charges of Rs. 120.66 for January 1975, totalling Rs. 508.20. The addressee’s 40% share is calculated as Rs. 203.28, written out in words at the foot of the letter.

The accompanying pages are a Certificate of Posting form and its printed instructions. The form is addressed to Mr. Piloo Mody, M.P., in New Delhi, and states that the articles listed were posted but were not registered. The instructions explain the certificate fee, the limits of postal responsibility for loss or damage, the absence of proof regarding the contents, and the recommendation that valuable articles be sent by registered post or insurance.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 15 February 1975 and is addressed to Bharatiya Lok Dal, Bombay 1.
- January 1975 rent is listed as Rs. 387.54.
- January 1975 electricity charges are listed as Rs. 120.66.
- The combined rent and electricity total is Rs. 508.20.
- The recipient's stated 40% share is Rs. 203.28.
- The letter is signed by P. Narayan, Office Secretary.
- The accompanying Certificate of Posting is addressed to Mr. Piloo Mody, M.P., New Delhi.
- The postal instructions distinguish ordinary posting from registration and disclaim compensation for loss, damage, or delay.
