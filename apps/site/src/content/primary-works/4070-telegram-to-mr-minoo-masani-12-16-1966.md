## Key points

- The document is an Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department telegram form.
- It is addressed to M. R. Masani at the Westend Hotel, Bangalore.
- The message refers to arrangements for a meeting with the Prime Minister.
- The meeting is stated to be fixed for Monday the 13th at 5 p.m.
- The sender asks Masani to confirm his arrival.
- The message also says that the Prime Minister’s Secretariat is not writing expressly and mentions Professor Ranga.
