## Summary

This two-page office letter, dated 11 August 1975, is issued on the letterhead of the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra Department, and addressed to the Executive Secretary of Bharatiya Lok Dal at 143 M. G. Road, Bombay 1. It reports the rent and electricity charges paid for July 1975 and requests payment of the addressee's 40 percent share.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 11 August 1975.
- It is issued by the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra Department.
- The addressee is the Executive Secretary of Bharatiya Lok Dal.
- July 1975 rent is recorded as Rs. 394.79.
- July 1975 electricity charges are recorded as Rs. 124.36.
- The combined expenditure is Rs. 519.15.
- The addressee's 40 percent share is calculated as Rs. 207.68.
- The letter is signed by P. Narayan, identified as Office Secretary.
