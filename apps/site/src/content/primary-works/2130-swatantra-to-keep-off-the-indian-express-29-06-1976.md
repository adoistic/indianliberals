## Key points

- The Swatantra Party decided against joining or merging with another political party.
- Its executive, general council, and workers met at Sirkali in Tamil Nadu on 25 and 27 June 1976.
- The party resolved to function independently while cooperating with other democratic parties.
- Such cooperation was to be based on a workable programme.
- The meeting opposed proposals to take over land held by religious institutions.
- B. Ganesan presided over the meeting as president of the Tamil Nadu unit.
