## Key points

- The letter is addressed to “Sir H.P.M.” and bears an unidentified handwritten signature.
- Mrs. Lynn reports that a note has been left for Rajaji.
- The recipient and Mr. Shroff are expected to meet Rajaji at Kasturi Building at 10 a.m. on Thursday the 6th.
- Because Mr. Masani will be away from Bombay on 10 August, the Swatantra Party’s Organizing Committee meeting is set for Wednesday the 12th at 6 p.m.
- The writer suggests changing the timing of the Bank Meeting scheduled for the following Wednesday.
- A partly legible handwritten annotation appears to request a 10:30 appointment.
