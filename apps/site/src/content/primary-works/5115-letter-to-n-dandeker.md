## Key points

- The letter requests an emergency meeting of the Swatantra Party’s General Council.
- The proposed meeting is intended to discuss Minoo Masani’s resignation.
- Masani’s resignation is identified as being from the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The letter calls for a decision that would allow the party to close ranks.
- Patwardhan appeals for the party to resume united work under the leadership of its respected leaders.
