## Key points

- Piloo Mody wrote to C. Rajagopalachari on 22 January 1965.
- Mody proposed a public meeting alongside the forthcoming General Council and Central Organising Committee meetings.
- The proposed date was Sunday, 10 February 1965.
- Chowpatty and Shivaji Park were suggested as venues, with Shivaji Park preferred.
- Mody requested Rajagopalachari's agreement and an early reply to facilitate preparations.
