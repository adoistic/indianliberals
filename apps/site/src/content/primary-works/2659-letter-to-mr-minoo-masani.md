## Key points

- Volckmann identifies South Asian political systems, public administration, comparative analysis, and socio-economic change as his areas of specialization.
- His doctoral research focuses on the impact of capital-intensive investment on district administration in Sundargarh, Orissa.
- The study proposes collecting indicators of social and economic change, including education, literacy, language, geographic origins, employment, and religion since 1951.
- Volckmann seeks information on development projects and the allocation of public resources from district and state agencies.
- He plans to examine the personnel, structure, functions, and interrelationships of district administration since 1951.
- The research also calls for interviews with administrators, election-data collection, and engagement with voluntary organizations in the district.
