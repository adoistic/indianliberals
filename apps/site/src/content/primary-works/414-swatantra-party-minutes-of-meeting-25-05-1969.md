## Key points

- The document is dated 25 May 1969 and issued from the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office at 53 North Avenue, New Delhi.
- It announces a proposed membership list for various consultative committees.
- The first two pages organize proposed members by ministry or department.
- The listed portfolios cover economic, industrial, agricultural, social, infrastructural, defence, and foreign-policy matters.
- A consolidated list on pages 3–4 assigns 41 named members to one or more committees.
- C. C. Desai signs the document as Secretary.
- The pages record party organization and committee assignments rather than presenting a substantive policy argument.
