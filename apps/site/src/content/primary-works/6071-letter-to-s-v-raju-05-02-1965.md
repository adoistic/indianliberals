## Key points

- The letter is dated 5 February 1965 and originates from C. Rajagopalachari’s Madras address.
- It is addressed to Sri S. V. Raju in Bombay.
- Rajagopalachari refers to a letter from Minoo Masani.
- The correspondence concerns a Swatantra Party workers’ meeting.
- The meeting is scheduled for 7 p.m. on Saturday the 13th at the Bhavan’s Gita Hall.
- Rajagopalachari expresses agreement with the proposed meeting.
