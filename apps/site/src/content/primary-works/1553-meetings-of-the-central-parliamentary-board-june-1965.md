## Summary

The June 1965 issue of Swatantra Newsletter (No. 52), issued by the Central Office of the Swatantra Party, combines internal party reports, policy resolutions, political commentary, and state-unit news. The opening report describes meetings of the Central Parliamentary Board and National Executive in Madras, including arrangements for parliamentary constituencies, participation in Gram Panchayat elections, and the organisation of state Youth Wings. Resolutions address South Vietnam, Malaysia, Indians in Ceylon, Indo-Pakistan relations, alleged totalitarian and obscurantist pressures, social justice, taxation, farmers’ needs, diesel-oil imports, and the cancellation of the West Indies cricket tour. Across these items, the party presents itself as a defender of democratic institutions, regional security, voluntary citizenship, agricultural producers, lower taxation, and opposition to state monopoly and statist socialism.
