## Key points

- The document is a formal contradiction of press reports attributed to R. N. Singh Deo.
- Singh Deo states that journalists in Calcutta asked him about West Bengal politics.
- He says he declined to make any comment.
- He disputes reports published in the Amrita Bazar Patrika and the Indian Express on 10 November 1967.
- He declares that the statements and comments attributed to him were baseless.
