## Key points

- The letter is dated 7 August 1959 and is written from Bombay.
- Ruttonchund Master addresses a recipient as “Dear Sir Homi,” identified in the metadata as Homi Modi.
- The writer says he forwarded Dr. Radhakrishnan’s report on the Swatantra Party in a weekly letter dated 3 August.
- The report is described as placing the Swatantra Party first and second on 1 and 2 August.
- The writer says that H.E. wanted a good opposition party in the country.
- The letter refers to a meeting with H.E. on 26 July and to an enclosure that may be referred to the writer.
