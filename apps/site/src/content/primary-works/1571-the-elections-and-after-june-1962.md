## Summary

Issue no. 23 of the Swatantra Newsletter, dated June 1962, reports on the party’s performance in the 1962 general elections and its subsequent organisational activity. It lists the party’s 26 Lok Sabha members, 11 Rajya Sabha members, and 210 members in state assemblies, noting that Swatantra was the official opposition in Bihar, Gujarat, Orissa, and Rajasthan. The issue also records by-election victories, the formation of parliamentary and research bodies, plans for membership enrolment and internal elections, and efforts to collect information on corruption and administrative irregularities.

The newsletter presents the party’s political programme through campaigns against excessive taxation and wasteful expenditure, criticism of state intervention and monopolies, and support for property, free trade, constitutional agitation, and parliamentary opposition. It clarifies the party’s position on youth organisations and the Bhoodan movement, reports state-level activity in Delhi, Madras, Bombay, Orissa, and Punjab, and reproduces press commentary on Swatantra’s electoral advance. The concluding pledge commits party candidates to implementing the manifesto, promoting a balanced two-party parliamentary system, restoring moral values in public life, and opposing “Statism.”
