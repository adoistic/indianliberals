## Summary

In this personal letter dated 28 May 1963, C. Rajagopalachari writes warmly to Minoo Masani about a successful week and expresses confidence in the political prospects ahead. He attributes this hope to divine grace and urges humility, wisdom, alacrity, and courage in responding to it.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 28 May 1963 and addressed to Minoo Masani.
- Rajagopalachari describes the preceding week as exceptionally successful.
- He interprets the moment as one of divine grace and new political promise.
- He calls for humility and wisdom alongside energy and courage.
- He identifies the united front presented at Rajkot as a successful beginning.
- The letter closes with an affectionate blessing and personal sign-off.
