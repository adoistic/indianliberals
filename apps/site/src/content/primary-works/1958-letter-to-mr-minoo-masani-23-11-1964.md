## Key points

- N. Dandekar writes to M. R. Masani about parliamentary accommodation.
- He specifically requests the independent bungalow at Windsor Place occupied by Ram Ratan Gupta.
- He acknowledges that the request may be impossible because of a waiting-list priority system.
- He also allows for the possibility that Gupta may not vacate the property.
- If the preferred bungalow cannot be obtained, Dandekar asks to be offered the next-best available accommodation.
- The letter is a typed personal communication on N. Dandekar's Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) letterhead, dated 23 November 1964.
