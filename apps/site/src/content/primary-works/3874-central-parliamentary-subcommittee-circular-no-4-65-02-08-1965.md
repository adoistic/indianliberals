## Summary

This one-page Swatantra Party circular, dated 2 August 1965, announces a meeting of the Central Parliamentary Sub Committee scheduled for 15 August at Rajaji's room in Madras. Signed by M. R. Masani as General Secretary, it sets out the meeting's agenda and asks recipients to consider parliamentary business and party organisation.

## Key points

- The circular is issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay.
- The Central Parliamentary Sub Committee is to meet on Sunday, 15 August, at 11 a.m. in Rajaji's room in Madras.
- The agenda includes confirmation of minutes from the previous meeting at Dhenkanal on 25 March 1965.
- The committee is to discuss business before the August–September session of Parliament.
- Listed parliamentary matters include the Bonus Ordinance, Aligarh University Ordinance, Rann of Kutch Cease-fire Agreement, and Economic Crimes.
- The agenda also includes Orissa Assembly elections and recommendations from State Units concerning prospective parliamentary candidates.
- M. R. Masani signs the notice as General Secretary.
