## Key points

- The letter is dated 12 May 1975 and was issued from the Swatantra Party National Headquarters in Madras.
- V. S. Srikumar writes in his capacity as Joint Secretary.
- The recipient is S. V. Raju, Joint Secretary of the All India Swatantra Party.
- Srikumar acknowledges receiving Raju’s letter addressed to Sri Budh Prakash.
- The correspondence concerns the possibility of reviving the Swatantra Party in Himachal Pradesh.
