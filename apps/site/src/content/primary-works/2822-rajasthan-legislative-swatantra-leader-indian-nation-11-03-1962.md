## Key points

- Maharawal Laxman Singh of Dungarpur was elected leader of the Swatantra Party in the Rajasthan Assembly.
- Raja Man Singh and Maharajkumar Jai Singh were elected deputy leaders.
- Man Singh of Matar was elected Chief Whip.
- Suresh Bihari Mathur and Radha Krishan Goel were elected secretaries.
- Bhairon Singh was re-elected leader of the Jan Sangh group in the Rajasthan Assembly.
- The sheet also includes a separate 17 March 1962 Madras clipping reporting that the Tamilnad Forward Bloc would not merge with the Swatantra Party.
