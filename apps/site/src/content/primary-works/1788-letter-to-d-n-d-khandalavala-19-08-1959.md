## Key points

- The letter is dated 19 August 1959.
- It is addressed to D. N. D. Khandalavala, identified as 'Dora' in the salutation.
- The sender thanks Khandalavala for a donation to the Swatantra Party's funds.
- The donation is described as unsolicited and therefore especially welcome.
- The sender agrees to register the donor's name and address as requested.
- A typed annotation specifies a cheque for Rs. 200 and requests party membership registration and an official receipt.
