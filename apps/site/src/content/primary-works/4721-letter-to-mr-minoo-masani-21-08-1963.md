## Key points

- The letter is dated 21 August 1963 and is written on C. Rajagopalachari's Madras letterhead.
- Rajagopalachari addresses M. R. Masani as “My dear Masani.”
- He praises Masani's speech on a no-confidence motion.
- He says that “Ranga” sent a letter greatly appreciating the speech.
- The letter is signed “C. Rajagopalachari” and addressed to Masani at a New Delhi camp address.
