## Summary

This three-page English correspondence file documents the dispatch and receipt of 100 copies of M. R. Masani's lecture in July 1985. In a letter dated 10 July 1985, C. L. Rao of the Bangalore Educational Trust asks S. V. Raju to confirm receipt of a registered parcel sent on 24 June 1985. The included envelope is addressed to Raju in Bombay.

In his reply dated 15 July 1985, Raju thanks Rao for the parcel and for distributing the lecture copies free of cost, confirming that the package arrived the previous week. The file is administrative in character and contains no discussion of the lecture's contents.

## Key points

- C. L. Rao writes from the Bangalore Educational Trust on 10 July 1985.
- The letter concerns a registered parcel containing 100 copies of M. R. Masani's lecture.
- The parcel was sent to S. V. Raju on 24 June 1985.
- Rao asks Raju to confirm that the parcel was received.
- An included envelope is addressed to S. V. Raju in Bombay.
- Raju's 15 July 1985 reply confirms receipt the previous week.
- Raju thanks Rao for providing the copies free of cost and distributing them.
