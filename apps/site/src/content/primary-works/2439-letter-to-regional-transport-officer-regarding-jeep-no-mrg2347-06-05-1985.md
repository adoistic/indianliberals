## Summary

This one-page typed carbon-copy letter, dated 6 May 1985, is from M. R. Masani to the Regional Transport Officer in Bombay concerning vehicle MRG-2347. Masani states that his protests that he neither owned nor possessed the vehicle had been disregarded and returns the demand notice relating to it.

## Key points

- M. R. Masani addresses the Regional Transport Officer, Bombay, regarding vehicle MRG-2347.
- He says that his protests denying ownership or possession of MRG-2347 have failed.
- He returns the demand notice concerning the vehicle.
- He states that MRG-2347 was one of the Jeeps belonging to the Swatantra Party and sold in 1967.
- He asks not to be harassed regarding eight additional vehicles that he says were also sold by the Swatantra Party in 1967.
- The listed vehicles are MRA-4092, MRA-4093, MRA-4095, MRA-4096, MRA-4024, MRG-2347, MRG-2184, MRG-2185, and MRZ-8939.
