## Summary

This untitled handwritten letter, dated Bhubaneswar, 1 April 1985, is addressed to S. V. Raju in Bombay. The writer acknowledges Raju's letter of 19 March 1985 and conveys good wishes on its receipt. The principal news is that Sri Chaitram Mishra has been elected to the Orissa Legislative Assembly on a Janata Party ticket.

## Key points

- The letter is dated Bhubaneswar, 1 April 1985.
- It is addressed to Mr. S. V. Raju.
- The writer acknowledges receipt of Raju's letter dated 19 March 1985.
- The writer reports that Sri Chaitram Mishra was elected to the Orissa Legislative Assembly.
- The election was secured on a Janata Party ticket.
- The writer refers to continuing pessimism about the Orissa Legislative Assembly.
- The letter closes with a request that further correspondence be sent to the writer's Bhubaneswar address.
