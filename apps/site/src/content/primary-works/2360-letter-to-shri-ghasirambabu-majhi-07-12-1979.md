## Summary

This single-page letter, dated December 7, 1979, is from S. V. Raju to Ghasirambabu Majhi, then Minister of Health in the Government of Orissa. Raju follows up on an enquiry from the Regional Transport Officer in Bombay concerning Jeep No. MRA 4093, which Maharaj P. K. Deo of Kalahandi purchased soon after the 1967 general elections.

## Key points

- The letter is dated December 7, 1979.
- S. V. Raju writes to Shri Ghasirambabu Majhi, Minister of Health, Government of Orissa.
- The correspondence concerns the present whereabouts of Jeep No. MRA 4093.
- Raju states that Maharaj P. K. Deo of Kalahandi bought the jeep soon after the 1967 general elections.
- Raju had written to the Raja Saheb and received a reply that the jeep was not with him.
- Raju asks Majhi to provide the jeep's current location and identify whom it is presently with.
