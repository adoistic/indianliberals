## Key points

- The document is a Swatantra Party press-publication note dated November 5, 1967.
- It reproduces a telegram sent by M. R. Masani, N. Dandekar, and Manubhai Amersay to Prime Minister Indira Gandhi.
- The telegram objects to Gandhi's planned participation in Moscow celebrations marking the fiftieth anniversary of the Communist Revolution.
- The signatories characterize the event as an occasion for totalitarian and anti-democratic propaganda.
- They argue that the visit conflicts with India's professed policy of non-alignment.
- They ask Gandhi to determine whether prime ministers of other democratic countries will attend and to reconsider her decision.
- S. V. Raju signs the publication note as Executive Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
