## Key points

- The document is headed “GENERAL SECRETARY'S LETTER NO. 134.”
- It was issued from the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay.
- The letter is dated 8 September 1966.
- The next National Convention was to be held in New Delhi.
- The venue was Sapru House.
- The convention dates were 16 and 17 November 1966.
- M. R. Masani signs the letter as General Secretary.
