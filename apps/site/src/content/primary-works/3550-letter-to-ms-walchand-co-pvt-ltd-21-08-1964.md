## Summary

This one-page English office letter, dated August 21, 1964, is from M. R. Masani to M/s Walchand & Co. Private Ltd. in Bombay. Masani acknowledges the recipient's letter no. W/197 of August 18 and states that he does not yet know the details of the matter referred to.

## Key points

- The letter is dated August 21, 1964.
- M. R. Masani acknowledges receipt of letter no. W/197 dated August 18.
- Masani says he is not yet informed about the matter raised by the recipient.
- He asks his office to investigate the matter immediately.
- He promises to write again in due course.
- The recipient is M/s Walchand & Co. Private Ltd., Construction House, Ballard Estate, Bombay 1.
