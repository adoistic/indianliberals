## Summary

This single-page English-language events listing from the Times of India, dated 5 May 1999, provides a Mumbai cultural and civic calendar. It groups events under Films, Lectures, Exhibitions, Events, Religious Discourses, Seminars/Workshops, and Miscellaneous, with venues, times, speakers, artists, and programme descriptions.

## Key points

- The Films section lists a screening of Hindi film 'Humayun', directed by Mehboob Khan, at the National Centre for the Performing Arts.
- Lectures include talks on Hasya Yoga, the 'Priceless Jewel', coalition government, Rotary Vision 2000, and central excise and service-tax matters.
- The Exhibitions section advertises painting, photography, sculpture, craft, ceramics, theatre photography, and collaborative art installations at venues across Mumbai.
- Religious programmes include discourses on the Bhagavad Gita and Shrimad Bhagwat Gita by Hindu and Jain spiritual teachers.
- Seminars and workshops cover service-tax administration, summer arts and crafts, mask-making, miniature painting, Thai art, fruit carving, ceramics, and Indus Valley civilisation.
- Miscellaneous listings include Rotary meetings, a free Ayurvedic camp, and a visual-arts encounter titled 'Unity in the Visual Field'.
