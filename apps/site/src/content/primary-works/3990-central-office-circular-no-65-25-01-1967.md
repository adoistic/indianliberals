## Summary

This two-page Swatantra Party Central Office Circular, dated 25 January 1967, transmits General Secretary M. R. Masani’s itinerary to party correspondents or members. The letter is signed by S. V. Raju in his capacity as Executive Secretary and identifies the enclosed document as Masani’s tour program.

## Key points

- The circular is issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay on 25 January 1967.
- It forwards M. R. Masani’s itinerary for travel from 28 January to 2 March 1967.
- S. V. Raju signs the communication as Executive Secretary.
- The enclosed itinerary records journeys departing from and returning to Bombay.
- The program includes visits or travel involving Bangalore, Chittoor, Madras, and Rajkot.
- Train numbers, departure and arrival times, and hotels are listed for several stages of the tour.
