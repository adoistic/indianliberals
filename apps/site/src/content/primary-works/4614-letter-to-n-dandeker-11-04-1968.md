## Key points

- The letter is dated 11 April 1968 and was sent from 2 Jugal Kishore Road, New Delhi.
- M. R. Masani forwards a copy of his letter to Professor Ranga.
- He asks N. Dandeker to bring the proposed action in that letter to the attention of the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- Masani encloses a separate letter received from Babubhai Vaidya.
- The enclosed letter is described as containing information relevant to the Swatantra Party’s National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- The substantive contents of both enclosed letters are not visible on this page.
