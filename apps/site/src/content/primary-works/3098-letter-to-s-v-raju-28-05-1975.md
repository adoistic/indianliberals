## Summary

This untitled letter, dated 28 May 1975, is written by D. N. Patodia from New Delhi to S. V. Raju, identified in the address as a member of the Swatantra Party. Patodia thanks Raju for his letter of 22 May 1975 and says he hopes to discuss the matter personally when they meet in Delhi or Bombay.

The letter does not disclose the subject of the correspondence. It closes by asking whether Raju has plans to come to Delhi, followed by a formal expression of regards and Patodia’s handwritten signature. Handwritten annotations on the page include a circled number at the top and a note near the bottom reading “Read on 30/5.”
