## Key points

- The rupee is described as a link between yesterday, today, and tomorrow.
- It is presented as a store of purchasing power.
- It functions as a measure of economic activity.
- The rupee facilitates exchange, division of labour, specialisation, and mass production.
- The pamphlet links monetary stability with better standards of living for all.
- A stable currency is compared to a uniform yardstick used to measure cloth.
- The page argues that the rupee should not undergo wide fluctuations in value.
