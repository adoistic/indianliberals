Subramanian says that letters addressed to the Swatantra Party's former national headquarters are being delivered to the Swatantra Party's office because the headquarters has moved to Madras, while letters addressed to the party at its old address cannot be delivered to Bhatia. He asks Bhatia to instruct his staff to forward all such letters promptly and appeals to their friendship to secure payment of an amount outstanding since September 1974.

## Key points

- The letter is dated May 14, 1975, and is sent from the Swatantra Party's Maharashtra Division.
- It is addressed to V. P. Bhatia of the Bharatiya Lok Dal in Bombay.
- Subramanian explains that Bhatia's April 22 letter, addressed to Girish Munshi, is being answered because Munshi is out of Bombay.
- The letter disputes objections to mail addressed to the Swatantra Party's old national headquarters being delivered to the party's office.
- It states that the Swatantra Party's national headquarters has moved to Madras following a December 1974 General Council decision.
- Subramanian asks Bhatia to have his staff forward all correspondence addressed to the Swatantra Party's national headquarters.
- The letter also requests settlement of a payment outstanding since September 1974.
