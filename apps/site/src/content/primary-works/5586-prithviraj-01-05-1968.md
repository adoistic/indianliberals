## Summary

This one-page English profile presents Prithviraj, identified as the 33-year-old son of Man Singh and a former political heir associated with Jaipur. It describes his upbringing as quiet and disciplined, his education in Harrow, and his return to India for business training. The profile lists his corporate roles, including director of Service Parts Ltd. and Sawai Man Singh Industries Corporation, a partner in Amar Corporation, and managing director of Sunshine India Ltd.

The article frames Prithviraj's entry into politics as a consequence of family circumstances rather than personal ambition. After Man Singh was drawn into political activity against the Congress in 1962, Prithviraj abandoned business to enter the political fray. He was elected to the Lok Sabha from the Dausa Parliamentary constituency by a large margin, but the profile says that his elder brother's defeat in the 1967 election altered the family's political position. It reports that Gayatri Devi then helped persuade Man Singh to enter politics after his term as Ambassador to Spain ended, while Prithviraj prepared to contest the forthcoming Rajasthan elections.

## Key points

- The profile introduces Prithviraj as Man Singh's 33-year-old son and a former political heir connected with Jaipur.
- It describes his quiet, disciplined upbringing and education at Harrow.
- After studying law, Prithviraj returned to India for business training and held several corporate positions.
- The article says he left business after his father and elder brother became involved in opposition politics.
- He was elected to the Lok Sabha from the Dausa Parliamentary constituency by a margin of about 80,000 votes.
- The defeat of his elder brother in the 1967 election is presented as a turning point in the family's political plans.
- The profile closes with Prithviraj preparing for Rajasthan elections while his father considered re-entering politics.
