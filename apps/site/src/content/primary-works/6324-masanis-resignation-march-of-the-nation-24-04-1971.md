## Key points

- Masani’s resignation from the Swatantra Party chairmanship is described as motivated by lofty principles.
- Masani reportedly accepted full responsibility for the party’s poor showing in the mid-term Lok Sabha elections.
- The party’s representation fell from 44 seats in 1967 to 8 seats in 1971.
- The article says many party workers viewed the electoral Alliance as a hindrance rather than a help.
- Masani’s organisational ability is presented as difficult to replace.
