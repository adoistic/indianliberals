## Key points

- The letter is dated March 22, 1977 and addressed to Girish.
- It refers to a disagreement over issues raised by the signatories.
- The parties are invited to submit the matter to the sole arbitration of a person of status.
- The writers agree to abide by the arbitrator’s decision.
- M. R. Masani and R. V. Murthy are proposed as possible arbitrators.
- The recipient is asked to confirm whether either proposed arbitrator is acceptable.
- The document is signed by K. H. Subramaniam, L. R. Sampat, and S. V. Raju.
