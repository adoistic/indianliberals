## Key points

- The Lok Sabha adjourned until January 21, 1963. The next sitting was expected to last approximately a week or ten days. The January sitting was intended to consider developments in the conflict with China and matters related to the national emergency. The regular Budget Session would normally begin in the second week of February, though its timing might be later that year. Speaker Sardar Hukam Singh characterized the session as unusually memorable and emphasized parliamentary unity.
- Some members wanted to return to their constituencies to assist with civil-defence and other emergency work.
