## Summary

This one-page English letter from M. R. Masani to N. Dandekar is dated 28 May 1968 and concerns a matter previously raised by Dandekar. Masani states that, in letters to Dandekar and members of the General Council, he has already said all that can usefully be said on the subject.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 28 May 1968.
- Masani addresses N. Dandekar as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party in Bombay.
- Masani refers to Dandekar's letter of 22 May.
- Masani says he has already communicated his position to Dandekar and the General Council.
- Masani remains unconvinced by Dandekar's argument.
- Masani concludes that Dandekar is pursuing the wrong approach.
