## Summary

This four-page Swatantra Party office memo, dated February 5, 1970, is addressed to M. R. Masani, with copies to Dr. R. C. Cooper and S. V. Raju. G. B. Sukhee reports on recent visits to Madras State, Pondicherry, Rajkot, and Kerala, and draws attention to attached organisational and political issues. The memo combines local political intelligence with recommendations for strengthening the party’s presence through personal visits, internal coordination, and recruitment.

## Key points

- Sukhee reports that Vallabh Bhai Patel’s political network controls village panchayats in his constituency and has marginalised Swatantra Party workers.
- The memo recommends that Masani visit the Rajkot constituency and assess the situation through direct contact with party members.
- The Pondicherry L.S.P. camp is described as small but well organised, with trainees drawn from Neyveli Corporation employees and linked to union activity.
- Sukhee proposes recognising newly elected office-bearers of the Electricity Board Employees’ Union and coordinating trained workers into a broader democratic organisation.
- The Kerala section raises concern about the durability of the Swatantra unit there and asks that a confidential personnel matter be discussed privately.
- The document presents party organisation, labour mobilisation, and constituency-level political competition as urgent governance and party-building concerns.
