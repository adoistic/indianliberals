## Key points

- The letter responds to Masani’s letter of 24 June and an enclosure sent after their conversation.
- Patel says further thought has not altered his position.
- He refers to discussions about appointing or recommending a president involving Masani, Shri Bhailalbhai, and others.
- Patel says the matter has not been discussed informally before the party executive.
- He questions the usefulness of continuing as Vice-President if the affairs of the Gujarat Party are to be run in this manner.
- The letter concludes by asking Masani to accept it as Patel’s resignation.
