## Key points

- Narayan writes to Minoo Masani from Patna on April 5, 1974.
- He has decided to travel to Vellore at the end of the month for prostate surgery.
- Dr. R. V. P. Sinha recommended Dr. R. Bhatt of the Vellore hospital for the procedure.
- Narayan explains that he must wear a catheter continuously until the prostate is removed.
- He rejects going to Bombay because of the discomfort and disruption that visits from friends would cause.
- He expects Vellore to provide the peace and rest he needs during a personally and nationally critical period.
- He thanks Masani for kind remarks about him in Reader's Digest and notes that Rahul Singh and a member of the magazine's editorial staff interviewed him in Banaras.
