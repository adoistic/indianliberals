## Key points

- The resolution was adopted by the Andhra Pradesh Swatantra Party Executive Committee at Bapatla on 19 April 1968.
- It responds to the defeat of the party's candidate in Rajya Sabha biennial elections in Gujarat.
- The defeat is described as having led to the resignation of M. R. Masani, M.P., from the Central Party's Executive Committee and Parliamentary Board.
- The Andhra Pradesh committee asks Masani to withdraw his resignation.
- The stated reason for the request is the interest of the Swatantra Party.
