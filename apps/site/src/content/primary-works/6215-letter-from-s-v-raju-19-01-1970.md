## Key points

- The letter is dated January 19, 1970.
- S. V. Raju responds to J. L. Dholakia's January 12 letter.
- Dholakia had invited Minoo Masani to address students.
- Masani is expected in Ahmedabad on February 1 but will return to Bombay that night.
- Masani's programme on February 1 is already heavily crowded.
- He therefore cannot accept the invitation to speak on the afternoon of February 2.
