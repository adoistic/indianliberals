## Key points

- The article discusses inter-State disputes and the constitutional relationship between the Union and the States.
- It recounts Karnataka Chief Minister Veerendra Patil’s 1968 proposal for a code regulating Ministers’ statements about other States.
- Noorani criticises public accusations and retaliatory statements by Chief Ministers as a poor substitute for institutional dispute resolution.
- The article argues that political grievances should be addressed through constitutional mechanisms rather than acrimonious public debate.
- Article 263 is presented as a basis for establishing an Inter-State Council.
- The Federal Structure Sub-Committee of the Round Table Conference is cited as having supported a permanent coordinating and dispute-settlement body.
- The article refers to a contemporary dispute involving Tamil Nadu and Kerala and to a reported suggestion that the National Integration Council consider the matter.
