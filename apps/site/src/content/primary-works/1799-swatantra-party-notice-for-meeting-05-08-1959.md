## Summary

This single-page English notice, issued on Swatantra Party (Bombay Office) letterhead, announces a meeting of the Organising Committee for Greater Bombay. The meeting is scheduled for 6 p.m. at the Swatantra Party office at 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1, and recipients are asked to attend.

The notice is dated 5 August 1958 and is signed by the joint secretary. Handwritten annotations appear to amend or mark parts of the meeting date and text, but the printed notice clearly concerns party organisation and attendance at the Greater Bombay committee meeting.

## Key points

- The document is a Swatantra Party organisational notice from the Bombay Office.
- It announces a meeting of the Organising Committee for Greater Bombay.
- The meeting is scheduled for 6 p.m. at 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
- The notice requests the recipient's attendance.
- The printed notice is dated 5 August 1958 and bears a joint secretary's signature.
