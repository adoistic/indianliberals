## Key points

- The circular is issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office in Bombay.
- It announces administrative arrangements for the party's National Executive Meeting in Madras on September 29–30, 1967.
- Recipients are asked to contact Mr. T. Sadasivam regarding hotel and return-journey reservations.
- The deadline for communicating reservation requirements is September 12.
- Recipients must specify whether they want the party to arrange hotel accommodation or intend to do so themselves.
- Return travel may be arranged by train or plane, with the desired mode and details to be provided.
- The circular notes that Mrs. and Mr. Sadasivan would welcome recipients for lunch on September 30.
