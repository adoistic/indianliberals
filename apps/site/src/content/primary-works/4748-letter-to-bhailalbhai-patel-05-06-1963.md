## Key points

- Masani acknowledges Patel’s congratulations of 29 May 1963 and apologises for the delayed response.
- He attributes the delay to the large volume of correspondence received at his office.
- He credits Patel with being the architect of the recent political success and calls it as much Patel’s victory as his own.
- He asks Patel to convey his gratitude to Gujarat party members who had travelled to Rajkot to thank them.
- Masani describes travel plans involving Ahmedabad, Vallabh Vidya Nagar, and a possible public meeting in Bombay.
- He notes that his wife has slipped and fractured her arm, limiting his ability to stay away from home.
- The letter refers to an enclosed press summary of Masani’s talk, but the enclosure is not included on the rendered page.
