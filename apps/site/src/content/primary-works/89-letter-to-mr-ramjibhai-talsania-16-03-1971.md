## Summary

This one-page English letter from M. R. Masani, dated 16 March 1971, thanks Mr. Ramjibhai Talsania for making a jeep available for the Yuvraj of Jasdan from 25 February to 2 March. Masani also acknowledges Talsania’s help and cooperation during that period.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 16 March 1971.
- M. R. Masani addresses Mr. Ramjibhai Talsania.
- Talsania placed a jeep at the disposal of the Yuvraj of Jasdan.
- The jeep was made available from 25 February to 2 March.
- Masani expresses thanks for Talsania’s assistance, help, and cooperation.
- The letter is signed “M. R. Masani” and addressed to Vishwakarma Engineering Works, Alkal Road, Jabadan.
