## Key points

- The National General Council meeting is scheduled for 8 December 1974 in Madras.
- The agenda includes filling vacancies caused by defections from the National Executive.
- The party plans to consider measures for strengthening the Swatantra Party across the country.
- Delegates are to formulate the party's position on current political problems.
- The Council is asked to decide whether to prepare for an expected mid-term poll or the regular 1976 general elections.
- The letter provides arrangements for hotel reservations and return journey tickets.
