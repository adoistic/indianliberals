## Key points

- The Parliamentary Board of the Swatantra Party adopted three resolutions unanimously in New Delhi.
- The first resolution demands that the Communist Party of India be banned under the Defence of India Regulations.
- The resolution characterises the Communist Party of India as a “Fifth Column” within the country.
- The Board calls for party politics to be excluded from the defence effort.
- It proposes joint defence committees presided over by the President and State Governors.
- It welcomes the National Defence Council but also advocates a wider advisory body including serving and retired generals.
