## Summary

This single-page English telegram, sent on an Indian Posts and Telegraphs Department form, concerns the transfer and accounting of funds associated with a Bihar unit. The message states that Rs. 3,75,000 is payable to the Bihar unit by the Centre, and reports that approximately Rs. 50,000 had already been received several months earlier.

## Key points

- The telegram records a payment of Rs. 3,75,000 payable to a Bihar unit.
- It says the Bihar unit had received approximately Rs. 50,000 from the Centre several months earlier.
- It refers to a further payment of Rs. 80,000 made to a bank.
- It records an additional Rs. 40,000 being paid to the bank.
- The remaining amount is described as being transferred for payment to Bihar.
- The sender, recipient, date, and year are not legible or filled in sufficiently to establish attribution or chronology.
