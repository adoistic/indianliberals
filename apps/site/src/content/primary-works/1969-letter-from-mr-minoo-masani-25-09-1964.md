## Summary

This one-page letter, dated 25 September 1964, was written by M. R. Masani from 57 Lodi Estate, New Delhi, to Bhanu Pratap Singh of the Swatantra Party in Uttar Pradesh. Masani addresses the resignation of Yashpal Singh from the party and his decision to join the S.S.P., explaining that the party had attempted to accommodate him, including by offering him a seat on the third bench.

## Key points

- Masani reports that Yashpal Singh resigned from the Swatantra Party and joined the S.S.P.
- He says the party made efforts to provide Singh with a suitable seat.
- The proposed arrangements included a seat on the third bench.
- Masani states that Singh insisted on sitting on the governing bench, which was unavailable because it had been allotted to Mr. P. K. Deb by the Parliamentary Group.
- He encloses the minutes of the relevant meeting and a copy of a letter sent to Singh.
- Masani accuses Singh of misstating the facts in an attempt to gain sympathy and describes him as a constant embarrassment to the party.
