## Key points

- Another 100 dacoits from the Chambal ravines were expected to surrender the following day.
- A conference was convened to address the dacoit problem through administrative and rehabilitative measures.
- The proposed rehabilitation plan included settling surrendered dacoits on agricultural land.
- The reclamation programme was estimated to cost approximately Rs. 70 crores.
- The plan was intended to include ravines in Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Rajasthan.
- The report links the proposed surrender to earlier episodes involving Maharaja Madhavrao Scindia and Acharya Vinoba Bhave.
- The state government planned to introduce the required legislation as soon as possible.
