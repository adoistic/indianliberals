## Key points

- The circular is dated 17 October 1967 and issued from the Swatantra Party's Central Office in Bombay.
- It announces the circulation of ten copies of “Naxalbari”: a Report.
- The report is attributed to N. Dandekar, M.P., and D. N. Patodia, M.P.
- Bulk orders of 100 copies or more are offered a 25 per cent discount.
- The stated cost of the Naxalbari booklet is 15 paise per copy.
- A specimen copy of M. R. Masani's “Must India Go The Vietnam Way?” is enclosed.
- Further copies of the Vietnam booklet could be requested from S. N. Kumar, Secretary of the Swatantra Party's Delhi Unit.
