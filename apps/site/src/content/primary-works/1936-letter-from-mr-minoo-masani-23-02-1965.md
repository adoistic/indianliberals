## Key points

- The letter is addressed to the Secretary of the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Group.
- Masani is responding to a circular and an inquiry contained in it.
- He indicates that he would move and cut motions on ministry grant demands.
- He offers to speak on the demands for grants.
- He ranks the Ministries of External Affairs, Transport, and Defence in that order of preference.
