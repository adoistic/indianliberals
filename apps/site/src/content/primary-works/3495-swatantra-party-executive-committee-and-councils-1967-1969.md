summary: |-
  This Swatantra Party directory records the composition and contact details of the party’s organisational bodies for the 1967–1969 period. The visible pages cover the General Council, National Executive, Central Parliamentary Board, state and regional executive committees and councils, offices of state and regional units, legislators, and Swatantra members of the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha. Entries are arranged principally by state or region and identify office-holders, members, constituencies, addresses, and telephone numbers.
  
  The document also functions as a corrigenda and membership-update record. Its opening pages list additions, deletions, and corrections to names, offices, addresses, and telephone numbers, while later pages provide fuller rosters for Andhra, Bombay, Gujarat, Kerala, Madras, Mysore, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Delhi, Assam, Bihar, Haryana, and other units. The pages read are administrative rather than programmatic: they document the party’s organisational reach and internal structure, but do not present an account of its political principles or policy positions.

## Key points

- The directory covers Swatantra Party bodies associated with the 1967–1969 organisational period.
- It lists the General Council, National Executive, Central Parliamentary Board, and state or regional committees and councils.
- State and regional listings identify presidents, secretaries, treasurers, joint secretaries, members, and office addresses.
- Separate sections record Swatantra legislators and members of the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
- The addenda distinguish additions, deletions, and corrections to names, offices, addresses, and telephone numbers.
- The visible pages show extensive organisation across numerous Indian states and regions.
- The document is an administrative directory and does not, in the pages read, articulate a political programme or ideological argument.
