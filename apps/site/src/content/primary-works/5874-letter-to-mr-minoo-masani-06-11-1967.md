## Summary

This one-page letter, dated 6 November 1967, is written by R. V. Murthy, president of the Matunga Unit of the Swatantra Party, to Minoo Masani. Murthy encloses an appeal concerning a cultural programme scheduled for 5 December 1967 at Shri Shanmukhananda Sabha Hall in Sion, Bombay, explaining that the event is intended to raise funds for the unit's constructive activities.

Murthy also announces a souvenir publication describing the unit's activities and future programme. He asks Masani to contribute a message for inclusion, noting that the unit's activities have received strong public appreciation and that members would value his support. The page records no substantive political argument beyond this appeal for participation and fundraising.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 6 November 1967 and comes from the Matunga Unit of the Swatantra Party.
- R. V. Murthy identifies himself as the unit's president.
- A cultural programme was scheduled for 5 December 1967 at Shri Shanmukhananda Sabha Hall, Sion, Bombay.
- The programme was intended to raise funds for the unit's constructive activities.
- The unit was preparing a souvenir outlining its activities and future programme.
- Murthy requests a message from Minoo Masani for publication in the souvenir.
- The letter emphasizes public appreciation of the unit's activities and the members' desire for Masani's support.
