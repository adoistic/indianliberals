## Summary

This two-page Lok Sabha Revised List of Business, dated 24 August 1961, records the House's scheduled business for the day. It includes a question on flooding in Delhi residential areas, the laying of notifications under the Drugs Act and Essential Commodities Act, and a ministerial statement concerning accommodation for Indian Airlines passengers and physicians on a Patna–Delhi flight.

## Key points

- The sitting was scheduled for 11 a.m. on Thursday, 24 August 1961.
- Balraj Madhok was to raise flooding in Delhi's Diplomatic Enclave and other residential colonies after heavy rainfall.
- Notifications under the Drugs Act, 1940, and Essential Commodities Act, 1955, were to be laid on the Table.
- The Minister of Transport and Communications was scheduled to address an Indian Airlines accommodation failure.
- The House was to resume consideration of the Third Five Year Plan.
- Further consideration and passage of an income-tax and super-tax Bill were listed as legislative business.
- A 5 p.m. discussion was scheduled on Indian jute mills' block closures owing to scarcity of raw jute.
