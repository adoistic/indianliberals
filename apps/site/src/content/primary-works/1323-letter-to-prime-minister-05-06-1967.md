## Key points

- The letter discusses the developments in West Asia and their implications for world peace.
- It links the regional situation to India's national interests and the well-being of its people.
- It urges the Indian government to adopt an objective attitude.
- It advises India to avoid taking sides or assigning blame at this stage.
- It argues that a neutral, country-wide perspective could support India's honourable role in achieving peace in West Asia.
- The sender and recipient are not identified by legible names on the page.
