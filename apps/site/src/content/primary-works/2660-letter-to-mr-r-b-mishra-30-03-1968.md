## Key points

- The document is a one-page typed telegram confirmation copy.
- It is addressed to Rajballabh Mishra, Minister of Agriculture and Community Development, Bhubaneswar.
- The sender identifies themselves only as “MASANI.”
- The message is dated 30 March 1968.
- The sender says they will not be in Delhi on the 13th and 14th.
- The sender asks Mishra to postpone his Delhi visit and proposes meeting in Bhubaneswar on 4 April.
