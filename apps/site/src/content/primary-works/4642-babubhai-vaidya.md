## Key points

- Babubhai Vaidya led the Independent Progressive Group in the Gujarat Assembly for at least five years.
- Several members of the group joined the Swatantra Party at the end of 1966 with Vaidya’s goodwill and approval.
- Vaidya campaigned intensively for Swatantra candidates in the February 1967 elections, including candidates in Rajkot, Junagadh, Jamnagar, and Surendranagar.
- The note presents him as a Gandhian social worker devoted to Harijans and underprivileged communities.
- Although a pacifist who sometimes appeared on Communist-inspired 'Peace Front' platforms, Vaidya is described as opposed to communism and committed to individual liberty.
- Vaidya was not prepared to formally join the Swatantra Party but promised cooperation and moral support in the Rajya Sabha.
