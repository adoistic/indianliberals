## Summary

This typed letter, dated 3 June 1975, was sent from the Swatantra Party's National Headquarters in Madras by Joint Secretary V. S. Srikumar to P. Narayan of the party's Maharashtra unit. Srikumar acknowledges Narayan's letter of 29 May and reports that he has written twice to S. V. Raju at his house address.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 3 June 1975 and issued on Swatantra Party National Headquarters letterhead in Madras.
- V. S. Srikumar acknowledges receipt of P. Narayan's letter dated 29 May 1975 and its enclosures.
- Srikumar says he has written two letters to S. V. Raju at Raju's house address.
- The party's advocate has promised to obtain a copy of an ex-parte decree immediately or by the following day.
- Srikumar offers to send Narayan a copy of the decree as soon as he receives it.
- The letter asks Narayan to inform Srikumar when Sri Girish Munshi returns to headquarters.
- The second rendered page shows the addressed and stamped envelope accompanying the letter.
