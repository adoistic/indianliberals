## Key points

- The letter is dated 5 April 1968 and was sent from Rajagopalachari's address on Naoroji Road, Kilpauk, Madras-10.
- Rajagopalachari acknowledges Masani's letter of 3 April.
- He expresses respect and affection toward Masani.
- He argues that the Party should take suitable action against the offenders.
- He insists that Masani withdraw his resignations.
