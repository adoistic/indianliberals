## Key points

- The letter is dated 13 September 1974 and is addressed to S. V. Raju.
- Srikumar encloses resolutions from several Swatantra Party bodies and office-bearers.
- Approximately 200 invitations were sent and 150 invitees attended the meeting.
- The meeting participants expressed enthusiasm for maintaining the Swatantra Party's integrity.
- The participants also supported building up the party as much as possible.
- Srikumar encloses observations by a City Civil Court judge concerning an order on his interim application.
