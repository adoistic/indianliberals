## Key points

- The clipping reports a statement by Swatantra Party leader K. M. Munshi.
- Munshi supported continuing the emergency in 1963.
- He linked continuation of the emergency to India’s ability to meet the Chinese challenge.
- He argued that India should first become militarily strong enough to deter neighbouring states from starting a war.
- The statement was delivered at a luncheon meeting of the Rotary Club of Bombay.
