# Real Land Reforms

*A Swatantra Party publication*

## Summary

Real Land Reforms is a Swatantra Party pamphlet setting out the party's alternative to the Congress government's land-reform programme. Consistent with the party's classical-liberal platform, it argues that genuine reform lies in securing the cultivator's property rights, incentives, and access to capital and markets — rather than in the land-ceiling legislation, tenancy controls, and cooperative-farming schemes the party regarded as counter-productive.
