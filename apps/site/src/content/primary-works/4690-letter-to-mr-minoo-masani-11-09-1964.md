## Key points

- The letter is dated September 11, 1964, and originates from 53, North Avenue, New Delhi.
- It is addressed to M. R. Masani.
- The Speaker has allocated new seats to members of the Swatantra Party.
- The stated reason is that the party has become the largest Opposition Group.
- The handwritten seat number allocated to the recipient appears to be 448.
- Masani is requested to occupy the newly assigned seat from Monday, September 14, 1964.
- The letter is signed by P. N. Solanki, Secretary of the Swatantra Party Parliamentary Office.
