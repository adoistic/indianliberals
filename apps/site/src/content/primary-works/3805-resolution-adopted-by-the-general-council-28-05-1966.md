## Key points

- The resolution was adopted by the General Council on May 28, 1966.
- It identifies a trend toward transferring constitutional power from Parliament to the Congress Party organisation.
- It characterises this transfer as an attack on India’s Constitution.
- It urges the electorate to act against the government’s course.
- It warns that unchecked centralisation could approximate systems of government in Communist countries.
- It defends the parliamentary system contemplated by the Constitution adopted in 1950.
