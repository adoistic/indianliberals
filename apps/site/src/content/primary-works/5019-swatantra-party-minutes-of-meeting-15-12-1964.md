## Key points

- The Executive Committee met on 14 December 1964 at Parliament House.
- S. Kapur Singh and Himmat Singhji are recorded as present.
- The minutes allocate speakers to ten parliamentary subjects.
- C. L. N. Reddy was assigned the Banaras Hindu University Bill, subject to clarification about Krishnapal Singh's nomination.
- P. N. Solanki was to represent the Party in discussions with the Minister of Parliamentary Affairs.
- The record preserves a party decision concerning freedom of conscience in relation to a Supreme Court opinion.
- An office stenographer, K. B. Nair, was granted one month's salary in advance during leave.
