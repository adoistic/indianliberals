## Summary

This advance page proof from Swarajya, Vol. XIII, No. 48, dated May 31, 1969, contains a signed article by C. Rajagopalachari on proposed merger talks among the Bharatiya Kranti Dal (BKD), Jan Sangh, and Swatantra Party. The article argues that the breakdown of law and order and the Union government's perceived inaction make cooperation among non-Congress democratic parties urgent. Rajagopalachari presents the merger as a possible way to consolidate opposition forces around constitutional government and public security.
