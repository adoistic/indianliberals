## Key points

- S. V. Raju writes to Minoo Masani on 16 January 1970.
- Cooper is reported to be in Europe and unavailable for the Ahmedabad programme.
- Mr. Dandekar’s doctors recommend an operation, delaying his availability.
- R. K. Amin may travel to Southeast Asia but is expected to be in Ahmedabad from 16 to 27 January.
- A proposed 1 February schedule includes meetings with the Gujarat unit, H. M. Patel, the press, and the public.
- Raju seeks confirmation of train reservations and Masani’s availability for a Patna Bill meeting in Delhi.
- The letter discusses arrangements for the Sundargarh District Conference at Rourkela and encloses potentially relevant press clippings.
- The handwritten pages contain partially legible travel and meeting notes with substantial deletions and corrections.
