## Key points

- The letter is dated 9 December 1986 and addressed to the Regional Transport Officer, Old Bodyguard Lines, Bombay.
- Masani disputes a motor-vehicle tax demand concerning vehicle MRZ 8939.
- He cites earlier correspondence dated 6 May 1985 and 5 September 1986.
- He states that no taxes were due from him and asks the office not to write to him again about the vehicle.
- He returns Bill No. MRZ-8939-860901 for Rs. 3,346.00 for the period September 1986 to August 1987.
- He maintains that the car did not belong to him and had not been in his possession since 1967.
- The accompanying pages document registered delivery and the postal envelope.
