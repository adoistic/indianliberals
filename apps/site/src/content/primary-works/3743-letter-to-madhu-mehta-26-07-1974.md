## Summary

This one-page letter, dated July 26, 1974, is written by P. Narayan on Swatantra Party Maharashtra letterhead to Madhu Mehta. Narayan explains that Mehta had not received a reply to his resignation letter as President of the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra.

## Key points

- The letter is dated July 26, 1974, and was issued from the Swatantra Party Maharashtra office in Bombay.
- P. Narayan writes to Madhu Mehta, addressing him as “Madhubhai.”
- The immediate subject is Mehta’s resignation as President of the Swatantra Party, Maharashtra.
- Narayan says he was surprised to learn that Mehta had not received a reply to his resignation letter.
- Narayan refers to a letter sent by G. Irish Munshi to Mehta on July 16, which Narayan had forwarded to Mehta on July 17 with Vijay.
- Narayan suggests that the earlier letter may have been misplaced and encloses a signed copy for Mehta’s information.
