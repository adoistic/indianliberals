## Summary

This single-page item is a printed compliments slip from 'Express Building', Churchgate, Bombay-20, personalised in ink with the sender's name, 'Mr. N. Dandeker, M.P.' The slip has been repurposed as a short handwritten covering note to 'Mr. Masani', which reads in full: 'I have redrafted this as discussed. I think it is o.k. now.' A signature and what appears to be a numerical notation ('1878' or a date) follow. In the top-right corner an archival hand has added 'Recd' and a date, and a circled '1247' has been written near the top, presumably a filing or catalogue reference.

The rendered page contains no substantive argument, essay, or address; it is a working document — an intra-parliamentary or intra-organisational note passing a revised draft from Dandeker to Masani for final approval. Its interest is documentary and biographical, evidencing a routine editorial exchange between two figures associated with the Swatantra Party circle. Because the note refers only obliquely to an unnamed enclosure ('this'), the substantive text under discussion is not present in the rendered page and cannot be summarised.
