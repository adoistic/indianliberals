## Key points

- The letter is dated June 11, 1964 and appears to be signed “Ranga.”
- Mr. Bhalla of the New India Assurance Company was in Bihar seeking possession of hypothecated jeeps.
- Two of the jeeps were reportedly located at Thakur Paper Mills in Samastipur.
- The mill was identified as being owned by Sachdanand Singh, described as Mahesh Babu’s son-in-law and an advocate.
- The letter reports public statements that the jeeps had been used in Mahesh Babu’s campaign during the last General Elections.
