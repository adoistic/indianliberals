## Key points

- The account covers the period from 1 June 1959 to 22 September 1959.
- The statement reports total payments and receipts of Rs. 1,11,219.81.
- The largest listed payments are to the Central Office Account (Rs. 25,762.61) and for Convention Expenses (Rs. 19,852.08).
- Other payments include hall rent, publicity, printing and stationery, office equipment, salaries, postage, conveyance, and fundraising expenses.
- The principal receipts are voluntary contributions received (Rs. 96,279.21), sale proceeds of reception committee tickets (Rs. 9,150.00), and sale proceeds of convention tickets (Rs. 4,864.00).
- The statement also records membership fees, publicity receipts, telephone call receipts, cash at bank, and cash in hand.
