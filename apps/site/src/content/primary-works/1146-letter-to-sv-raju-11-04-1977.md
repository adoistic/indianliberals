## Key points

- Short administrative letter from M. R. Masani to S. V. Raju, dated 11 April 1977.
- Follows up on an earlier letter of 4 April and calls a meeting at Masani's Army & Navy Building office on 14 April at 3 p.m.
- Stated purpose: a preliminary discussion of unspecified 'matters under dispute'.
- Masani reports he has also written to 'Girish' about the meeting.
- Asks Raju to notify two further participants referred to only as 'KH' and 'Sanpat'.
- Addressed care of the Maharashtra Swatantra Party office at 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
