## Key points

- The letter requests an emergency meeting of the Swatantra Party's General Council.
- The proposed meeting would address Mr. Masani's resignation.
- The resignation concerns membership of the National Executive and the Central Parliamentary Board.
- The writer asks the party to decide how to close ranks and resume united work.
- The letter appeals to the leadership of the party's senior leaders.
- The recipient is N. Dandekar, M.P., identified as General Secretary of the Swatantra Party.
