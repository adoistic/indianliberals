## Key points

- The letter is dated 2 March 1985 and is written from Munshi Sadan, Bombay.
- Masani addresses S. V. Raju in response to Raju’s letter of 28 February 1985.
- Masani expresses approval of Raju’s decision to prepare a booklet on Swatantra.
- He refers to his writings from 1963 onward.
- He says he has no records or papers relating to those writings.
- The letter ends with good wishes for Raju’s project.
