## Summary

This one-page Swatantra Party office statement, issued from the Bombay Office on 16 October 1959, records remittances made on behalf of the Central Office. It lists Rs. 22,500 sent between 7 July and 21 September 1959 to C. Rajagopalachari, Krishna Swamy, Sardar Hockamsingh of Rajasthan, and the party offices in Uttar Pradesh and Andhra.

## Key points

- The document is dated 16 October 1959 and is issued on Swatantra Party Bombay Office letterhead.
- It reports remittances made on behalf of the Central Office.
- The listed remittances total Rs. 22,500.
- Recipients include Shri Rajagopalachari, Mr. Krishna Swamy, Sardar Hockamsingh of Rajasthan, and the U.P. and Andhra offices.
- Additional Central Office-directed expenditure totals Rs. 8,262.69 nP.
- Reported expenditures include central-party-office travelling expenses, an Air Lines Hotel payment, a payment to Professor B.R. Shenoy, and printing bills for ticket books.
