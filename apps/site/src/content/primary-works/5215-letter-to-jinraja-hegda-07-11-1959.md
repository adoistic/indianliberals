## Summary

In this letter dated 7 November 1959, M. R. Masani thanks Jinaraja Hegde for inviting him to attend the Swatantra Party Conference at Mangalore on 6 December. He declines because of prior engagements in Calcutta on 5 and 6 December, a local engagement in Bombay on 7 December, and a flight back to Delhi on 8 December for Parliament. Masani asks Hegde to explain his difficulty to Mr. Mohamed Imam, Mr. V. B. Menon, and Mr. G. M. Manjunathayya.

## Key points

- The letter is dated 7 November 1959 and is signed by M. R. Masani.
- Masani acknowledges and thanks Hegde for an invitation to the Swatantra Party Conference at Mangalore.
- He declines the invitation because of engagements in Calcutta, Bombay, and Delhi.
- His travel schedule includes returning to Bombay on 7 December and flying to Delhi on 8 December for Parliament.
- Masani asks Hegde to explain his absence to Mohamed Imam, V. B. Menon, and G. M. Manjunathayya.
- The letter closes with Masani recalling their brief encounter in Mangalore and anticipating a meeting in Hyderabad.
