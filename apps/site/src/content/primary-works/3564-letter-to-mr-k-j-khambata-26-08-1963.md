## Key points

- The letter is dated August 26, 1963.
- S. V. Raju acknowledges receipt of Mr. K. J. Khambata's letter of August 14.
- Khambata contributed Rs. 100 to the Party.
- An official receipt is enclosed, identified in handwriting as Receipt No. 253.
- Minoo Masani did not personally reply because he was away attending Parliament.
- The letter is addressed to Khambata at Rajab Mahal, 144 Queen's Road, Fort, Bombay 1.
