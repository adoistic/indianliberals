## Key points

- The letter is dated 29 November 1974 and is issued on Madras District Swatantra Party letterhead.
- It announces an impending notice convening the National General Council in Madras on 8 December 1974.
- The proposed meeting date is linked to the 96th birthday of the late C. Rajagopalachari.
- Srikumar asks S. V. Raju to bring all National General Council members from his state who are firmly aligned with the party.
- The letter sets an attendance target of at least 50 percent of the council’s August 1974 strength.
- A postscript quotes a telegram from G. K. Sundaram urging state leaders to mobilise members and telegraph the likely attendance.
