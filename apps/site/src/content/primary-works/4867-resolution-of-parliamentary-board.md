## Key points

- The Swatantra Party Parliamentary Board expresses grief over violence against minorities in East Pakistan and India.
- It describes the violence as a horrible and barbarous outbreak.
- It deplores the continuation of these conditions in East Pakistan.
- It links the situation in East Pakistan to a continuous stream of refugees entering India.
- It refers to refugees from all minority communities as unfortunate and destitute.
- It calls on the Government to respond with sympathy and assist in refugee rehabilitation.
