## Summary

This two-page archival communication, dated 24 July 1968, is a telegram and confirmation copy sent by Professor Ranga from New Delhi to Minoo Masani of the Swatantra Party in Bombay. It concerns parliamentary committee appointments related to the Patents Bill and gold control.

## Key points

- The document is a telegram rather than a conventional letter, despite the catalogued work type.
- It is dated 24 July 1968.
- The sender is identified as Professor Ranga, with addresses associated with New Delhi and the Swatantra Party.
- The recipient is Masani at the Swatantra Party office in Bombay.
- The message concerns the Patents Bill being referred to a select committee.
- The message also concerns gold control and proposes a name for a committee dealing with it.
- The document refers to Dandekar in connection with gold control.
