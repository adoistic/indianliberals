## Key points

- The notice is dated 29 December 1969.
- It announces the Central Parliamentary Board of the Swatantra Party.
- M. R. Masani is listed as President.
- N. G. Ranga is identified as Leader of the Party in the Lok Sabha.
- Dahyabhai Patel is identified as Leader of the Party in the Rajya Sabha.
- R. M. Singh Deo and the Maharaval of Dungarpur are associated with party and opposition leadership in state legislatures.
- G. Latchanna is listed as Leader of the Party in the Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly.
- Dr. R. C. Cooper is listed as General Secretary.
