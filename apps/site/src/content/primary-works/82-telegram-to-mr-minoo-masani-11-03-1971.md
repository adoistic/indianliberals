## Key points

- The telegram is addressed to Minoo Masani in his capacity as President of the Swatantra Party.
- The sender refers to Masani as having been defeated or adversely affected by an electoral tide.
- It conveys admiration for Masani and sympathy for his constituency.
- It wishes Masani continued public service to Indians for many years.
- The message compares Masani's continuing service with that of the revered Rajaji.
- The sender's identity is recorded only as “THAKORE SAHEB.”
