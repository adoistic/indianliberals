## Key points

- The Punjab State Convention of the Swatantra Party is scheduled for 25 October in Patiala.
- K. M. Munshi is identified as the convention's inaugurator.
- N. G. Ranga is identified as the proposed presiding figure.
- Minoo Masani is invited to give a special address explaining the party's aims, objects, justification, and parliamentary position.
- The organizers expect approximately 5,000 delegates from across the state.
- The letter reports that district conventions and enrolment drives are being organized in preparation for the state convention.
- The organizers describe the response to the party's campaign as encouraging and promising.
