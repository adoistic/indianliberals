## Key points

- The South Kanara District Unit of the Swatantra Party writes from Hampankatta, Mangalore.
- The letter thanks M. R. Masani for delivering an inaugural address in Mangalore.
- The Reception Committee acknowledges Masani’s other engagements and travel effort.
- The writer invites Masani to visit one or two mofussil centres on a future trip.
- A draft for Rs. 240 is enclosed to cover Masani’s return air passage.
- The handwritten signatory appears to be H. U. Shetty, identified as an advocate and secretary in Mangalore.
