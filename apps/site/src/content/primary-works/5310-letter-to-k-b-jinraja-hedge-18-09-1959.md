## Key points

- The letter is dated 18 September 1959 and addressed to Mr. K.B. Jinaraja Hegde.
- Miss B.S. Mirza writes as secretary to M.R. Masani.
- Masani is reported to be in the United Kingdom and expected to return to Bombay around 6 October.
- Because of his absence, Masani cannot inaugurate the Swatantra Party Conference scheduled for 4 October in Mangalore.
- Mirza promises to place Hegde's letter before Masani after his return to India.
