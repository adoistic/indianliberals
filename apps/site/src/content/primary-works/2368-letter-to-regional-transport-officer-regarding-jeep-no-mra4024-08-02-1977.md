  In this letter dated February 8, 1977, M. R. Masani responds to the Regional Transport Officer, Bombay, concerning vehicle M.V. No. MRA 4024 and a claim for arrears of taxation. Masani states that the vehicle never belonged to him personally; it was one of the vehicles owned by the Swatantra Party, of which he had been General Secretary.

## Key points

- Masani disputes personal liability for taxation arrears relating to vehicle M.V. No. MRA 4024.
- He states that the vehicle belonged to the Swatantra Party rather than to him individually.
- The vehicle was sold to Tata Services Limited, with possession reportedly taken at Bhubaneswar by Tata Iron and Steel Company Limited.
- The sale is stated to have occurred on May 3, 1967.
- Masani cites a 1975 letter from the Regional Transport Officer stating that the Collector of Bombay had been instructed to drop the proceeding.
- The letter concludes that no question of payment of taxation arrears by Masani arises.
