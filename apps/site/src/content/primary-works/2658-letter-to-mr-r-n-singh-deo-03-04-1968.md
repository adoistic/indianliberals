## Key points

- M. R. Masani wrote the letter from 2 Tughlak Road, New Delhi, on 3 April 1968.
- The recipient is addressed as “Maharaja Saheb” and identified at the foot of the letter as Mr. R. N. Singh Deo, Chief Minister, Bhubaneswar.
- Masani encloses a note received from Russell W. Volckmann.
- Masani asks Singh Deo to provide facilities and assistance for Volckmann’s research.
- The requested practical arrangement is the designation of an official whom Volckmann should contact on arriving in Bhubaneswar.
- The enclosure’s visiting card identifies Volckmann as a University of California, Berkeley Ph.D. candidate.
