## Key points

- The document is a directory of newspaper, periodical, news-agency, and broadcasting contacts.
- Page 1 presents 25 numbered entries, with several crossed out or annotated by hand.
- The addresses identify editorial offices, chief reporters, special correspondents, and representatives.
- Bombay is especially prominent, but the list covers press contacts across many Indian cities.
- The directory includes publications in English and multiple Indian languages.
- Internal abbreviations and handwritten marks accompany many entries, without an explanation of their coding system.
- The rendered pages do not identify an author, issuer, date, publisher, or distribution purpose.
