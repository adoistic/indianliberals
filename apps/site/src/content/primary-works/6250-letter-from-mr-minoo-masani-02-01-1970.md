## Key points

- Masani writes to Budhi Prakash on January 2, 1970.
- He regrets being unable to meet Prakash and his colleagues from Himachal Pradesh.
- He asks Prakash to convey his thanks for their support.
- He praises the group's work in a recent membership drive.
- He notes that the group has elected a new committee.
- He urges them to make renewed efforts to strengthen the party in Himachal Pradesh.
