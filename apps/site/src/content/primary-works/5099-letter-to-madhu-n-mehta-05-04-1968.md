## Key points

- The letter was issued from the Swatantra Party office at Raj Sadan, Gonda, on 5 April 1968.
- K. M. Pandey, identified as a party General Council member and college principal, writes to Madhu N. Mehta.
- Pandey forwards the text of a letter addressed to N. Dandekar, the party's General Secretary.
- The enclosed letter refers to Mr. Masani's resignation from the National Executive and Central Parliamentary Board.
- Pandey requests an emergency meeting of the party's General Council.
- The proposed meeting is intended to discuss the situation and restore unity within the party.
