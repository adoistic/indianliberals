## Summary

This two-page English-language roster lists Swatantra Party members in the Rajya Sabha as of May 7, 1968. It presents members' names alongside permanent addresses, telephone numbers, Delhi addresses, and Delhi telephone numbers. The first page lists members including Dahyabhai Patel, Sundermani Patel, B. N. Antani, B. K. Deo, S. S. Mariswamy, Loknath Misra, M. Ruthnaswamy, Devi Singh, G. K. Sundaram, J. Chandramouli, Mahendra Kumar Mohta, and Krishna Chandra Panda; several entries are struck through or amended by hand.

The second page continues with entries under Uttar Pradesh and then repeats a shorter Rajya Sabha listing, with further handwritten corrections and crossed-out names. The document is an administrative membership and contact record rather than an argumentative or narrative text. Its imprint identifies Inland Printers, Bombay 7.

## Key points

- The document is titled "SWATANTRA MEMBERS IN THE RAJYA SABHA" and is dated May 7, 1968.
- It records parliamentary members' permanent and Delhi addresses.
- Telephone numbers are provided for both permanent and Delhi addresses where available.
- The first page contains a principal membership roster with entries associated with several Indian states.
- The second page includes an Uttar Pradesh section and a repeated Rajya Sabha listing.
- Multiple names, addresses, and telephone numbers have been crossed out or amended in handwriting, indicating later updates.
- The document carries the imprint "INLAND PRINTERS, BOMBAY 7".
