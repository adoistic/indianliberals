## Summary

In this letter dated July 29, 1964, M. R. Masani writes to Prof. N. G. Ranga about a letter from Dahyabhai and encloses a parallel letter to C. Rajagopalachari (“Rajaji”). Masani asks Ranga for his views on the proposed venue and dates, and requests a prompt reply so that arrangements can be made.

## Key points

- Masani refers to Dahyabhai's letter to Ranga dated July 27.
- He encloses a copy of his own letter to C. Rajagopalachari.
- He seeks Ranga's views on the proposed venue and dates, subject to Rajagopalachari's approval.
- Masani requests a quick reply to facilitate timely planning.
- He proposes using Rajagopalachari's presence in Delhi to influence members during party preparations.
- He suggests holding a Parliamentary Board meeting on August 6 and a party meeting for the election of office-bearers on August 8.
- Masani offers to prepare a draft notice, including the procedure for nominations, if Ranga and Rajagopalachari agree.
