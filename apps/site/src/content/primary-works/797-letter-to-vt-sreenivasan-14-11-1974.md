## Summary

This single-page letter from M. R. Masani to V. T. Sreenivasan is dated October 14, 1974, and was written after Masani's return to India. Masani acknowledges receiving Sreenivasan's letters of August 24, September 3, and September 5.

## Key points

- The letter is dated October 14, 1974.
- M. R. Masani writes to V. T. Sreenivasan in Bangalore.
- Masani had recently returned to India.
- He acknowledges receiving three earlier letters dated August 24, September 3, and September 5.
- Masani was not then contemplating a visit to Bangalore.
- He says he will inform Sreenivasan if a visit becomes possible.
