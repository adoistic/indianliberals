## Key points

- The letter is dated 17 September 1959.
- It is addressed to Mr. Nathlal Shah at Paper Mills P.O., Nihatganj, Lucknow, U.P.
- Miss B.S. Mirza writes in her capacity as secretary to M.R. Masani.
- Mirza acknowledges Shah’s letter of 30 July.
- Masani’s appreciation is conveyed for Shah’s sentiments and good wishes concerning the Swatantra Party.
- Masani was expected back in India around the first week of October.
- Masani was expected to be in Delhi in November for the next session of Parliament.
