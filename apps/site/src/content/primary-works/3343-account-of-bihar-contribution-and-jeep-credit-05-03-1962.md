## Summary

This single-page English financial statement records Bihar contributions and jeep credit as of 5 March 1962. It states that Rs. 4,50,000 was due for 15 endorsed constituencies, calculated at Rs. 30,000 per constituency.

The statement lists payments of Rs. 50,000 in cash during May/June 1961; Rs. 90,000 to the President, Darbhanga District, for three endorsed constituencies; Rs. 15,000 each to the Presidents of Saharsa and Purnea Districts in lieu of one new jeep apiece; and Rs. 1,40,000 paid to the Central Bank in monthly instalments of Rs. 20,000 through March 1962. It records total payments of Rs. 3,10,000 and a balance on hand of Rs. 1,40,000. The page contains no printed author, issuing body, publisher, or explanatory narrative; the handwritten mark at lower right is unidentified.

## Key points

- The account is dated as of 5 March 1962.
- It records Rs. 4,50,000 due for 15 endorsed constituencies at Rs. 30,000 each.
- Rs. 50,000 was paid in cash during May/June 1961.
- Darbhanga District received Rs. 90,000 for three endorsed constituencies.
- Saharsa and Purnea Districts each received Rs. 15,000 in lieu of a new jeep.
- Rs. 1,40,000 was paid to the Central Bank through monthly instalments ending in March 1962.
- The statement totals payments at Rs. 3,10,000 and shows Rs. 1,40,000 as the balance on hand.
