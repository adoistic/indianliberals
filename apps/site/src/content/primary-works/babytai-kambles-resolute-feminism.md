# Babytai Kamble’s Resolute Feminism

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sTQm6JP1xE4
Duration: 25.1s

**Speaker 1** (00:00):
Did you know Baby Thai Kamli was a pioneering Dalit woman and a fearless Indian liberal who used her voice to challenge caste oppression. Her groundbreaking autobiography, The Prisons We Broke, exposed the harsh realities faced by Dalit women, giving a platform to those often silenced.
