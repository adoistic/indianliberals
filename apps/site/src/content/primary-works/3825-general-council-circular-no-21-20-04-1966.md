## Key points

- The circular confirms a General Council meeting in Bombay for April 26–27, 1966, with May 16–17 as alternative dates.
- The meeting is to be held at the Grand Hotel, 17 Sprott Road, Ballard Estate, Bombay 1.
- The Council is asked to confirm the minutes of its previous meeting, held in Bombay on February 13–14, 1965.
- The agenda includes consideration of the General Secretary’s report on party organisation since the previous Council meeting.
- The Council is to review the current national and international situation and the Party’s position on contemporary issues.
- The agenda also allows for matters referred by the National Executive and other business with the Chair’s permission.
- The document is signed by M. R. Masani as General Secretary and includes a postscript about reception arrangements.
