## Summary

In this one-page letter dated March 20, 1975, M. R. Masani informs V. P. Bhatia that, following Masani's correspondence with the Regional Transport Officer, the Collector of Bombay has been asked to drop proceedings concerning four jeeps: MRA 4095, MRA 4096, MRA 4092, and MRA 4024. Masani presents this as the resolution of the matter previously discussed. He then addresses a fifth jeep, MRQ-2347. Masani has received a Rs. 35 tax receipt from the Municipal Corporation of Greater Bombay for the period July 1 to September 30, 1972, and notes that although the tax was presumably paid by the person to whom Bhatia sold the jeep, the vehicle still appears to remain registered in Masani's name. He asks Bhatia to identify the purchaser so that Masani can write to the Assessor and Collector of the Bombay Municipal Corporation and prevent further trouble.

## Key points

- Masani reports that proceedings concerning four specified jeeps are to be dropped. The Regional Transport Officer communicated the Collector of Bombay's decision. A Rs. 35 municipal tax receipt concerns jeep MRQ-2347 for July 1–September 30, 1972. Masani believes the fifth jeep was sold but still appears in his name.
- He asks Bhatia to identify the purchaser so the municipal records can be corrected.
