## Key points

- The report is datelined Lucknow, June 10.
- Bhanu Pratap Singh denied that M. R. Masani was planning to resign from the Swatantra Party.
- Singh had attended the party's national executive and general council meetings at Madras.
- Masani's absence from formal office is presented as intentional rather than as a departure from the party.
- The stated purpose of remaining outside office was to encourage greater awareness and dedication among party members.
- The report identifies these qualities as essential to the proper functioning of the party.
