# The Women's Question

*Background paper for the Shetkari Sanghatana women's conference, Chandvad, 9 November 1986 — by Sharad Joshi*

## Summary

The Women's Question is Sharad Joshi's background paper for the deliberations of the women's conference convened by the Shetkari Sanghatana — the farmers' movement he founded — at Chandvad in District Nashik, Maharashtra, on 9 November 1986. It is the English statement of the argument that produced the conference's landmark charter (later published in Marathi as *Chandvadchi Shidori*).

Writing from within the movement, Joshi observes that although women had taken part in the Shetkari Sanghatana's agitations from its inception in 1980 — often in unprecedented numbers and at great personal risk — the organisation had until then addressed the problems of the farming household without squarely confronting the specific burdens borne by its women: the crushing double load of domestic and field labour, and their exclusion from the economic and political gains the movement sought. The paper sets out to make the "women's question" central to the movement's agenda rather than incidental to it.
