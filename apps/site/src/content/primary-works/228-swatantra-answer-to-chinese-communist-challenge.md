summary: |-
  In the rendered pages, this Swatantra Party compilation presents the party's response to China's 1962 offensive against India through an official emergency statement, party resolutions, press interviews, parliamentary speeches, and political essays. Its central demands are a more effective defence effort, acceptance of military aid from friendly countries, closer practical cooperation with Pakistan and other states, and a reassessment of non-alignment in wartime. The contributors repeatedly argue that national unity must not mean uncritical support for the government: parliamentary opposition, press scrutiny, democratic safeguards, and the Rule of Law must continue during the emergency.
  
  In the rendered pages, the contributors also connect military unpreparedness with failures of leadership, planning, diplomacy, and political judgment. Rajagopalachari and Ranga advocate stronger Western ties, assistance from abroad, a joint front with Pakistan, and an offensive rather than merely defensive strategy. Munshi and Masani emphasize the danger of Communist expansion, the need to exclude Communists from defence committees or national platforms, and the importance of treating citizens as responsible adults while preserving democratic institutions. The rendered pages end during H. P. Mody's contribution; N. Dandekar and any remaining material were not seen.
