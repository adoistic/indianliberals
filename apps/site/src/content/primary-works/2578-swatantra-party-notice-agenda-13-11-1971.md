## Key points

- The Swatantra Party's Bombay Region issued the notice on November 13, 1971.
- An Executive Committee meeting was scheduled for November 24, 1971, at 6:15 p.m.
- The meeting was to take place at the party office in Sassoon Building, Bombay.
- The first agenda item was confirmation of the minutes of the previous meeting held on October 22, 1971.
- The second agenda item was consideration and approval of a draft election manifesto for the forthcoming Assembly elections.
- The agenda also allowed discussion of any other matter with the permission of the Chair.
- P. Narayan signed the notice as Office Secretary.
