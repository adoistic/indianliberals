## Key points

- The letter is dated September 20, 1994 and is addressed to Mr. Raju Patil at the Bombay High Court.
- S. V. Raju sends copies of the Swatantra Party's Statement of Policy and Statement of Principles.
- The letter notes that the enclosed policy documents do not explicitly reject socialism.
- Raju says that the documents nevertheless reject statism.
- The letter equates statism with socialism.
- The enclosed policy documents are not present in the rendered item.
