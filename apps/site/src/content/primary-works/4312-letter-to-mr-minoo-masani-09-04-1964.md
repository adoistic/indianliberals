## Summary

This two-page English correspondence from Bombay consists of a letter dated 9 April 1964 from Fazal A. Fazalbhoy to Minoo Masani, enclosing a letter dated 8 April to C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji). Fazalbhoy acknowledges Masani’s letter and says he is enclosing his own invitation-related correspondence concerning Rajaji’s forthcoming talk.

## Key points

- Fazal A. Fazalbhoy writes to Minoo Masani from Bombay on 9 April 1964.
- The letter encloses a separate letter dated 8 April 1964 addressed to C. Rajagopalachari.
- Fazalbhoy thanks Masani for his letter and expresses anticipation of Rajaji’s talk.
- The enclosed letter invites Rajagopalachari to address the Rotary Club of Bombay on 16 June 1964.
- The proposed talk is titled "What About Our Neighbours?"
- The invitation describes the subject as relevant to India and to a wider part of the world.
- Fazalbhoy offers to make special arrangements concerning the food Rajagopalachari would prefer.
