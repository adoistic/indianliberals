## Summary

This untitled English office notice, dated 18 November 1976, was issued by the Regional Transport Office, Bombay, to Shri M. R. Masani concerning unpaid tax on motor vehicle MRG 2347. The notice states that tax had remained unpaid from February 1976 onward and warns that the recipient could face prosecution under section 16 of the Bombay Motor Vehicles Tax Act, 1958.

The notice further explains that the arrears may be recovered as land revenue under section 12 of the same Act, offers an opportunity to compound the offence under rule 19 of the Bombay Motor Vehicles Tax Rules, 1959, and asks the recipient to provide payment particulars if the tax has already been paid. It gives seven days for compliance, failing which further action will be taken without additional notice. The second page is an enclosed Regional Transport Office bill dated 1 February 1984, covering February 1984 to January 1985, and records a grand total of Rs. 1,275 for vehicle MRG 2347; this later bill does not establish the date of the 1976 notice.

## Key points

- The Regional Transport Office, Bombay, issued the notice on 18 November 1976.
- The notice concerns unpaid tax for motor vehicle MRG 2347, allegedly outstanding from February 1976 onward.
- It invokes section 16 of the Bombay Motor Vehicles Tax Act, 1958, and warns of prosecution.
- The arrears are also described as recoverable as land revenue under section 12 of the Act.
- The recipient is offered the option of compounding the offence under rule 19 of the Bombay Motor Vehicles Tax Rules, 1959.
- The notice requests proof of payment if the tax has already been paid and allows seven days before further action.
- The enclosed bill, dated 1 February 1984, shows a grand total of Rs. 1,275 for the same vehicle.
