## Key points

- Gayatri Devi addressed a conference examining the Swatantra Party's defeat in the Dausa poll.
- She alleged misuse of government officials and vehicles in support of Congress candidates.
- She said Congress had used trucks, camel-driven carts, and other transport to bring voters to polling booths.
- She described the election as having been fought on communal, caste, and religious lines.
- She argued that the Jaipur ruling house retained influence and attributed the result to poor mobilisation of Swatantra voters.
