## Key points

- The page calls for stronger Western involvement in the Pakistan crisis and warns that delay benefits China.
- It presents the Swatantra Party’s mission as creating a consolidated democratic opposition to the ruling power.
- It argues that the party must educate voters about economic laws and accept electoral outcomes.
- It criticises taxing the rich to finance investment when the policy merely generates inflation and rising prices.
- It praises the Tamil Nadu Chief Minister for objecting to construction on Madras’s Island Grounds.
- It questions whether the Congress should select a presidential candidate based on independence, experience, and personal standing.
