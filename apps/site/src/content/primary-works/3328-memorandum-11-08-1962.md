## Summary

This single-page English memorandum is issued on a printed form of The Central Bank of India, Ltd. It is dated Bombay, 11 August 1962, a date corroborated by the bank’s official stamp. The handwritten text records a cash payment to a recipient whose name appears to be “Manalalbhai [unclear]” and identifies the recipient as “Raja Bahadur of Ramgarh”; the wording is partly difficult to decipher.

## Key points

- The document is headed “MEMORANDUM” and uses a Central Bank of India printed form.
- It was issued from Bombay on 11 August 1962.
- The handwritten entry records that cash was paid to a named recipient.
- The recipient is described as “Raja Bahadur of Ramgarh.”
- The amount appears to be Rs. 20,000, written also as “Twenty Thousand.”
- The document bears an official Central Bank stamp and is signed or designated by an accountant.
- The recipient’s name and portions of the payment wording are not fully legible.
