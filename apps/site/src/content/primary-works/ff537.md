## Summary

The March 2012 issue of Freedom First mixes electoral-democratic unease with economy, migration, defence, and history. The editorial says the Anna Hazare moment has quieted while old forms of inducement and vote-bank politics continue, and warns about pressure on institutions such as the judiciary, Election Commission, and Army.
