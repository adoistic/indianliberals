## Summary

This letter from Barun Mitra to S. V. Raju, sent on 8 January 2008, reports on a public-interest litigation petition concerning the legal impact of the word “socialism” in the Preamble to the Constitution and its relationship to the RP Act. Mitra notes that a three-judge Supreme Court bench headed by the Chief Justice of India has issued notice to the Government of India on the petition’s second part, and that the case was mentioned during the hearing to argue that socialism in the Preamble has practical and legal significance.

Mitra adds that Nariman hopes the court hearing will broaden the discussion to the constitutional dimensions of socialism. The second rendered PDF page is unrelated manuscript material headed “The Biography of M.R.Pai – Penultimate Draft 3”; it does not appear to belong to this letter and is not incorporated into the summary.

## Key points

- The letter concerns a socialism-related public-interest litigation petition before the Supreme Court.
- A three-judge bench headed by the Chief Justice of India heard the petition.
- The Supreme Court issued notice to the Government of India on the legal impact of the Preamble’s reference to socialism on the RP Act.
- The case was mentioned during the hearing to assert that socialism in the Preamble has practical and legal impact.
- Nariman hopes the hearing will expand the debate to the constitutional aspects of socialism.
