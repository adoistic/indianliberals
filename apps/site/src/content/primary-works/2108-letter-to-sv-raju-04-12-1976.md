## Key points

- The letter is dated 4 December 1976 and is issued from the Swatantra Party National Headquarters in Madras.
- V. Srikumar identifies himself as Joint Secretary and addresses S. V. Raju.
- Srikumar refers to earlier letters dated 22 and 26 November 1976.
- He asks whether Raju has met the Tax Recovery Officer and conveyed the relevant facts.
- He requests information about Piloo Mody's current thinking.
- He asks what steps the BLD and other opposition parties may take in the near future.
