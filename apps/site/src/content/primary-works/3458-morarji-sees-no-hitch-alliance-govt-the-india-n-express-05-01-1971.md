## Key points

- Morarji Desai sees no immediate obstacle to an alliance government at the Centre despite differences over foreign policy.
- Desai rejects any proposal for a confederation between India and Pakistan.
- He denies that the Sangh is advocating war against Pakistan.
- The clipping reports a proposed broad electoral front involving the SSP, BLD, and other parties.
- The proposed front is intended to create an adjustment formula capable of dislodging the New Congress.
- M. R. Masani objected to the SSP's demand for adjustment only on an issue-by-issue basis.
