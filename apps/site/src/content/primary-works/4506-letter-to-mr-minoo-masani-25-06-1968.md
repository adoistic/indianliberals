## Key points

- J. R. D. Tata writes to Minoo Masani on 25 June 1968.
- Tata thanks Masani for his letter of 10 June and its enclosures.
- He endorses the stand Masani has taken.
- He says Masani’s perseverance will help his party and improve the political atmosphere in the country.
- A handwritten note extends the endorsement to political leaders who might follow Masani’s example.
- The letter is issued from Tata Industries Private Limited’s Chairman’s Office in Bombay.
