## Key points

- The letter is dated August 9, 1973, and is written on P. G. Mavalankar's Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) letterhead.
- Mavalankar thanks M. R. Masani for a reply sent on behalf of Jayaprakash Narayan.
- He reports that Jayaprakash Narayan is recovering from a heart ailment.
- Mavalankar plans to visit Bombay later in August for three Jain Lecture Series engagements on August 25 and 26.
- He hopes to call on Jayaprakash Narayan during that visit.
- He asks whether Masani's autobiography has been published.
- The second page shows the addressed airmail envelope, with no additional substantive correspondence.
