## Summary

This single-page typed letter, dated October 18, 1967, is addressed by S. V. Raju to the Manager of Mitan Saurashtra in Rajkot. Raju responds to the recipient's September 28 letter to M. R. Masani and explains that Masani is away in Delhi attending to the work of the Public Accounts Committee.

## Key points

- The letter is dated October 18, 1967.
- It is addressed to the Manager of Mitan Saurashtra, Rajkot.
- Raju refers to the recipient's letter of September 28 to M. R. Masani.
- M. R. Masani is reported to be away in Delhi.
- Masani's absence is attributed to his work with the Public Accounts Committee.
- Raju regrets that Masani is therefore unable to respond with his comments.
