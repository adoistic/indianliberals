## Summary

This single-page English telegram, sent in July 1967 to Shri Minoo Masani, M.P., in New Delhi, concerns a political development at Bairagarh in Madhya Pradesh. Its compressed message reports that the Bairagarh resident had resolved to close the town, apparently in order to create an opportunity for the Sanyukt Vidhayak Dal, described as having a majority, to form a ministry in Madhya Pradesh.

The document is an urgent political communication focused on party manoeuvring and the formation of a state government. The sender is not identified on the visible page, and some wording is difficult to read because of the telegram's condition and apparent transmission or OCR errors.

## Key points

- The telegram is addressed to Shri Minoo Masani, M.P., in New Delhi.
- The visible postmark appears to date the communication to 22 July 1967.
- The message concerns the Bairagarh resident and events in Madhya Pradesh.
- It refers to the Sanyukt Vidhayak Dal as having a majority.
- The stated political objective is to provide an opportunity to form a ministry in Madhya Pradesh.
- The sender is not fully identified on the visible form.
