## Key points

- The letter is marked confidential and dated 13 May 1968.
- It is addressed to C. Rajagopalachari, using the salutation “Dear Rajaji,” and gives his Kilpauk, Madras address.
- The sender is identified only as the secretary to Minoo Masani.
- At Minoo Masani’s request, the sender encloses a copy of Masani’s letter to N. Dandekar dated 13 May.
- An advance copy of the letter for General Council members was also being sent; it was scheduled to go out on 15 May.
