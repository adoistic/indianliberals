summary: |-
  Speaking in Hindi/Marathi, the activist recalls how Sharad Joshi's arguments about farmers resonated with her and drew her and other village women into his agitations, including the cotton and Rajivastra (cloth) movements. She describes the 1986 Chandwad women's convention, where Joshi met the women activists and advanced the slogan of Stri-Purush Mukti (liberation of women and men), and her growing involvement in movement after movement.

  She recounts the large 1986 cotton agitation, a dharna at Telegaon, and being jailed for the first time along with thousands of men and women when protesters blocked roads; detainees filled the jails and she was moved to Nagpur jail for eight days. She mentions a major rail-roko agitation at Wardha on 12 December and the Suregaon police firing that killed protesters, the movement's turn to contesting elections to change laws and crop prices in the assembly, and the earlier inter-state movement ban (prantbandi) on cotton.
