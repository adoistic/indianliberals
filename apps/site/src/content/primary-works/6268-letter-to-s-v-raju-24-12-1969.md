## Summary

This single-page letter, dated November 24, 1969, from S. V. Raju to M. R. Masani, M.P., sets out logistical arrangements for Masani’s forthcoming visit to Bhubaneswar. Raju confirms a meeting on November 29 at 2:30 p.m., accommodation and local arrangements, a reserved air-conditioned berth on the Puri–Howrah Express, and Masani’s seat number (111).

## Key points

- The letter confirms a meeting in Bhubaneswar on November 29 at 2:30 p.m.
- A reserved air-conditioned berth on the Puri–Howrah Express has been arranged for Masani.
- Masani’s berth number is stated as 111.
- Accommodation for Masani’s stay in Bhubaneswar is to be arranged.
- Raju reports that a list of members of the Orissa State Council has been sent to Masani.
- Raju has conveyed Masani’s wish to meet Mr. Pradhan.
- The itinerary lists travel from Calcutta to Bhubaneswar and onward to Howrah.
