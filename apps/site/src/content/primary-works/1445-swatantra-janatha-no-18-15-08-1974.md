## Summary

This rendered material comes from the English-language periodical issue SWATANTRA JANATHA, Vol. X No. 18, an Independence Day Special Number dated August 15, 1974, published from Kottayam. The visible editorial item, “A Time to Rally,” responds to the reported decision of the National Council to dissolve the Swatantra Party and merge it with the Bharatiya Lok Dal. It argues that the party's remaining supporters represent a Gandhian trusteeship tradition and urges them to consolidate, maintain contact with their constituencies, and rally around “truth, honesty, patriotism and all-round goodwill.”
