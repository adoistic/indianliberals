## Summary

This one-page English office record documents a meeting of the Swatantra Party Executive Committee held on Monday, 30 August 1965, in the Party Committee Room, Central Hall, Parliament House, New Delhi. Prof. N. G. Ranga chaired the meeting, which was attended by seven listed members, including M. R. Masani, Dahayabhai V. Patel, Lok Nath Misra, M. M. Ruthnaswamy, Sardar Kapur Singh, and P. N. Solanki.

The committee selected Prof. N. G. Ranga and Sardar Kapur Singh from the Lok Sabha, and Shri S. S. Mariswamy from the Rajya Sabha, to represent the party on a Select Committee examining the Hindu Religious Trusts & Endowment Bill. The meeting then adjourned with a vote of thanks to the chair. No broader policy argument or discussion is recorded on the page.

## Key points

- The Swatantra Party Executive Committee met on 30 August 1965 at Parliament House in New Delhi.
- Prof. N. G. Ranga chaired the meeting.
- Seven party members are listed as present.
- Prof. N. G. Ranga and Sardar Kapur Singh were chosen as Lok Sabha representatives.
- Shri S. S. Mariswamy was chosen as the Rajya Sabha representative.
- The representatives were selected for a Select Committee examining the Hindu Religious Trusts & Endowment Bill.
- The record was signed by P. N. Solanki, identified as secretary.
