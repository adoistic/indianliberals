## Key points

- A confirmation copy of an express telegram sent by Minoo Masani is dated 1967.
- The telegram is addressed to Shrimati Indira Gandhi at the Prime Minister's residence in New Delhi.
- Masani requests an appointment for an informal discussion.
- He proposes Tuesday the fourteenth and asks the recipient to reply by telegram.
- Masani states that he will be at his estate from Monday evening.
- The sender's address is given as the Swatantra Party, 143 Mahatma Gandhi Road, Bombay 1.
- The heading and the lower-right copy date appear inconsistent: “MARCH 12” versus “March 1, 1967.”
