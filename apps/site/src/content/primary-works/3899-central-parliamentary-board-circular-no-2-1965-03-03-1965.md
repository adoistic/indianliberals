## Summary

This one-page English-language circular, issued by the Swatantra Party Central Office on March 3, 1965, announces a meeting of the Central Parliamentary Board at M. R. Masani’s residence in New Delhi on March 14. Signed by Masani as General Secretary, it asks recipients to make every effort to attend.

The revised agenda covers confirmation of the previous board’s minutes, action arising from a General Council resolution endorsing the General Secretary’s organisational proposals, recommendations concerning prospective parliamentary candidates received from state units, and any other business permitted by the Chair. The document is an administrative party communication rather than a statement of political doctrine.

## Key points

- The circular is dated March 3, 1965, and is issued on Swatantra Party Central Office letterhead.
- The Central Parliamentary Board is scheduled to meet on March 14 at 10 a.m. at 57, Lodi Estate, New Delhi.
- The agenda includes confirmation of the minutes of the previous meeting held on September 30.
- The Board is to consider action arising from the General Council resolution endorsing the General Secretary’s organisational proposals.
- The Board will consider recommendations for prospective parliamentary candidates received from state units.
- The agenda also allows for other business with the Chair’s permission.
- M. R. Masani signs the circular as General Secretary.
