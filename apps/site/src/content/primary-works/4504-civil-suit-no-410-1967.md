## Summary

This is the judgment in Civil Suit No. 410 of 1967 before the Court of the 3rd Joint Civil Judge, Junagadh, concerning a dispute over elections within the Junagadh District Unit of the Swatantra Party. The plaintiff, a party member elected as a district representative from the Manavadar Primary Unit, challenged the validity of elections held on 24 September 1967 and sought a declaration that they were legal, together with an injunction preventing the defendants from interfering with the elected members. The dispute arose after the Central Office cancelled the elections and ordered fresh elections, alleging violations of Rule 27 of the party’s election procedure.

The court held that the Returning Officer had breached Rule 27 by standing as a candidate and by appointing another person to preside over the meeting. Because the elections were conducted in a single meeting affected by that procedural violation, the court treated the proceedings as invalid. It nevertheless rejected the plaintiff’s challenge to the Central Office’s decision to cancel the elections, finding that the action was not ultra vires, arbitrary, mala fide, or contrary to natural justice. The court distinguished cases involving statutory bodies from the internal governance of voluntary political associations, emphasized that political parties enjoy greater freedom of action, and dismissed the suit without awarding costs. The judgment was pronounced on 29 April 1968.

## Key points

- The suit challenged the cancellation of Junagadh District Swatantra Party elections and the direction to hold fresh elections.
- The elections of 24 September 1967 were conducted under the party’s internal election rules, especially Rule 27.
- The Returning Officer violated Rule 27 by becoming a candidate and appointing another person to supervise part of the election.
- The court held that the procedural breach affected all elections conducted at the meeting.
- The court upheld the Central Office’s authority to cancel the elections and order fresh elections.
- The court declined to apply statutory-body standards mechanically to a voluntary political association.
- The plaintiff’s suit was dismissed without an order for costs.
