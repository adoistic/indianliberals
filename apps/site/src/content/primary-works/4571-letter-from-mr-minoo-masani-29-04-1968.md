## Summary

This two-page confirmation copy of Minoo Masani’s letter, dated 29 April 1968, is addressed to S. V. Raju in Bombay. Masani asks Raju to send him a Bombay press clipping concerning Vajpayee’s counter-statement to Pravinsing Solanki, and requests Raju’s suggestions on draft letters to Dandekar and to members of the General Council before their meeting in Bombay on 3 May.

## Key points

- Masani requests a Bombay press clipping about Vajpayee’s counter-statement to Pravinsing Solanki.
- He sends or refers to a draft letter to Dandekar and a confidential letter to members of the General Council.
- He asks S. V. Raju to suggest changes before their 3 May meeting in Bombay.
- Masani seeks clarification from the minutes regarding disciplinary action against the N.L.As in Uttar Pradesh over their refusal to elect Kripalani in 1966.
- He recalls that B. P. Singh proposed dissolving the State Executive and Parliamentary Board and reconstituting the State unit on an ad hoc basis.
- He asks Raju to make General Secretary reports available for possible quotation.
