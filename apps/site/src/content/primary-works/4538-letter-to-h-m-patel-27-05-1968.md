## Summary

This Gujarati-language letter, dated 27 May 1968, was written by advocate Narayanbhai R. Patel to H. M. Patel of the Gujarat Swatantra Party. It serves as a legal notice concerning unpaid salary and service-related dues owed to the sender’s client, K. B. Patel, who had reportedly worked as a secretary in the Gujarat Swatantra Party office for approximately two and a half years, ending in November 1967.

The letter itemizes claims for salary, leave-related payments, and other employment benefits, and argues that the client’s services were terminated without adequate notice or settlement. It requests payment of the outstanding amount within a specified period and warns that, failing a satisfactory response, the client will pursue remedies before the appropriate court. Copies were marked to several Gujarat Swatantra Party figures, including Morarji Desai, Dandekar, M. R. Masani, and Chimanbhai Desai.

## Key points

- The document is a legal notice sent by advocate Narayanbhai R. Patel on 27 May 1968.
- It is addressed to H. M. Patel and concerns the Gujarat Swatantra Party.
- The underlying claimant, K. B. Patel, is described as having served as a party-office secretary for about two and a half years.
- The notice seeks payment of unpaid salary and related employment dues, including leave or service-related amounts.
- The sender states that the client’s service ended without proper settlement and demands a response and payment within a prescribed period.
- The letter threatens court proceedings if the claim is not resolved.
- Copies were sent to other senior figures associated with the Gujarat Swatantra Party.
